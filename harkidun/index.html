Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Har Ki Dun is an easy valley trek: no summit, just a long walk past old wooden villages into a high valley at the foot of the big peaks.

The route

6 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Dhatmeer · 2,400 mkm 0.1

Where the road ends

2,400mAltitude · The road head

Day 1 is the long drive from Dehradun to Sankri, about 197 km. This morning a short drive up the valley ends at Dhatmeer, and the walking begins.

02Day 2 · Pauni Garaat · 2,500 mkm 6.0

Camp by the river

6kmToday · 4–5 h, gentle

The trail runs up the valley beside the river to Pauni Garaat, a camp near the water.

03Day 3 · Seematra · 3,250 mkm 14.0

Above the villages

+750mGain today · 8 km · 5–6 h

A steady climb out of the villages and up the valley to Seematra, a high camp below the big peaks.

04Day 4 · Har Ki Dun · 3,566 mkm 21.0

The valley of gods

14kmToday · There and back, 7–8 h

A day walk from Seematra into Har Ki Dun, a wide high valley at about 3,566 m.

05Day 5 · Osla · about 2,750 mkm 33.5

A village of carved wood

7kmToday · 3–4 h, downhill

The way back crosses to Osla, a village of double-storied wooden houses with slanted roofs.

06Day 6 · Dhatmeer · 2,400 mkm 41.0

Out of the valley

6kmToday · 4–5 h

The last walk follows the river back down to the road at Dhatmeer, then a short drive to Sankri.

Elevation: 41.0 km, 1,110 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

No ATM in Sankri. The last ATMs are in Purola, on the way.

Bikat Adventures
📶Which SIM

Only BSNL and Jio work in Sankri. Bikat's homestays have paid Wi-Fi.

Bikat Adventures
🔌Charging

Sankri homestays have plugs, but power cuts are long. No electricity after Sankri.

Bikat Adventures
🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days.

Bikat Adventures
🚻Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents. Dry pit toilet tents at camp, with no water. Carry toilet paper.

Bikat Adventures
🪪Permits

Operators get the permits and include the fee. Carry your ID and medical certificate.

Questions

How long is the Har Ki Dun trek?

About 41.0 km of walking over 7 days on the trail.

How high does the Har Ki Dun trek go?

The highest point is 3,566 m, about 1,110 m above the start.

How difficult is the Har Ki Dun trek?

Its sources grade it Easy.

When is the best time for the Har Ki Dun trek?

Sep–Dec. It is also possible in Mar–Aug.

How much does the Har Ki Dun trek cost?

₹8,799 to ₹12,500 per person: base prices from 7 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Do I need a permit or pay an entry fee for the Har Ki Dun trek?

Operators get the permits and include the fee. Carry your ID and medical certificate.

Where do I get cash before the Har Ki Dun trek?

No ATM in Sankri. The last ATMs are in Purola, on the way.

What should I pack for the Har Ki Dun trek?

Warm jacket, Full-sleeve sweater or fleece, Thermals, upper and lower, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Quick-dry track pants ×2, Rain jacket and pants, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Har Ki Dun trek in winter?

Yes: Dec is among its best months.

Is the Har Ki Dun trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy and it takes 7 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,566 m.

Which treks are near the Har Ki Dun trek?

Bali Pass, Phulara Ridge, Kedarkantha, Rupin Pass, Dodital.
