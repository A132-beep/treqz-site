Stories and nature

The stairway to heaven

🏔️The peak

Swargarohini

The name comes from the Mahabharata. The Pandavas gave up their kingdom and walked north toward heaven; Swargarohini's peaks were their stairway, and only Yudhishthira reached the top.

🛕The village

Someshwar of Osla

Osla's double-storied wooden houses gather around the Someshwar temple, dedicated to Lord Shiva and covered in detailed carving.

✨The valley

Valley of Gods

Trekkers call Har Ki Dun the Valley of Gods. It is snow-covered from October to March, and the forest department closes the trek in deep winter.

Best time for the Har Ki Dun trek

Pick your season

Bikat calls September to December the best time. The trek is closed from after December until mid-March.

🍂Sep–Dec

Clear skies and the first snow on the peaks. Late in the season the valley itself turns white.

🌸Spring and summer

Pleasant temperatures and flowers in bloom after the trek reopens in mid-March.

⛔Closed in deep winter

The forest department closes the trek from after December until mid-March.

🌡️Winter cold

Days −5 to 5 °C, nights −10 to −15 °C.

How to reach the Har Ki Dun trek

Getting to Sankri

Har Ki Dun shares its base with Kedarkantha: everyone sleeps in Sankri before the walk.

  1. Dehradun🚌 About 197 km, 9–10 h by road
  2. Sankri🚙 18 km, about 1.5 h, to the road head
  3. Dhatmeer
💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹12,500 + 5% GST, Sankri to Sankri. Dehradun transport is ₹2,200 extra.

📶Last signal

Sankri is the last place with a stable mobile signal.

⛰️Hardest part

Day 4: 14 km to Har Ki Dun and back, 7–8 hours on the trail.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

No ATM in Sankri. The last ATMs are in Purola, on the way. Bikat Adventures

📶Which SIM

Only BSNL and Jio work in Sankri. Bikat's homestays have paid Wi-Fi. Bikat Adventures

🔌Charging

Sankri homestays have plugs, but power cuts are long. No electricity after Sankri. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

🚻Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents. Dry pit toilet tents at camp, with no water. Carry toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🪪Permits

Operators get the permits and include the fee. Carry your ID and medical certificate. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is in Mori, 21 km from Sankri. Purola also has one. Himalaya Shelter

🐴If you fall ill

The trail passes Taluka, Gangad and Osla. You can hire a horse or mule there to get back to Sankri. Himalaya Shelter

🎒Offloading

A porter or mule for your bag costs ₹1,750 with Bikat, ₹1,800 with Trekkaro. Bikat Adventures

🚌Bus to Sankri

Buses to Sankri leave the Mussoorie bus stand near Dehradun railway station at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 a.m. Himalayan Dream Treks
