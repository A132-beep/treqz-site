Stories and nature

Two forts on a machi

🏯The forts

Shrivardhan and Manaranjan

Twin fortresses around a machi, a plateau. Rajmachi played a part in the First Anglo-Maratha War and is a protected monument.

☸️The caves

Kondana

Sixteen Buddhist caves cut in the 1st century BC, among them a chaitya hall.

🛤️The pass

Above the Borghat

The forts overlook the old Borghat trade route. In the monsoon the Kataldhar waterfall can be seen from Shrivardhan.

Best time for the Rajmachi trek

Rain, winter or fireflies

Bikat runs it from June to September and November to March.

🌧️Jun–Sep

Heavy rain, 20–30 °C, forest and waterfalls. The jeep road closes.

🌤️Nov–Mar

Winter nights can drop to about 10 °C.

✨Mid-May–mid-June

Firefly season, before the rains.

How to reach the Rajmachi trek

Start in Lonavala

The walk starts on the Lonavala side and ends near Karjat; both are on the Mumbai–Pune railway.

  1. Lonavala station🚙 Drive to the start
  2. Rajmachi🥾 Down past Kondana
  3. Kondhavane → Karjat
💧Water

Don't swim in the lake or the fort tanks: villagers drink from them.

📵No signal

Expect no mobile network beyond the base village.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚆Trains to Lonavala

From Mumbai, the Konark Express (11019) leaves CSMT at 2:00 pm and reaches Lonavala at 4:27 pm. From Pune, a 3:00 pm local reaches Lonavala at 4:20 pm. Treks and Trails

🚙Jeeps fill up

An 8 km jeep ride takes you towards the base village. In peak season the jeeps get overbooked, so expect to wait. Treks and Trails

🎫Tiny village fee

No permit is needed. The local committee collects ₹5 a person at the base village. Trekhievers

🏠Village homestays

Udhewadi homestays keep men and women in separate rooms. There are no couple rooms. Treks and Trails

🌧️If it rains

If rain stops camping, groups sleep in a villager's common hall for 40–50 people. Trekhievers

🚻Toilets in the village

There are common washrooms in the base village. There are none on the trail. Trekhievers

🍛Village food

Meals are home-cooked local food in the village. Treks and Trails

✨Firefly crowds

Firefly season brings big weekend crowds. Pick a Friday–Saturday batch if you can. Bhatakna

🎒No offloading

Bikat has no option to offload your bag. Bikat Adventures
