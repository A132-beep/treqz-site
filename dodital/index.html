Stories and nature

Ganesha's lake

🐘The legend

Where Ganesha was born

Dodital is believed to be the birthplace of Ganesha, and a Ganesha temple stands on its shore. Wikipedia says the name comes from a sage called Dodi; Himalaya Shelter says dodi is the local word for trout.

🐟The trout

A British gift

Trout were put into Dodital in 1862 by the British eccentric known as Pahari Wilson. Bikat lists golden, snow and brown trout in the lake today.

🌊The flood

The night of 3 August 2012

A cloudburst high in the Assi Ganga valley sent a flash flood down to Uttarkashi. Nineteen labourers at the Assi Ganga hydel project were reported missing, and the river's trout were wiped out.

🐒The mountain

The monkey's tail

Bandarpunch, 6,316 m, fills the view from Darwa. Hanuman is said to have put out his burning tail on its summit. Tenzing Norgay was in the team that first climbed it, in 1950.

Best time for the Dodital trek

Spring and early winter

Bikat runs the trek in April–June and October–December. Himalaya Shelter says snow usually reaches the lake from mid-December to March.

🌸April–June

About 15–20 °C by day and 5–10 °C at night.

🌧️July–August

Monsoon. Neither Bikat nor Himalaya Shelter recommends these months.

🍂Sep–Nov

Himalaya Shelter's autumn window: clear and cool.

❄️Dec–March

Snow on the trail and a frozen lake. Nights fall to −10 °C, and to −15 °C at the lake in mid-December.

How to reach the Dodital trek

Getting to Agoda

Uttarkashi is the last town. Taxis go on to Agoda.

  1. Dehradun🚌 145 km, 5–6 h by bus or shared taxi
  2. Uttarkashi🚕 About 20 km, 45–60 min
  3. Agoda

Nearest airport: Jolly Grant, Dehradun. Trains run to Dehradun and Rishikesh. Rishikesh to Uttarkashi is about 162 km.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹11,600 + 5% GST, Agoda to Agoda. Transport from Dehradun is ₹2,500 return extra.

🎫Permits

Dodital is in the Uttarkashi Forest Division. The district's Explore Uttarkashi portal lists the trek; you apply through a registered agency with ID, a medical certificate and an affidavit.

💵Cash

The last ATMs are in Uttarkashi. Agoda is very small.

📶Network

Phones work up to Uttarkashi. Signal returns about an hour into the drive out of Agoda.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Uttarkashi has the last reliable ATM. There is none on the trail, and cards are not accepted there. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Agoda is the last place with a steady signal, and only Jio works well there. Expect no reliable signal after Agoda. Trekkaro

🔋No power on the trail

There is no electricity beyond the base village. Carry a power bank. Himalayan Hikers

🩺Medical certificate

Uttarkashi's trek portal asks for ID, a medical fitness certificate and an affidavit before a permit is issued. Allow 2–3 working days. Explore Uttarkashi

👥Group rules

Uttarkashi's trek portal lists Sangam Chatti–Dodital for ages 18–60, in groups of up to 10. Explore Uttarkashi

🧭Trained guides

Since December 2025, every agency on Uttarkashi's trek portal must register at least two trained guides. The Daily Jagran

🎒Offloading

Trekkaro carries your bag for ₹1,000 + 5% GST for the whole trek, up to 9 kg. Trekkaro

🧳Porters at Agoda

Himalayan Hikers says a porter costs ₹400 a day, paid at Agoda, for bags of 8 kg or more. Himalayan Hikers

🚕Shared cab

A cab from Dehradun railway station to Agoda costs about ₹6,500 for a 5–6 seater, split between trekkers. The return costs the same. Trekkaro

🥾Free gear

Trekkaro lends shoes, a pole, a padded jacket, gaiters and microspikes free, against a ₹3,000 refundable deposit and a ₹200 washing charge. Trekkaro
