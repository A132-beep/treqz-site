Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

Uttarkashi has the last reliable ATM. There is none on the trail, and cards are not accepted there.

Trekkaro
📶Phone signal

Agoda is the last place with a steady signal, and only Jio works well there. Expect no reliable signal after Agoda.

Trekkaro
🔋No power on the trail

There is no electricity beyond the base village. Carry a power bank.

Himalayan Hikers
🩺Medical certificate

Uttarkashi's trek portal asks for ID, a medical fitness certificate and an affidavit before a permit is issued. Allow 2–3 working days.

Explore Uttarkashi
👥Group rules

Uttarkashi's trek portal lists Sangam Chatti–Dodital for ages 18–60, in groups of up to 10.

Explore Uttarkashi
🧭Trained guides

Since December 2025, every agency on Uttarkashi's trek portal must register at least two trained guides.

Questions

How long is the Dodital trek?

About 44.0 km of walking over 6 days on the trail.

How high does the Dodital trek go?

The highest point is 4,150 m, about 1,880 m above the start.

How difficult is the Dodital trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Dodital trek?

Apr–Jun, Oct–Dec. It is also possible in Mar, Sep.

How much does the Dodital trek cost?

₹10,000 to ₹64,900 per person: base prices from 7 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Dodital trek?

Agoda is the last place with a steady signal, and only Jio works well there. Expect no reliable signal after Agoda.

Where do I get cash before the Dodital trek?

Uttarkashi has the last reliable ATM. There is none on the trail, and cards are not accepted there.

What should I pack for the Dodital trek?

Padded jacket (rated to −10 °C), Thermal innerwear, Fleece or woollen layers, Quick-dry trek pants ×2, Full-sleeve collared T-shirts ×3, Rain jacket or poncho, Sun cap, Woollen cap, balaclava in winter. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Dodital trek in winter?

Yes: Dec is among its best months.

Is the Dodital trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate and it takes 6 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,150 m.

Which treks are near the Dodital trek?

Dayara Bugyal, Bali Pass, Har Ki Dun, Phulara Ridge, Kedarkantha.
