Stories and nature

The home of the serpent god

🐍The name

The serpent's peak

Nag Tibba means "the serpent's peak". The mountain is said to be the home of the serpent god, Nag Devta.

🛕The camp

Nag Mandir

The small Nag Mandir temple stands at the centre of the campsite clearing. In deep snow, camp moves 2 km down to Khatian.

🏔️The view

Snow peaks to the north

From the top: Swargarohini, Bandarpoonch and Black Peak to the north, and Nanda Devi far to the east.

Best time for the Nag Tibba trek

A winter weekend

December to March brings snow on the summit ridge.

❄️Dec–Mar

Snow on the ridge; nights at camp around −7 °C.

📅Batches

Bikat Adventures runs 22–24 Nov 2026, 25–27 Dec 2026 and 24–26 Jan 2027.

💰Cost

₹5,400 + 5% GST with Bikat. Dehradun transport is extra.

How to reach the Nag Tibba trek

Getting to Pantwari

Pantwari is about 90 km from Dehradun, past Mussoorie and Kempty Falls.

  1. Dehradun🚙 About 90 km, 3–4 h, via Mussoorie and Kempty Falls
  2. Pantwari

Bikat's groups leave the Dehradun bus stand at 7 a.m. and start walking around 11.

⛰️Hardest part

The first afternoon: 925 m of climbing straight after the drive.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵ATMs in Pantwari

Pantwari has a few ATMs: State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank and Union Bank of India. Bikat Adventures

📶Good phone signal

Signal covers almost the whole trail. Airtel and Jio work well. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging at camp

There is no electricity at the camp. Charge your phone and power bank before you leave. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

🚻Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents. A dry pit toilet tent at camp. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Colder at the top

Days in Pantwari are 5 to 10 °C. At the summit it can drop to −10 to −15 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is the Government Hospital in Nainbagh. Your operator drives you there from Pantwari. Himalayan Dream Treks

🎒Offloading

A porter or mule for your bag costs ₹918 with Bikat, ₹800 with Trekkaro. Himalaya Shelter charges ₹400 a day for a bag up to 12 kg. Bikat Adventures

🚙Local jeeps

Bolero jeeps run regularly between Dehradun and Pantwari. They are crowded but the cheapest way up. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚕Shared cab cost

Bikat's cab leaves the Dehradun bus stand at 7 a.m. A Sumo for 5–6 people costs ₹4,000 each way. A Tempo for 11–12 costs ₹6,000. Bikat Adventures
