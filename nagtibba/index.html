Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Nag Tibba is a short winter trek: one camp by a temple, one summit morning, home on day three.

The route

4 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Pantwari · 1,400 mkm 0.1

A weekend summit

~90kmFrom Dehradun · 3–4 h, via Mussoorie

The road from Dehradun climbs past Mussoorie and Kempty Falls to Pantwari, a village at about 1,400 m. You arrive before noon and start walking straight away.

01Day 1 · Nag Mandir · 2,325 mkm 5.5

The serpent temple

+925mGain today · 5.5 km · 4–5 h

A steady climb through forest brings you to a clearing with the small Nag Mandir temple at its centre. Camp is pitched around it.

02Day 2 · Nag Tibba · 3,022 mkm 7.5

The serpent's peak

+700mFrom camp · 2 km · 2–3 h

An early start, and a 2 km climb up the final ridge to the top of Nag Tibba at 3,022 m.

03Day 3 · Pantwari · 1,400 mkm 14.9

Down by lunch

5.5kmToday · 3–4 h, downhill

Back down through the forest to Pantwari by early afternoon.

Elevation: 15.0 km, 1,590 m up and back down

Questions

How long is the Nag Tibba trek?

About 15.0 km of walking over 3 days on the trail.

How high does the Nag Tibba trek go?

The highest point is 3,022 m, about 1,590 m above the start.

How difficult is the Nag Tibba trek?

Its sources grade it Not graded.

When is the best time for the Nag Tibba trek?

Dec–Mar. It is also possible in Nov.

How much does the Nag Tibba trek cost?

₹2,350 to ₹5,400 per person: base prices from 9 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Nag Tibba trek?

Signal covers almost the whole trail. Airtel and Jio work well.

Where do I get cash before the Nag Tibba trek?

Pantwari has a few ATMs: State Bank of India, Punjab National Bank and Union Bank of India.

What should I pack for the Nag Tibba trek?

Full-sleeve tops ×2, Fleece T-shirt, Fleece jacket or sweater, Thermal inners, Trek pants ×2, Poncho, Woollen gloves, Woollen cap. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Nag Tibba trek in winter?

Yes: Dec–Feb is among its best months.

Which treks are near the Nag Tibba trek?

Dodital, Dayara Bugyal, Kedarkantha, Phulara Ridge, Bali Pass.
