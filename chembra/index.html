Stories and nature

The heart of Wayanad

💗The legend

Hridaya Thadakam

The heart-shaped lake does not dry out in any summer. Local legend says the goddess Sita bathed in it.

🔥The fire

Closed in 2018

A forest fire in February 2018 burned the grassland and closed the peak. It reopened with far fewer visitors allowed each day.

🦋The slopes

Tea and butterflies

Tea and coffee estates cover the lower slopes. The White-bar bushbrown butterfly has been recorded here.

Best time for the Chembra trek

Go early

None of the sources we used gives a season. The forest department admits visitors from 7 am to 3 pm.

🕖Hours

Entry 7 am to 3 pm; start early.

💰Fees

Trekking ₹4,000 for up to 5 people, ₹800 for each extra person; students ₹1,000 for 5.

📷Cameras

₹53 for a camera, ₹301 for a video camera.

How to reach the Chembra trek

Getting to Meppadi

Meppadi is the nearest town; the peak is about 13 km away by road. Permission comes from the forest department, booked offline.

  1. Meppadi🚙 About 13 km by road
  2. Forest office🚙 Jeep
  3. Trail start
🐘Beyond the lake

The second half of the trek, to the summit, is restricted because of wildlife activity.

⛺No camping

There is no camping at the top.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚌Bus to Kalpetta

Take a Bangalore–Calicut bus and get off at Kalpetta: 7–8 hours, about ₹800–900. Then a bus or shared jeep to Meppadi, 11.5 km. From Meppadi mosque, an auto to the start is another 8–9 km. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

🏨Stay in Meppadi

There are hotels near the forest office if you want to stay the night before. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

📞Book at the office

Booking is offline only. The forest office numbers are +91 4936 282001 and 203428. Kerala Tourism

⏰Come early

DTPC gives trekking hours as 7 am to 12, for up to 88 people a day. Bikat says no one is let past the forest office after 2 pm. DTPC Wayanad

📶No signal on the trail

Signal is patchy up to the start. There is none on the trek. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

💧Carry food and water

There are no stalls on the trail. Buy food and water in Meppadi. Don't drink from the pond or the heart lake. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

🚯Plastic is tagged

Declare plastic bottles and other single-use plastic at the checkpoint. They are tagged and checked when you come back. I'm Voyager

🌡️Cold mornings

Wayanad's valleys are about 20–25 °C. Near the heart lake, mornings from November to February can drop to 8–12 °C. Bring a light jacket. StayVista

🩸Leeches

Leeches are common in the forest, most of all in the rains. Use insect repellent and wear long sleeves, long pants and high socks. The Hosteller
