Stories and nature

The five treasures

🏔️The mountain

Five treasures

Kanchenjunga is said to mean "five treasures of the great snow", for its five peaks.

🏯The start

Yuksom

The first capital of Sikkim, near the monasteries of Tashiding and Katok. Tshoka has its own monastery, beside a still pond.

🐼The park

Kanchenjunga National Park

The whole trek is inside the park, home to the red panda and the snow leopard.

Best time for the Goechala trek

Spring or autumn

Bikat runs it in March–April and October–December.

🌸Mar–Apr

Rhododendrons and oak in bloom.

🍂Oct–Dec

Autumn colours. About 10–15 °C lower down, 0–5 °C at Dzongri and above.

💰Cost

₹21,500 + 5% GST with Bikat, permits included. NJP–Yuksom transport is extra.

How to reach the Goechala trek

Getting to Yuksom

Groups meet in Siliguri (NJP) and drive about 150 km to Yuksom. Foreign visitors need a Restricted Area Permit to enter Sikkim and a Protected Area Permit for the trek (see below), and cannot join alone.

  1. Siliguri / NJP🚙 About 150 km, 7–8 h
  2. Yuksom
⛰️Hardest part

Day 7: a 1 am start and 12–13 hours on foot. Bikat asks for high-altitude experience first.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The nearest ATMs are in Yuksom, Pelling and Gangtok. There are none on the trail. Bikat Adventures

📶No phone signal

Usually no signal beyond Yuksom. You may catch some near the first hut at Tshoka. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

There is no electricity on the trail. Bring a big power bank. Bikat Adventures

🍵Food on the trail

There are no shops on the route. A hut caretaker may sell tea or snacks, at two or three times Yuksom prices. Limboo Homestay, Yuksom

🪪ID for the permit

Indians need a passport, voter ID or driving licence. Aadhaar is not accepted. Bring two photos and a fitness certificate from a government doctor. Trek in Sikkim

🌡️Cold nights

Autumn nights fall to −5 to −10 °C. Dzongri, Thansing and Lamuney are the coldest camps. Limboo Homestay, Yuksom

🏥Nearest hospital

Primary Health Centre in Yuksom. District Hospital, Gyalshing, is about 5 hours by road: +91 3595 250648. Trekup India

🚑Evacuation

Beyond Dzongri, evacuation options are very limited. Getting down can take hours or days on foot. Packages don't include evacuation insurance. Limboo Homestay, Yuksom

🐴Offloading

A horse or yak carries your backpack for ₹300 a day, or ₹500 if you ask mid-trek. Black Yak Expeditions

🚙Jeep to Yuksom

A Sumo or Bolero from Siliguri costs ₹8,000 each way, shared by 5–6 trekkers. Bikat Adventures
