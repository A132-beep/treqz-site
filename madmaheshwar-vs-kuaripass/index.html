Kuari Pass goes 317 m higher (3,814 m against 3,497 m). Kuari Pass takes 1 more day on the trail. Madmaheshwar is the easier of the two (Easy–moderate against Moderate). Their best seasons don't overlap: Madmaheshwar in May–Jun, Sep–Oct, Kuari Pass in Dec–Feb. The trailheads are about 40 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Madmaheshwar if you want an easier trek (Easy–moderate), fewer days away (4 on the trail) and to go in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

Pick Kuari Pass if you want a lower price (middle ₹9,400), more height (3,814 m) and to go in Dec–Feb.

Questions

Which is easier, Madmaheshwar or Kuari Pass?

Madmaheshwar, graded Easy–moderate against Moderate.

Which costs less, Madmaheshwar or Kuari Pass?

The middle operator price is ₹10,999 for Madmaheshwar and ₹9,400 for Kuari Pass, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Madmaheshwar or Kuari Pass?

Madmaheshwar reaches 3,497 m and Kuari Pass 3,814 m.

Can I do Madmaheshwar and Kuari Pass in the same season?

Not at their best: Madmaheshwar is best in May–Jun, Sep–Oct and Kuari Pass in Dec–Feb.

How far apart are Madmaheshwar and Kuari Pass?

About 40 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
