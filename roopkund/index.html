Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Roopkund is a hard, high trek: six days on foot from village forest to a frozen lake above 5,000 m.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Wan · 2,450 mkm 0.1

The last village

~290kmFrom Dehradun · A full day by road

The drive from Dehradun follows the rivers up through Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Lohajung to Wan, the last road village below the meadows.

02Day 2 · Gheroli Patal · 3,200 mkm 6.0

Into the forest

+750mGain today · 6 km · 5–6 h

The trail leaves Wan and climbs through forest to Gheroli Patal, a camp in a clearing.

03Day 3 · Bedni Bugyal · 3,500 mkm 10.0

The meadow of the gods

+300mGain today · 4 km · 3–4 h

Out of the forest and onto Bedni Bugyal, a vast high meadow with Trishul above it.

04Day 4 · Patar Nachauni · 3,870 mkm 15.0

Where the dancers fell

+370mGain today · 5 km · 4–5 h

Past the meadows of Ghora Lotani to Patar Nachauni, a camp above the tree line.

05Day 5 · Bhagwabasa · 4,330 mkm 19.0

Past Kalu Vinayak

+460mGain today · 4 km · 4–5 h

The trail climbs past Kalu Vinayak, a small temple to Ganesha on an exposed ridge at about 4,300 m with views of Chaukhamba, Trishul and Nanda Ghunti.

06Day 6 · Roopkund · 5,029 mkm 24.9

The skeleton lake

16kmToday · 10–12 h, from 2–3 a.m.

Before dawn the trail climbs to Roopkund, a small glacial lake at about 5,029 m, iced over for most of the year.

07Day 7 · Wankm 48.9

Down through the meadows

14kmToday · 7–8 h

The long way down retraces the meadows of Bedni and the forest of Gheroli Patal to Wan.

Elevation: 49.0 km, 2,350 m up and back down

Questions

How long is the Roopkund trek?

About 49.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Roopkund trek go?

The highest point is 5,029 m, about 2,350 m above the start.

How difficult is the Roopkund trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Roopkund trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How much does the Roopkund trek cost?

₹14,900 to ₹32,000 per person: base prices from 6 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Roopkund trek?

Lohajung is the last place with a stable signal. Beyond it, networks are patchy or absent.

Where do I get cash before the Roopkund trek?

Dewal has the last reliable ATM before Lohajung. Draw enough for your cab share and offloading.

What should I pack for the Roopkund trek?

Padded jacket (−10 °C), Thermal innerwear, 1 set, Fleece or woollen layers ×2, Rain jacket or poncho, Trek pants ×2, Socks, 3–4 pairs + 2 woollen, Balaclava or buff, Gloves, two pairs. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Roopkund trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Roopkund trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 5,029 m.

Which treks are near the Roopkund trek?

Ali–Bedni Bugyal, Brahmatal, Pangarchulla, Pindari Glacier, Kuari Pass.
