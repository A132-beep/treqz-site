Stories and nature

The lake of bones

🦴The lake

Two journeys, one lake

Genetic tests on 38 skeletons found South Asians who died around 800 CE and a group with eastern Mediterranean ancestry who died around 1800 CE.

👑The legend

The king's pilgrimage

Local legend says Raja Jasdhaval of Kanauj, his queen and a troupe of dancers were caught by a storm of giant hailstones on the way to Nanda Devi and died by the lake.

🚩The pilgrimage

Nanda Devi Raj Jat

Every twelve years the Raj Jat, the Himalayan Mahakumbh, walks this same route from Wan over Bedni Bugyal and Roopkund to Homkund.

Best time for the Roopkund trek

Two windows

The lake is iced over for most of the year. The bones show for about a month after the ice melts.

🌸May–June

Snow on the upper trail and long days. Bikat's first season.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear autumn skies. Wikipedia calls mid-September to October the best time.

❄️Winter

The lake freezes and the high trail is closed in by snow.

⚖️The meadows

A 2018 High Court order stopped overnight camping on the bugyals. On 20 July 2026 the Supreme Court set aside that limit and the 200-person cap; permits and the other rules still apply (JustWravel, Aug 2026).

How to reach the Roopkund trek

Getting to Wan

Wan is reached through Lohajung, a long day by road from Dehradun or Kathgodam.

  1. Dehradun🚌 About 290 km via Rishikesh, Rudraprayag, Karnaprayag and Lohajung
  2. Lohajung🚙 About 45 minutes by jeep
  3. Wan

From Kathgodam, the nearest railhead, Lohajung is about 216 km and 8 hours by shared taxi. Nearest airport: Pantnagar.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹18,250 + 5% GST, Wan to Wan. Dehradun transport is ₹3,200 extra.

⏰Summit day

16 km and 10–12 hours, starting at 2–3 a.m. over snow.

🪪Permits

Forest permits are needed. Operators include them in the price.

🤕Altitude sickness

This trek goes above 5,000 m. Acclimatise, tell your leader about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

⚖️Open again

The Supreme Court lifted the bugyal camping ban on 20 July 2026. You still need forest permits and a registered operator. JustWravel

💵Bring cash

Dewal has the last reliable ATM before Lohajung. Draw enough for your cab share and offloading. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Lohajung is the last place with a stable signal. Beyond it, networks are patchy or absent. Trekkaro

🏡Stays in Lohajung

Homestays and guesthouses cost ₹500–1,200 a night. A thali is ₹150–300. Discover with Dheeraj

🚕Shared taxi

Shared taxis from Kathgodam to Lohajung leave 7–8 a.m. and cost about ₹800 a person. Discover with Dheeraj

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹3,500 to have your backpack carried for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🛒Shopping

Lohajung's few shops stock only basics. Bring everything you need. Trekkaro

🏥Nearest hospital

For serious problems, Karnaprayag, about 98 km away. Himalayan Hikers

🚑Evacuation

Evacuation from the upper camps is slow, and its cost is not in the trek fee. Himalayan Dream Treks

🛡️Insurance

Get a policy that covers high-altitude trekking and helicopter evacuation. Discover with Dheeraj
