Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Pindari is an easy, beautiful classic of Kumaon: villages, forest, and a glacier at the end.

The end of the road

~200kmFrom Kathgodam · Day 1, 8–9 h

The road from Kathgodam runs via Almora, Bageshwar and Kapkot to Kharkiya.

02Day 2 · Khati · 2,222 mkm 5.0

The last village

5kmToday · From the road

Khati, on the Pindar river, is the biggest and last village on the trail.

03Day 3 · Dwali · 2,575 mkm 16.9

Where two rivers meet

12kmToday · The long day

A long day up the Pindar through forest to Dwali, where the Kafni river joins the Pindar.

04Day 4 · Phurkiya · 3,240 mkm 21.9

Past the waterfall

5kmToday · Steep

A steep climb with stream crossings, past a high waterfall and the Jwarpani clearing, to Phurkiya.

05Day 5 · Zero Point · 3,660 mkm 29.5

Face to face with the glacier

20kmToday · Zero Point, then down to Dwali

The trail climbs to Zero Point, facing the Pindari glacier under Nanda Kot and Changuch.

Down the Pindar

Down the valley to Khati, then to the road at Kharkiya and a jeep onward.

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

The last ATMs are at Bharadi and Kapkot. Draw all the cash you need before Khati.

Super Trekkers
📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL works, and only patchily, at Khati. There is no signal beyond Khati.

Super Trekkers
🔌No charging

There is nowhere to charge devices on the trek. Carry a power bank.

Trekup India
💧Water

Guest houses have water, and streams run along the way. Refill whenever you can.

Holidify
🍲Food on the trail

Some KMVN rest houses serve simple dhaba meals, and there is tea near Phurkiya. Carry snacks for the rest.

Holidify
🏠Where you sleep

A homestay in Khati, 2–3 to a room. At Dwali and Phurkiya, simple guest houses with 5–6 to a room.

Questions

How long is the Pindari Glacier trek?

About 58.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Pindari Glacier trek go?

The highest point is 3,660 m, about 1,820 m above the start.

How difficult is the Pindari Glacier trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Pindari Glacier trek?

Apr–May, Sep–Oct. It is also possible in Mar, Jun.

How much does the Pindari Glacier trek cost?

₹9,500 to ₹19,950 per person: base prices from 10 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Pindari Glacier trek?

Only BSNL works, and only patchily, at Khati. There is no signal beyond Khati.
