Stories and nature

A glacier walking backwards

📉The glacier

Measured since 1906

First surveyed in 1906, the snout had retreated 1,040 m by 1958, and another 1,569 m between 1976 and 2014.

🙏The baba

Pindari Baba

Near Zero Point, Baba Dharmanand keeps a hut beside a small Nanda Devi temple. Operators say he feeds, and sometimes shelters, trekkers.

🚰The water

Pindar for dry rivers

In 2022 Uttarakhand's water corporation announced a plan to pipe Pindar tributaries to the drying Gomti, Kosi, Lodh and Gagas rivers.

Best time for the Pindari Glacier trek

Spring and autumn

The monsoon brings rain, leeches and landslides.

🌸Mid-March–mid-June

Rhododendrons and primulas in March–April.

🍂Mid-Sep–late Oct

The clearest views of Nanda Kot; nights below zero in October.

🌧️July–August

Avoid: heavy rain, leeches and landslides.

❄️Winter

Snow usually blocks the trail beyond Dwali.

How to reach the Pindari Glacier trek

Getting to Kharkiya

Kathgodam is the railhead. The last stretch of road is rough.

  1. Kathgodam🚌 ~200 km via Almora, Bageshwar, Kapkot
  2. Kharkiya🚶 5 km
  3. Khati

Nearest airport: Pantnagar, about 34 km from Kathgodam. Shared jeeps run from Bageshwar.

💰Cost

BMC Adventures charges ₹13,400 for 8 days from Kathgodam. Himalayan Dream Treks charges ₹11,500 for 6 days from Khati.

🎫Permits

Forest fees apply in the Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve; operators include them. Registration was reported mandatory from 2025.

⛰️Hardest part

The long Khati–Dwali day and the 20 km Zero Point day.

🤕Altitude sickness

Tell your guide about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last ATMs are at Bharadi and Kapkot. Draw all the cash you need before Khati. Super Trekkers

📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL works, and only patchily, at Khati. There is no signal beyond Khati. Super Trekkers

🔌No charging

There is nowhere to charge devices on the trek. Carry a power bank. Trekup India

💧Water

Guest houses have water, and streams run along the way. Refill whenever you can. Holidify

🍲Food on the trail

Some KMVN rest houses serve simple dhaba meals, and there is tea near Phurkiya. Carry snacks for the rest. Holidify

🏠Where you sleep

A homestay in Khati, 2–3 to a room. At Dwali and Phurkiya, simple guest houses with 5–6 to a room. Super Trekkers

🌡️Weather

Mid-March to mid-June: days 4 to 20 °C, nights −10 to 4 °C. Autumn days 9 to 18 °C. Himalayan Dream Treks

🐴Offloading

A mule or porter carries your bag for ₹500 a day. The bag must weigh 10 kg or less. Super Trekkers

🚕Cab from Kathgodam

Operators add a seat in their cab from Kathgodam and back for about ₹2,400 per person. The Searching Souls

🚑If you can't go on

The leader brings you back down to Khati. Evacuation insurance is not included, so buy your own. Super Trekkers
