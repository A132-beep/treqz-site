Stories and nature

A road, a war and a grove

🎩The man

David Scott

Born in Scotland in 1786, Scott was the Governor-General's agent on the North-East Frontier. He died at Cherrapunji in 1831; a stone obelisk there marks him.

⚔️The road

Tirot Sing's war

After the Treaty of Yandabo in 1826 the British wanted a road from Guwahati to Sylhet through the Khasi Hills. On 4 April 1829 Khasi fighters under Tirot Sing attacked the British at Nongkhlaw, starting about four years of war.

🌳The grove

Law Lyngdoh

The Mawphlang grove covers about 80 hectares and holds about 450 kinds of trees and plants. Cutting even a branch is forbidden.

🪨The stones

Megaliths

Mawphlang is named after its stones. Megaliths along the trail mark victories, sacrifices and graves.

Best time for the David Scott Trail trek

The dry months

Meghalaya Tourism says the trek can be done all year, but is best from October to May.

🌤️Oct–Apr

Dry and cool: the best time.

🌸May

Still good, before the rains.

🌧️Jun–Sep

Heavy rain and landslides. Take rain gear and anti-slip shoes.

🧥Winter

Cold mornings. Warm layers, and book stays early.

How to reach the David Scott Trail trek

Getting to Mawphlang

Shillong is the base. Mawphlang is about 25 km away, roughly 45 minutes by road.

  1. Shillong🚙 25 km, about 45 min; shared taxis leave from near Bada Bazaar
  2. Mawphlang🥾 16 km on the trail
  3. Lad Mawphlang🚙 By road back to Shillong

Nearest airport: Shillong, about 63 km from Mawphlang. Guwahati airport is about 151 km away. Nearest railhead: Guwahati.

🧭Guide

Hire a local guide who knows the trail. Taxi drivers often double as guides.

💰Cost

Unexplored Northeast charges ₹6,800 a person (groups of 8) for 3 days from Guwahati, with stays, meals and a guide.

🎫Entry

There is a small ticket kiosk at the Mawphlang trailhead.

📶Signal

Jio and Airtel work best on the trail.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Carry enough cash for the entry ticket, the guide and food. AllBlogs trek guide

🧭Book a guide

Guides come from the Mawphlang Guide Committee. A full trek costs ₹1,550. Call +91 88374 56191 a day ahead. Rangers of Mountains

📶Patchy signal

One walker found Jio signal only on the hilltops. Tell someone your plan and return time before you start. AllBlogs trek guide

💧Water

Carry your own bottles. You can refill at a water tap in Mawsahep. Holidify

🥪Pack lunch

There are almost no shops on the trail. Small stalls near the start and end sell tea and biscuits. Bring snacks and lunch. AllBlogs trek guide

🚕Getting to Mawphlang

Shared sumos leave from near Bara Bazaar in Shillong in the morning, about ₹70–120 a person. A private taxi for drop and pickup costs about ₹1,500–2,500. AllBlogs trek guide

🛻Getting back

Transport is limited at Lad Mawphlang. Expect to wait for a shared vehicle. Holidify

🌧️Leeches and high water

Leeches are out in the monsoon, and the river runs high and is hard to cross. Take precautions if you go after the rains begin. Holidify

🏥Medical help

Medical help on the trail is limited. Pharmacies and proper care are in Shillong. AllBlogs trek guide

☎️Tourism helpline

Meghalaya Tourism: toll-free 1800 599 2026, WhatsApp +91 76400 03050. Meghalaya Tourism
