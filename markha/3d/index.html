Stories and nature

The valley of homestays

🐆The park

Snow leopard country

Hemis National Park was founded in 1981 to protect the Rumbak and Markha catchments. It holds about 200 snow leopards.

🛕The monastery

Rinchen Zangpo's Skiu

The monastery at Skiu dates from the 11th century; its foundation was laid by Rinchen Zangpo.

💧The spring

The Amchi's sour spring

Below Kongmaru La, the sour spring of Chuskurmo is used in traditional medicine by Ladakh's Amchi doctors.

Best time for the Markha Valley trek

A summer trek

Hemis National Park's trekking routes are open from mid-June to mid-October.

🌼June

Nights −10 to 5 °C, and snow is possible.

☀️July–August

Peak season: days 10–25 °C.

🍂September

Clear, but Nimaling can drop below freezing.

❄️Winter

The high route closes; the Zanskar freezes into the Chadar.

How to reach the Markha Valley trek

Getting to Chilling

Leh is the start and end. Plan two days there to acclimatise first.

  1. Leh🚙 60 km to Chilling, about 1.5 h
  2. Chilling

At the end, Shang Sumdo is about 2 hours' drive from Leh.

💰Cost

Ju-Leh's 8-day fixed departure is €690. Bikat's 10-day Zingchen version is ₹35,500 + 5% GST.

🪪Permits

Indians no longer need an Inner Line Permit: since 2021 they pay Ladakh's online environment fee at lahdclehpermit.in (₹400 once, ₹20 a day wildlife fee, ₹50 optional Red Cross; Discover with Dheeraj, May 2026). Foreigners: see below.

⛰️Grade

Ju-Leh: moderate, 3 of 5. Bikat: difficult, BRS 5.

🤕Altitude sickness

Kongmaru La is above 5,000 m. Acclimatise in Leh first, and go down if symptoms get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Leh has plenty of ATMs. There are none on the trek. Bikat Adventures

📶Phone signal

Only postpaid SIMs work in Ladakh. On the trek, BSNL and Airtel signal comes and goes. Bikat Adventures

☎️Emergency phone

Each village has a satellite phone for emergencies. Personal satellite phones are banned in Ladakh. Ju-Leh Adventure

🔌Charging

Most homestays have solar panels, so you can charge. Bring spare batteries: the cold drains them. Ju-Leh Adventure

💧Drinking water

Homestays give boiled or filtered water. Add purification tablets to be safe. Ju-Leh Adventure

🍲Food

Families cook simple vegetarian Ladakhi food: rice, dal, momos, thukpa. They pack your lunch each morning. Ju-Leh Adventure

🏠Homestays

No running water, so no showers. Bring a sleeping bag: the blankets can be dusty. Nimaling has tents and mattresses. Ju-Leh Adventure

🐴Horses, not porters

Horses carry the luggage in Ladakh. Use a soft bag: hard suitcases can't go on horseback. Ju-Leh Adventure

🎒Offloading

Himalaya Shelter charges ₹500 a bag a day, for up to 12 kg. Himalaya Shelter
