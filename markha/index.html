Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Markha is a homestay trek: villages, monasteries and high pastures, then one big pass.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Chillingkm 0.1

Off the Zanskar

60kmFrom Leh · About 1.5 h by car

The road from Leh follows the Indus and then the Zanskar to Chilling, where the walk begins.

01Day 1 · Skiu · 3,366 mkm 6.9

An eleventh-century monastery

6.9kmToday · +189 m

Skiu has an old monastery whose foundation was laid by Rinchen Zangpo in the 11th century.

02Day 2 · Markha · 3,760 mkm 28.1

The valley's biggest village

21.2kmToday · 6–7 h

The longest day follows the river past Sara to Markha, the valley's largest village.

03Day 3 · Hankar · 4,030 mkm 39.2

Under Kang Yatse

11.2kmToday · 4–5 h

Above Markha, the Techa monastery sits on a cliff. After Umlung comes the first view of Kang Yatse.

04Day 4 · Nimaling · 4,841 mkm 49.9

The summer pastures

+836mGain today · 10.7 km · 5–6 h

A steep climb past Thachungtse onto the Nimaling plateau, right below Kang Yatse.

05Day 5 · Kongmaru La · 5,260 mkm 52.8

The top of the trek

+439mGain · From Nimaling

From Nimaling the trail climbs to Kongmaru La, the high pass of the trek, hung with prayer flags.

05Day 5 · Shang Sumdokm 66.9

Down the gorge

17kmToday · 6–8 h, −1,601 m

A steep, long descent into the Shang gorge, with several river crossings, past the sour spring of Chuskurmo and the village of Chogdo to Shang Sumdo.

Elevation: 67.0 km, 2,030 m up and back down

Questions

How long is the Markha Valley trek?

About 67.0 km of walking over 5 days on the trail.

How high does the Markha Valley trek go?

The highest point is 5,260 m, about 2,030 m above the start.

How difficult is the Markha Valley trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Markha Valley trek?

Jul–Aug. It is also possible in Jun, Sep–Oct.

How much does the Markha Valley trek cost?

₹18,450 to ₹78,500 per person: base prices from 6 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Markha Valley trek?

Only postpaid SIMs work in Ladakh. On the trek, BSNL and Airtel signal comes and goes.

Where do I get cash before the Markha Valley trek?

Leh has plenty of ATMs. There are none on the trek.

What should I pack for the Markha Valley trek?

Thermal top and bottom, Quick-dry T-shirts ×2–3, Fleece jacket, Down jacket, Waterproof, windproof jacket, Trek pants ×2, Woollen cap and sun cap, Gloves: woollen + waterproof. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Markha Valley trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Markha Valley trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 5 days on the trail, with a highest point of 5,260 m.

Which treks are near the Markha Valley trek?

Chadar, Rumtse to Tso Moriri, Beas Kund, Bhrigu Lake, Hampta Pass.
