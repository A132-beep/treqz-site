Stories and nature

The mountain of poisonous flowers

😴The view

The Sleeping Buddha

From Sandakphu the Kangchenjunga range lies along the northern horizon in the shape of a sleeping figure. To the west, Everest, Lhotse and Makalu complete four of the world's five highest peaks.

🌺The name

Height of the poisonous plant

Sandakphu is a Lepcha name for the aconite and poisonous rhododendrons on its slopes. Nearby Bikheybhanjang means "the poisonous valley".

🎏The lake

Kalapokhri

"The black lake" on the ridge is hung with prayer flags, which snap in the wind as you pass.

Best time for the Sandakphu trek

Two windows

Clear views decide this trek. Bikat recommends spring and autumn.

🌸Mar–May

Spring: days about 10–20 °C, with the ridge in flower.

🍂Sep–Nov

Autumn: days about 5–15 °C, and the clearest skies for the big peaks.

🌧️Monsoon

Cloud often hides the view entirely. Plan around it.

🌙Nights

Colder than the days suggest on the ridge, in every season.

How to reach the Sandakphu trek

Getting to Manebhanjan

The trek starts in the Darjeeling hills and ends at Sepi, so arrange transport at both ends.

  1. Bagdogra / NJP🚙 Shared or private jeep up into the hills
  2. Darjeeling🚙 About 28 km to the trailhead
  3. Manebhanjan

At the end, Sepi to Bagdogra is about 140 km and 5 hours by road.

🪪Permits

Checked at the Singalila National Park entry post. Operators include them; foreign nationals pay about ₹2,000 more.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹13,400 + 5% GST, with homestays and meals.

🛂Border

The trail crosses between India and Nepal along the ridge. Carry photo ID.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last ATM on the way is at Mirik. Draw cash in NJP, Siliguri or Bagdogra. Himalaya Trekker

📶Phone signal

BSNL has the best signal on the ridge. Jio and Airtel work at Manebhanjan but drop out above Tonglu. Trek in Sikkim

🔌Charging

You can charge at every night stop except Phalut. Carry a power bank for that night. Bikat Adventures

💧Water

Water is available at Chitrey, Lamaydhura and Meghma. There is none between Sandakphu and Phalut, so carry the whole day's water. Bikat Adventures

🍜Food on the trail

Small shops come every 2–3 km. In Tumling they sell tea, Maggi and bottled water. Discover with Dheeraj

🏠Stays and toilets

Guest houses and homestays, mostly dorms. Camps have dry-pit toilet tents. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

March–May: days 10 to 20 °C, nights 5 to 10 °C. Mid-Sep to mid-Nov: days 5 to 15 °C, nights 0 to 5 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Primary Health Centre at Sepi, about an hour from the end of the trail. The nearest big hospital is Darjeeling District Hospital. Trekup India

🐴Porter or horse

A porter costs ₹1,200 a day with food and carries up to 17–18 kg. A horse costs about ₹1,200 a day for up to four rucksacks. Himalaya Trekker

🚙Reaching Manebhanjan

A shared jeep from NJP via Darjeeling costs ₹400–600 a seat and takes 5–6 h. A private taxi costs ₹3,500–5,000. Trek in Sikkim
