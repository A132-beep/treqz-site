Stories and nature

Why it is called Kedar's throat

🧘The summit

The meditation that moved

Local telling says Shiva chose this summit to meditate, until a bull running in the valley below broke his peace. He left for Kedarnath, and the peak kept his name.

🐂The name

Kantha, the throat

In a Pandava version, Shiva took the form of a bull to avoid Bhima and sank into the earth. His body surfaced in pieces across Garhwal, and his throat, kantha, rose here.

💧The lake

A strand from the juda

Thirsty during meditation, Shiva pulled a strand of hair from his bun, the juda, and dropped it in the forest. Water gathered where it fell: Juda ka Talab.

From village to snow

Four worlds in 1,860 m

The trail climbs through the forests of Govind National Park and out above the tree line. In late March and April the snow melts and the rhododendrons bloom.

How to reach the Kedarkantha trek

Getting to Sankri

Sankri is where the walk starts. Everyone reaches it from Dehradun.

  1. Dehradun🚌 Bus all the way: 8 a.m., 9–10 h, ₹300–450
    or 🚕 shared taxi to Purola: 4.5 h, ₹300
  2. Purola🚕 Shared cab: ₹150–200, leaves before noon
  3. Sankri

Private cab from Dehradun: ₹3,500–7,000 for 4–5 people. A seat in your operator's cab: about ₹750–850.

💵Bring cash

No ATM in Sankri. Use the ATMs in Dehradun or Purola.

📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL works after Purola. Tell family you'll be offline for 4 days.

🪪Permit and ID

Park entry is given at the Sankri forest gate. Operators arrange it.

💰Cost

Guided treks cost about ₹5,000–11,500 + 5% GST per person.

🌡️Weather

Days 0 to 10 °C. Nights −10 to −15 °C in winter.

⛰️Hardest part

Summit day: a 4 a.m. climb, then a long walk down.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

🏥Nearest hospital

Health Centre, Purola. There is no doctor on the trek.

For foreign trekkers

What's different for you

Foreigners pay three times the Indian fee to enter Govind park: ₹600 instead of ₹200, plus ₹250 instead of ₹50 for each extra day. Rules change: confirm with the issuing office before you travel. Checked October 2026.

🎫Park fee

Non-Indians pay ₹600 for the first 3 days (2 nights) in Govind park and ₹250 for each extra day. Indians pay ₹200 and ₹50. These are the rates on the park's website as of October 2026. Govind park website

⛺Camping fee

Tent fees are doubled for non-Indians: ₹100 for your own 2-person tent and ₹200 for a 4-person or kitchen tent. Indians pay ₹50 and ₹100. Govind park website

💰Package prices

Bikat Adventures' price covers forest permits and camping fees only up to the Indian rate, so foreigners may be asked to pay the difference. Bikat Adventures
