Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Kedarkantha is often called the best first Himalayan winter trek: a real summit, a short approach, and snow from December to March.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Sankri · 1,950 mkm 0.1

The last road

1,950mAltitude · Village, wood and stone

The road from Dehradun climbs past Purola and Mori, then follows the Tons and Supin rivers into Govind National Park. It ends at Sankri, a village of carved wooden houses and terraced fields.

02Day 2 · Juda ka Talab · 2,773 mkm 4.0

A lake from a lock of hair

+823mGain today · 4 km · about 5 h

The trail leaves the village and climbs steadily through pine, oak and rhododendron forest. In winter the snow begins well before camp.

03Day 3 · Base Camp · 3,413 mkm 8.0

Camp on the snowline

+640mGain today · 4 km · about 3 h

Above the lake the forest thins into open meadows and snowfields. For the first time the whole summit ridge stands above camp.

04Day 4 · Summit push · 3,600 mkm 9.6

Headlamps at 4 a.m.

~400mTo the top · About 3 km, steep at the end

The group leaves camp in the dark, a line of headlamps on the snow. The angle steepens as the ridge narrows, and the last section is the hardest climbing of the trek.

04Day 4 · Summit · 3,810 mkm 11.0

The top of winter

3,810mSummit · About 12,500 ft

From the top the main Himalaya fills the eastern sky: Swargarohini, Black Peak (Kalanag), Bandarpoonch and the Ranglana and Yamunotri ranges.

04Day 4 · Hargaon · 2,700 mkm 17.0

The long way down

−1,110mDescent · From the summit

The descent goes back through Base Camp and then takes a different trail through meadows and forest to Hargaon.

05Day 5 · Sankri · 1,950 mkm 22.9

Back to the village

23kmTotal on foot · In four days

A last 6 km through the forest brings you down to Sankri by late morning, and the long drive back to Dehradun begins.

Elevation: 23.0 km, 1,860 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

Questions

How long is the Kedarkantha trek?

About 23.0 km of walking over 5 days on the trail.

How high does the Kedarkantha trek go?

The highest point is 3,810 m, about 1,860 m above the start.

How difficult is the Kedarkantha trek?

Its sources grade it Easy–moderate.

When is the best time for the Kedarkantha trek?

Dec–Feb. It is also possible in Oct–Nov, Mar–Apr.

Can I do the Kedarkantha trek in winter?

Yes: Dec–Feb is among its best months.

Is the Kedarkantha trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy–moderate and it takes 5 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,810 m.

Which treks are near the Kedarkantha trek?

Phulara Ridge, Bali Pass, Har Ki Dun, Rupin Pass, Dodital.
