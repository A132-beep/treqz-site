Stories and nature

Cold water

💧The name

Dzü-kou

Popularly read as "cold water". In the Viswema Angami dialect it is also read as "dull" or "soulless".

🌸The flower

The Dzükou lily

A pink lily that blooms in May and June, first collected in 1991 and now treated as the same species as the Shirui lily.

🐸The wildlife

Found only here

The Dzükou Valley horned frog, described in 2020, is known only from this area and is critically endangered. Blyth's tragopan, Nagaland's state bird, lives in the forest around.

Best time for the Dzükou Valley trek

Open all year

The valley is open year-round. October to April is the usual season; avoid the June–September rains.

🌼Late Sep–Oct, Mar–Apr

Wildflowers in the valley.

🌸May–Jun

How to reach the Dzükou Valley trek

Getting to Viswema

Viswema and Jakhama are villages about 2.5 km apart on the highway south of Kohima. Indian visitors need a Nagaland Inner Line Permit, from ilp.nagaland.gov.in.

  1. Kohima🚙 South on the highway
  2. Viswema🥾 Over the ridge
  3. Dzükou → Jakhama
🚯Plastic-free

The valley has been plastic-free since 2019; plastic carries a refundable deposit. No camping by the river.

📵No signal

No electricity or mobile network in the valley; calls only from the watch tower.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

There are no shops or eateries on the trail. Carry cash for the rest house, food and taxis. Muddie Trails

🚌Dimapur to Kohima

Fly to Dimapur or take the train from Guwahati. Kohima is 2–2.5 hours on by bus or shared sumo. Jessie on a Journey

🚕To the trailhead

Shared taxis to Viswema and Jakhama leave from the Networks Travel AOS bus stop in Kohima. From the Viswema village gate, a taxi to Trekker's Point costs ₹1,000–1,500. Walking the 8 km road adds 2–3 hours. Jessie on a Journey

🥪Carry lunch and water

Carry packed food from town and at least 2 litres of water for the climb. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍜Food at the valley

The rest house kitchen cooks veg and non-veg meals once a day, and noodles the rest of the time. A small stall sells biscuits, sweets and noodles. Jessie on a Journey

💧Water at camp

In summer, water comes from a stream-fed tank at the campsite. In winter, fetch it from a stream 5 minutes away. Jessie on a Journey

🛏️Beds go early

A cottage with its own bathroom costs ₹1,500. Blankets and mats are given, and tents can be rented. There is one public toilet. Arrive early, or the beds may be gone. Jessie on a Journey

🌡️Weather

Oct–Nov: days 10–20 °C, nights 3–7 °C. Dec–Feb: days 3–15 °C, nights −6 to 0 °C, with snow possible in Jan and Feb. Treks and Trails India
