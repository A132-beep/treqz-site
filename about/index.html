About Treqz

A free atlas of 59 Indian treks, each flown in 3D, with every number linked to where it came from. Treqz sells nothing: no bookings, no affiliate links, no paid listings.

Where the numbers come from

Routes and terrain. Trails follow OpenStreetMap. Heights come from AWS Terrain Tiles (SRTM elevation), and the ground is Esri World Imagery. The 3D map, the distance and the elevation profile are drawn from that data.

Days, highest point, difficulty, seasons and permits. Each trek's 3D page has a Sources section that links every source used. Where sources disagree, for example on a lake's height, the page says so and gives the range.

Prices. Operators' base prices, per person, read from their own pages. They are not quotes: GST and transport are often extra, and the starting point varies, so each price shows its basis. On each trek guide, operators are listed by price, lowest first, and none pays to be listed. All of them are on what treks cost in India, with a CSV you can download.

Run clubs. From each club's own pages and public club directories. Every city page shows the day it was last checked.

How facts are dated

Every trek guide shows "Facts checked" and a date under its name: the day that trek's research last changed. Seasons, permits and prices change, so confirm with the operator or the forest department before you go.

Found a mistake?

Email hello@treqz.com with the page and a source for the right figure, and we'll correct it and update the date.
