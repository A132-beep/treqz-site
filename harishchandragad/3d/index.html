Stories and nature

Cliff, cave and citadel

🌈The cliff

Kokankada

A huge west-facing overhang above the Konkan. A circular rainbow (a Brocken spectre) appears here; Colonel Sykes recorded one in 1835.

🕉️The cave

Kedareshwar

A 1.5 m Shiva linga stands in waist-deep water. The cave is the source of the Mangalganga river.

🏯The history

1,400 years of fort

The citadel dates to the 6th century under the Kalachuris; the caves probably to the 11th. The sage Changdev meditated here, and the Marathas took the fort in 1747.

Best time for the Harishchandragad trek

Green or cool

Bikat says it can be done all year: July–September for the scenery, October–March for cooler days.

🌧️Jul–Sep

The most scenic months, and the most popular.

🌤️Oct–Mar

Cooler, drier walking.

🐆Wildlife

Leopards may be seen in the forest beyond Taramati.

How to reach the Harishchandragad trek

Getting to Pachnai

Groups meet at Kasara station by 8 am and drive about 5 hours to Pachnai.

🌊Monsoon caution

Avoid the Kedareshwar cave in the monsoon. Don't swim in the fort's water tanks.

🔦After dark

Carry a head torch; walking at night after heavy rain is dangerous.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

From the Kalyan side, the last ATMs are in Murbad. Carry about ₹1,500–2,000 in cash. Treks and Trails

📶Signal on top

Signal is good along the highway but weak at the base village. It comes back on the fort and on Taramati. Treks and Trails

🍛Food and water

There are small eating places at the base. Stalls on the trail sell snacks, tea and bottled water. Treks and Trails

🚻No toilets at camp

There are no washrooms at the Kokankada campsite. They are only in the base village. Treks and Trails

⛺Sleep in a tent

Snakes and scorpions live on the plateau, so sleep inside a tent. The campsite can be crowded. Treks and Trails

🎫Permits

No permit is needed. The fort is inside the Kalsubai Harishchandragad Wildlife Sanctuary, and the forest office sometimes sets a fee and timings. Treks and Trails

🥾Trek shoes only

People in sandals, running shoes or repaired shoes are sent back at Kasara or Pachnai without a refund. Treks and Trails

🌡️Weather

Monsoon days are 20–30 °C with heavy rain. Winter nights drop to 10–15 °C. Bikat Adventures

🎒No offloading

Bikat has no option to offload your bag. Bikat Adventures
