Treqz · Privacy

Privacy

Short version: no accounts, nothing sold. If you sign up, we keep your details only to tell you when Treqz launches. We count visits to learn which treks people read.

What we measure

Treqz uses Google Analytics 4 to count page views, how far people scroll, which links they follow out to operators, and roughly where visits come from (country, device, browser). We use this only to decide which treks to improve and add.

Google Analytics doesn't receive anything you type into a form.

When you sign up

The early-access, waitlist and team forms ask for your email and, depending on the form, your name, city, team details and what you're interested in. We also note which page you signed up on. This goes to a Google Form and Google Sheet owned by Treqz, and we use it only to contact you about Treqz and your team. We don't sell it or share it with anyone else.

To see or delete what we hold about you, email akashtrivedi19@gmail.com and we'll do it within 30 days.

Cookies

Outside the EU, UK and Switzerland, Google Analytics sets first-party cookies (named _ga) to tell a returning visitor from a new one. In the EU, UK and Switzerland, analytics cookies are off: Google receives only cookieless signals, so no identifier is stored on your device. No advertising cookies are used anywhere.

Opting out

Block analytics with any content blocker, or install Google's Analytics opt-out browser add-on. The site works the same either way.

Third parties

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts and 3D code from jsDelivr, are hosted on GitHub Pages, and send anonymous visit counts to Cloudflare Web Analytics; each sees your IP address when your browser fetches from it. Sign-ups are sent to Google Forms. Links to operators, Wikipedia and other sources take you to their sites and their privacy policies. See Google's privacy policy for how Google handles Analytics data.
