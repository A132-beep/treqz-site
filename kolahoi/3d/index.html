Stories and nature

The source of the Lidder

📉The glacier

Shrinking fast

The glacier covered 13.57 km² in 1963 and 10.69 km² in 2005. It was about 5 km long in 1974, and in the Ice Age it reached at least 35 km.

🌊The river

Water for Anantnag

Kolahoi is the main source of the Lidder, which brings drinking and farm water to Anantnag district and joins the Jhelum near Khanabal.

🏔️The peak

Climbed in 1912

Kolahoi (5,425 m) is the highest mountain in Kashmir Division. A British medical team led by Dr Ernest Neve first climbed it in 1912.

🦌The sanctuary

Hangul and brown bear

The Overa-Aru Wildlife Sanctuary, 511 km² around Aru up to Kolahoi, shelters the hangul, the Himalayan brown bear and the Kashmir musk deer.

Best time for the Kolahoi Glacier trek

High summer

Most sources put the season between June or July and September.

🌨️June

The high section above Lidderwat may still be under snow.

☀️Jul–Aug

The main season, with some monsoon showers.

🌼September

Wildflowers and quieter trails.

❄️October

Cold and windy, with snow and whiteouts possible.

How to reach the Kolahoi Glacier trek

Getting to Aru

Srinagar is the gateway. Aru is 12 km beyond Pahalgam, at the road end.

  1. Srinagar🚕 Shared cab, about ₹3,000–5,000 a car, ~3 h
  2. Pahalgam🚙 12 km, about 30 min
  3. Aru

Local taxi unions may stop cabs from outside the region from going up to Aru.

⚠️Check it's open

Aru was shut after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack and reopened on 29 September 2025. In February 2026 high-altitude treks still needed clearance. We found no 2026 notice for Kolahoi itself.

💰Cost

Himalaya Shelter charges ₹25,000 + 5% GST, from Pahalgam. Offloading is ₹400 a bag a day.

🎫Permits

J&K charges ₹100 a day for Indians to trek in a sanctuary. Guides arrange the trek permit in Pahalgam; carry ID and a medical certificate.

📶Phone

Prepaid SIMs from outside J&K don't work. Only BSNL postpaid works in Aru.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

⚠️Check it's open

Aru was shut after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack and reopened on 29 September 2025. In February 2026, forest trek permits were still described as suspended and high-altitude treks as needing clearance. Super Trekkers, Feb 2026

💵Bring cash

Draw enough cash in Srinagar before you leave for Aru. Bikat Adventures (Tarsar Marsar, also from Aru)

📶Phone signal

Prepaid SIMs from outside J&K don't work. Only BSNL postpaid works in Aru. Bikat Adventures (Tarsar Marsar, also from Aru)

🔌No charging

There are no charging points once you leave Aru. Bring a charged power bank. Bikat Adventures (Tarsar Marsar, also from Aru)

🎫Trek permit

The permit comes from the Wildlife Warden office in Pahalgam, about ₹200–400 a person. Your guide usually arranges it. Forest staff check for a guide at Aru. ViaKashmir (April 2026)

🎟️Aru entry

J&K Tourism charges ₹30 a person to enter Aru valley. Charzan Holidays

🧭Guide and pony

A registered guide costs about ₹1,000–1,500 a day, plus food. A pony for gear is about ₹700–900 a day. ViaKashmir (April 2026)

🐴Offloading

Himalaya Shelter charges ₹400 a bag a day, for up to 12 kg. Himalaya Shelter

🚕Getting to Aru

A shared cab from Srinagar is about ₹3,000–5,000 a car for 5–6 people. A cab from Pahalgam to Aru is ₹350–500 one way. TrekUp India

🌡️Cold nights

In October, nights at the base camp drop to freezing or below. ViaKashmir (April 2026)
