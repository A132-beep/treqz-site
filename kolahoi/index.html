Questions

How long is the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

About 37.0 km of walking over 5 days on the trail.

How high does the Kolahoi Glacier trek go?

The highest point is 3,850 m, about 1,350 m above the start.

How difficult is the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Its sources grade it Easy–moderate.

When is the best time for the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Jul–Sep. It is also possible in Jun, Oct.

How much does the Kolahoi Glacier trek cost?

₹25,000 to ₹25,000 per person: base prices from 1 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Prepaid SIMs from outside J&K don't work. Only BSNL postpaid works in Aru.

How do I reach the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

A shared cab from Srinagar is about ₹3,000–5,000 a car for 5–6 people. A cab from Pahalgam to Aru is ₹350–500 one way.

Do I need a permit or pay an entry fee for the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

The permit comes from the Wildlife Warden office in Pahalgam, about ₹200–400 a person. Your guide usually arranges it. Forest staff check for a guide at Aru.

Where do I get cash before the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Draw enough cash in Srinagar before you leave for Aru.

What should I pack for the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Thermals, top and bottom, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Full-sleeve sweater, Warm jacket, closed at wrist and neck, Rain jacket and rain pants, Quick-dry track pants ×2, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap and sun hat. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Kolahoi Glacier trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Kolahoi Glacier trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy–moderate and it takes 5 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,850 m.

Which treks are near the Kolahoi Glacier trek?

Tarsar Marsar, Kashmir Great Lakes, Chadar, Markha Valley, Kareri Lake.
