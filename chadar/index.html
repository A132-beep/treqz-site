Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Chadar barely climbs at all. What makes it hard is the cold, and the ice under your feet.

The route

4 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

04Day 4 · Shingra Koma · 3,200 mkm 0.1

Onto the ice

70kmFrom Leh · 3–4 h by road

After three days in Leh to acclimatise and pass a medical, the road along the Indus and Zanskar ends near Shingra Koma.

05Day 5 · Tibb Cave · 3,190 mkm 12.4

Inside the gorge

7kmToday · About 3 h

The gorge closes in: its walls rise up to 600 m and in places the river is only about 5 m wide.

06Day 6 · Nerak · 3,350 mkm 25.5

The frozen waterfall

13kmToday · 5–6 h

The turnaround is Nerak, where a frozen waterfall hangs above the river: Ju-Leh Adventure calls it the largest in Zanskar.

08Day 8 · Shingra Komakm 51.0

Back up the river

25.5kmDays 7–8 · Back over the ice

The way back retraces the ice to Tibb, then to Shingra Koma and the drive to Leh.

Elevation: 51.0 km, 190 m up and back down

⚠️Check the ice first

The 2026 season never opened: the river did not freeze solid enough. Check with ALTOA and the Leh DC office before you book flights.

🏛️Where to get permits

Everything is done at one window: the Tourist Information Centre in Leh's main market, opposite J&K Bank.

Discover with Dheeraj
🎫Keep your boarding pass

The TIC uses it to check you spent three nights in Leh. No boarding pass, no permit.

Discover with Dheeraj
🛡️Insurance in Leh

After the medical, you buy ASC360 adventure insurance at the TIC. It covers rescue and evacuation.

Discover with Dheeraj
🚑Rescue

ASC360 rescue staff are posted along the route, and a doctor usually patrols it. Your Medical Rescue Card gets you free treatment.

Discover with Dheeraj
📶No phone signal

BSNL and Jio work in Leh. On the river there is no signal at all.

Questions

How long is the Chadar trek?

About 51.0 km of walking over 9 days on the trail.

How high does the Chadar trek go?

The highest point is 3,399 m, about 190 m above the start.

How difficult is the Chadar trek?

Its sources grade it Hard.

When is the best time for the Chadar trek?

Jan. It is also possible in Feb.

How much does the Chadar trek cost?

₹22,500 to ₹32,000 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Chadar trek?

BSNL and Jio work in Leh. On the river there is no signal at all.

Do I need a permit or pay an entry fee for the Chadar trek?

Everything is done at one window: the Tourist Information Centre in Leh's main market, opposite J&K Bank.

What should I pack for the Chadar trek?

Thermal top and bottom ×2, Fleece jacket, Heavy down jacket, Waterproof, windproof outer jacket, Trek pants ×2–3, Woollen cap or balaclava, Neck gaiter or scarf, Inner gloves + waterproof outers. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Chadar trek in winter?

Yes: Jan is among its best months.

Is the Chadar trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Hard and it takes 9 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,399 m.

Which treks are near the Chadar trek?

Markha Valley, Rumtse to Tso Moriri, Kolahoi Glacier, Beas Kund, Tarsar Marsar.
