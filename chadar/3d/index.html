Stories and nature

The winter road

🧣The porters

Gonchas and 'Julley'

Local porters in woollen robes called gonchas haul sledges over the ice, greet trekkers with 'Julley', and sometimes sing as they walk.

🛣️The future

A road that will end it

The Nimmu–Padam–Darcha road will give Zanskar year-round access. Its construction has disturbed snow leopards.

⚠️The ice

When it won't hold

In 2026 the start was postponed in January, and the river never froze solid enough: the season did not open at all (Discover with Dheeraj, citing The Tribune and Newsgram).

Best time for the Chadar trek

Only in deep winter

Leh's authorities set the start date each year after checking the ice.

❄️January

Bikat's month. Days −5 to −10 °C, nights −25 to −30 °C.

🧊February

Wikipedia and Discover Leh Ladakh extend the season into February.

⚠️Thin ice

The season depends on the ice. In 2026 it never froze solid and the trek did not open.

☀️Summer

No Chadar: the river runs free. Markha Valley is next door.

How to reach the Chadar trek

Getting to Leh

In winter Leh can be reached only by air. Plan for three days there before the ice.

  1. Delhi✈️ About 2 h to Leh
  2. Leh🚙 About 70 km via Nimmu, 3–4 h
  3. Shingra Koma

The rules reported by Discover Leh Ladakh: two days' acclimatisation, a medical on day 3, insurance, and at most 10 groups of 10 a day.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹25,500 + 5% GST, Leh to Leh. The ALTOA and wildlife fees (about ₹6,000–8,000) are paid on the spot.

🩺Medical

A medical check-up at the Tourist Information Centre in Leh is required before you get permits.

🥾Gear

Gum boots for the ice; operators list them as extra.

🥶The cold

Hypothermia and frostbite are the real risks. Layer up, keep dry, and tell your leader early.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

⚠️Check the ice first

The 2026 season never opened: the river did not freeze solid enough. Check with ALTOA and the Leh DC office before you book flights. Discover with Dheeraj, Aug 2026

🏛️Where to get permits

Everything is done at one window: the Tourist Information Centre in Leh's main market, opposite J&K Bank. Discover with Dheeraj

🎫Keep your boarding pass

The TIC uses it to check you spent three nights in Leh. No boarding pass, no permit. Discover with Dheeraj

🛡️Insurance in Leh

After the medical, you buy ASC360 adventure insurance at the TIC. It covers rescue and evacuation. Discover with Dheeraj

🚑Rescue

ASC360 rescue staff are posted along the route, and a doctor usually patrols it. Your Medical Rescue Card gets you free treatment. Discover with Dheeraj

📶No phone signal

BSNL and Jio work in Leh. On the river there is no signal at all. Discover with Dheeraj

🔋Batteries

No electricity at camp, and the cold drains batteries fast. Keep spares inside your jacket. Discover with Dheeraj

💧Hot water

Camps give hot water. Fill a flask every morning and drink all day. Discover with Dheeraj

🛌Sleeping bag

Operators give sleeping bags, but quality varies. Bring a −30 °C bag, or at least a liner. Discover with Dheeraj

⛺Tents and toilets

You sleep two to a tent. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures
