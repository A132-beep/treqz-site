The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Taluka · 2,050 mkm 0.1

Up the Supin

12kmFrom Sankri · By jeep

A jeep from Sankri reaches Taluka, and the trail follows the Supin river past Datmir and Gangad.

02Day 2 · Seema · 2,560 mkm 12.0

Across from Osla

12kmToday · From Taluka

Camp at Seema, across the river from the old village of Osla.

04Day 4 · Ruinsara Tal · 3,565 mkm 28.9

The stairway to heaven

7kmToday · About 5 h

Camp is beside Ruinsara Tal, under the massif of Swargarohini.

05Day 5 · Odari · 4,008 mkm 33.0

Bali's cave

4kmToday · 3–4 h

Odari means rock cave. Locals say the monkey king Bali crossed these mountains and killed a demon here, spending a night in the cave.

07Day 7 · Bali Pass · 4,950 mkm 37.4

The monkey king's pass

14kmPass day · About 10 h

From Bali Col camp, snow to Bali Pass, then a very steep scree and snow descent on fixed ropes.

08Day 8 · Yamunotri · 3,293 mkm 42.2

Rice in the hot spring

8kmToday · About 4 h

The trek comes down at Yamunotri, seat of the goddess Yamuna and westernmost of the Char Dham.

08Day 8 · Janki Chatti · 2,650 mkm 59.5

Down the pilgrim path

60kmTrek total · Over 7 days

The pilgrim footway leads down to the road at Janki Chatti.

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

The closest ATM is in Purola, about 25 km before Sankri. It can run out of cash.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🔌Charge in Sankri

Your Sankri stay is the last place to charge. Carry power banks for 7 days in camp.

Bikat Adventures
🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days.

Bikat Adventures
🚻Toilets

Dry pit toilet tents at camp. On hard treks they may not go up to the highest camps.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Weather

May–June days are 10 to 20 °C lower down. In Sep–Oct, days are 5 to 15 °C. High camps freeze at night.

Bikat Adventures
💧Carry 2 litres

There are no water points between Odari and the Bali Col camp. Carry at least 2 litres.

Questions

How long is the Bali Pass trek?

About 60.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Bali Pass trek go?

The highest point is 4,950 m, about 2,750 m above the start.

How difficult is the Bali Pass trek?

Its sources grade it Hard.

When is the best time for the Bali Pass trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How much does the Bali Pass trek cost?

₹16,999 to ₹22,000 per person: base prices from 7 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Where do I get cash before the Bali Pass trek?

The closest ATM is in Purola, about 25 km before Sankri. It can run out of cash.

What should I pack for the Bali Pass trek?

Full-sleeve tops ×2–3, Fleece T-shirt, Fleece jacket or sweater, Down or Hollofil jacket, Waterproof jacket or poncho, Thermal inners, Trek pants ×2, Waterproof + woollen gloves. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Bali Pass trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Bali Pass trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Hard and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,950 m.

Which treks are near the Bali Pass trek?

Har Ki Dun, Phulara Ridge, Kedarkantha, Dodital, Rupin Pass.
