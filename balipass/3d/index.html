Stories and nature

Through the Ramayana

🐒Bandarpunch

Hanuman's tail

Bandarpunch, monkey's tail, is where Hanuman is said to have put out his burning tail after Lanka. Tenzing Norgay called it the Doon School mountain.

🌺The flowers

Flowers that heal and kill

Brahma Kamal grows at Odari; poisonous aconite and buttercups line the trail too.

⏱️The record

Over in four days

In May 2025 a four-member team crossed from Gangad to Janki Chatti in four days, despite storms and snow at base camp.

Best time for the Bali Pass trek

Spring snow or autumn

The Sankri–Taluka road often closes in the monsoon.

❄️May–June

Snow above ~3,700 m; microspikes and snow slides on the descent.

🌧️Monsoon

Landslides; not run.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clearer skies, nights below zero up high.

🥶Winter

Closed.

How to reach the Bali Pass trek

Getting to Sankri

Start at Sankri, finish at Janki Chatti on the Yamunotri road.

  1. Dehradun🚌 ~220 km via Purola, 9–10 h
  2. Sankri🚙 12 km
  3. Taluka

Exit: Janki Chatti, about 180 km by road from Dehradun. No mobile signal after Sankri.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹21,500 + 5% GST, Sankri to Janki Chatti. Trekup India charges ₹19,450.

🪪Permits

Govind National Park permits; operators include them. Bring ID and a medical certificate.

⛰️Grade

Difficult (BRS 5): exposed ridges, a 60° scree slope, ropes on the descent.

🤕Altitude sickness

The pass is near 5,000 m. Tell your leader about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The closest ATM is in Purola, about 25 km before Sankri. It can run out of cash. Himalayan Dream Treks

🔌Charge in Sankri

Your Sankri stay is the last place to charge. Carry power banks for 7 days in camp. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

🚻Toilets

Dry pit toilet tents at camp. On hard treks they may not go up to the highest camps. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

May–June days are 10 to 20 °C lower down. In Sep–Oct, days are 5 to 15 °C. High camps freeze at night. Bikat Adventures

💧Carry 2 litres

There are no water points between Odari and the Bali Col camp. Carry at least 2 litres. Himalayan Dream Treks

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is in Mori, 21 km from Sankri. Himalaya Shelter

🐴If you fall ill early

The trail passes Taluka, Dharkot and Gangad. You can hire a horse or mule there to get back to Sankri. Himalaya Shelter

🎒Offloading

A porter or mule for your bag costs ₹4,800 with Bikat, ₹4,250 with Trekkaro and ₹5,500 with The Searching Souls. Bikat Adventures

🚌Bus to Sankri

Buses to Sankri leave the Mussoorie bus stand near Dehradun railway station at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 a.m. Himalayan Dream Treks
