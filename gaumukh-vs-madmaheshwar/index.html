Gaumukh Tapovan goes 963 m higher (4,460 m against 3,497 m). Gaumukh Tapovan takes 4 more days on the trail. Madmaheshwar is the easier of the two (Easy–moderate against Moderate). Both are at their best in May–Jun, Sep–Oct. The trailheads are about 42 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Gaumukh Tapovan if you want more height (4,460 m) and to go in Apr.

Pick Madmaheshwar if you want an easier trek (Easy–moderate), fewer days away (4 on the trail) and a lower price (middle ₹10,999).
