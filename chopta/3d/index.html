Stories and nature

The Lord of the Peaks

🛕The temple

Tungnath

The highest of the five Panch Kedar temples of Shiva, called by some the "Lord of the Peaks".

🏹The route

The Pandavas' path

The trek is said to follow the journey of the Pandavas from the Mahabharata through these hills.

🏔️The view

The moon rock

From Chandrashila: Chaukhamba, Kedarnath and Kedar Dome, Trishul, Nanda Devi, Thalaysagar, Bandarpoonch and the Gangotri range.

Best time for the Chopta Chandrashila trek

Snow and rhododendrons

Bikat says the trek works any time of year, but March and April are best: snow on the trail and rhododendrons in bloom.

🌸Mar–Apr

Snow on the upper trail and rhododendrons in flower.

❄️Winter

Snowfall can block the upper trail.

💰Cost

₹9,500 + 5% GST with Bikat. Dehradun transport is extra.

How to reach the Chopta Chandrashila trek

Getting to Sari

Sari is in the Ukhimath block of Rudraprayag district, about 225 km from Dehradun.

  1. Dehradun🚙 About 225 km, 7–8 h
  2. Sari

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

No ATM in Sari or Chopta. Ukhimath has plenty of ATMs. Bikat Adventures

📶Phone signal

Jio and Airtel work all along the trail. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

No electricity at the camps. Carry a power bank. Bikat Adventures

💧Drinking water

Camp water is boiled or treated with chlorine tablets. Trekkaro

⛺Stays

A homestay or guesthouse in Sari, then tents at Deoria Tal and Baniya Kund. Trekkaro

🪪Permits and papers

Operators get the permits, and the cost is in the trek fee. Carry ID and a medical certificate. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹1,650 to have your backpack carried for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared cab

A cab from Rishikesh costs about ₹6,500 one way per 5–6 seat car, split between the group. Trekkaro

🚌By bus

Buses run from Haridwar and Rishikesh to Rudraprayag or Ukhimath. Then take a shared jeep or taxi. Himalayan Hikers

🚑Rescue

The team carries you down to the road head, then a car takes you to a hospital. You pay, unless insurance covers it. Bikat Adventures
