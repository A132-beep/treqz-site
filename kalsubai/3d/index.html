Stories and nature

The three sisters

👭The name

Kalsubai

The peak is named after one of three sisters, Kalsubai, Ratnabai and Katrabai. Nearby Ratangad takes its name from Ratnabai.

🛕The summit

A temple on top

There is a Kalsubai temple near the start of the trail and another on the summit.

🏰The view

Forts all round

Alang, Madan, Kulang, Ratangad and Ajoba, and on clear days Harihar and Harishchandragad. Arthur Lake at Bhandardara lies about 6 km away.

Best time for the Kalsubai trek

After the rains

Bikat recommends October to March. Monsoon (June–September) is lush but wet and slippery.

🌤️Oct–Mar

Cooler; winter nights about 10–15 °C.

🌧️Jun–Sep

Heavy rain, 20–30 °C, slippery ladders.

🌸Sep–Oct

Flowers on the slopes.

How to reach the Kalsubai trek

Getting to Bari

Bari is the base village, reached by road from Kasara station on the Mumbai suburban line.

  1. Mumbai🚆 Train to Kasara
  2. Kasara🚙 By road
  3. Bari
🪜Hardest part

The steep last section and its ladders, hard for anyone with vertigo.

🔦Night walking

Carry a head torch; the climb is in the dark.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚆Night train to Kasara

The CSMT–Kasara fast local leaves at 8:44 pm and reaches Kasara at 11:04 pm. It stops at Dadar, Thane and Kalyan. Treks and Trails

🚙Shared jeep to Bari

From Kasara, a shared jeep to Bari costs about ₹2,600–3,000. Split it between 6 to 9 people. Trekup India

🛣️Bad last road

The road to Bari is in poor condition. Expect delays. Treks and Trails

📶Little phone signal

Signal is weak or absent around the base village. Treks and Trails

🍛Food on the way

The trail passes homes and dhabas in the farmland below the climb. Trekup India

👥Monsoon crowds

In the monsoon, 2,000–3,000 people can be on the trail. It gets slushy and slippery. Trekup India

🛕Navratri pilgrims

Thousands of pilgrims climb to the temple during Navratri. Treks and Trails

✨Firefly season

Fireflies glow around Kalsubai from mid-May to late June, before and as the rains begin. Mischief Treks

🎒No offloading

Bikat has no option to offload your bag. Carry a light daypack. Bikat Adventures
