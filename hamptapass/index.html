Stories and nature

Two valleys

🌲The Kullu side

Green

Oak, deodar and maple forests, waterfalls, and the flower meadows of Jwara.

🏜️The Lahaul side

Desert

Over the pass the land turns dry and bare: the high, desolate Chandra valley.

💧The bonus

Chandratal

A turquoise lake at 4,250 m, a drive of about 70 km from Chhatru.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs after Manali. Use the ATMs in Manali's main market. Bikat Adventures

📶No signal at camp

Manali has every network. There is no signal after Sethan, the last village, and none at the campsites. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

The last charging point is your hotel in Manali. Bring a full power bank. Bikat Adventures

💧Fill up on Day 3

On pass day the last fresh water is about 3 km out of Balu Ka Ghera. Fill both bottles before the climb. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

Days 10–15 °C. Nights 0–5 °C. Expect rain on the Kullu side. Bikat Adventures

⛺Toilets

Each camp has a toilet tent over a dry pit. Water is not allowed inside, so bring toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🐴Offloading

Bikat carries your bag for ₹1,500 for the trek. Book it with your seat. Bikat Adventures

🚕Getting to Jobra

Most fixed departures include the drive. On your own, a car from Manali to Jobra costs about ₹3,000, shared by the group. Chhatru back to Manali is about ₹9,000 for a car of five. Ascent Descent Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Civil Hospital, Manali: 01902 253385. Manali police: 01902 252326. HP trekking portal

🚑Rescue

A sick trekker is walked down to the road and driven to hospital. Rescue is not in the trek fee; insurance can cover it. Bikat Adventures
