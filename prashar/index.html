Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL and Jio, on and off at the lake. No signal on the trail. Airtel and Vi don't work. UPI may fail too.

Discover with Dheeraj
💧Fill up early

The only reliable water on the trail is about 15 minutes from Bagi. Fill your bottles there and carry at least 2 litres.

Discover with Dheeraj
🍲Food

No food on the trail. Eat at the small shops in Bagi. Cafés at the lake serve tea for ₹30–50 and meals for ₹80–150, with fewer open in winter.

Discover with Dheeraj
🛕Temple dormitory

The temple committee keeps a basic dormitory by the lake. Ask the pandit. A donation of ₹100–200 is usual.

Discover with Dheeraj
🚌Bus to Bagi

An HRTC bus leaves Mandi at 8 a.m. and reaches Bagi around 10:30, for about ₹50–80. It goes on to the lake and leaves there at 1:30 p.m. A taxi to Bagi costs ₹800–1,200.

Discover with Dheeraj
🌡️Temperatures

April–June: days 15–21 °C, nights 5–10 °C. October–November: days 8–15 °C, nights 0–5 °C. December–February: nights −10 to −5 °C.

Questions

How long is the Prashar Lake trek?

About 18.0 km of walking over 2 days on the trail.

How high does the Prashar Lake trek go?

The highest point is 2,730 m, about 1,010 m above the start.

How difficult is the Prashar Lake trek?

Its sources grade it Easy.

When is the best time for the Prashar Lake trek?

Apr–Jun, Sep–Nov. It is also possible in Dec–Mar, Jul–Aug.

How much does the Prashar Lake trek cost?

₹2,199 to ₹6,599 per person: base prices from 4 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Prashar Lake trek?

Only BSNL and Jio, on and off at the lake. No signal on the trail. Airtel and Vi don't work. UPI may fail too.
