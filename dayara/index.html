Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Dayara Bugyal is an easy trek: forest for two days, then a vast open meadow with the Garhwal peaks lined up on the horizon.

The route

6 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Raithal · 2,100 mkm 0.1

A village above the Bhagirathi

2,100mAltitude · Village of homestays

The road from Dehradun climbs to Uttarkashi and on toward Gangotri. Raithal sits on the slope above the Bhagirathi valley, 30–40 km past Uttarkashi.

02Day 2 · Gui · 2,948 mkm 6.0

Into the oak forest

+850mGain today · 6 km · about 4 h

The trail leaves Raithal and climbs steadily through forest to Gui, a clearing with a small lake.

03Day 3 · Chilapada · 3,094 mkm 12.0

The edge of the meadow

6kmToday · 5–6 h, with Barnala Tal

A gentle day. The forest opens onto the first grassland, and the trail detours to Barnala Tal, a small lake in a meadow of its own.

04Day 4 · Dayara Top · 3,750 mkm 17.0

A meadow with a view

+650mFrom Chilapada · About 5 km

The morning is a long, open climb across Dayara Bugyal, a vast high meadow, to the top at about 3,750 m.

04Day 4 · Nayata · 2,697 mkm 21.0

Back into the trees

−1,050mDown from the top · About 4 km

From the top the trail drops off the meadow and back into the forest to Nayata, the last camp.

05Day 5 · Raithal · 2,100 mkm 24.9

Down to the village

−600mDescent · 4 km · about 3.5 h

A short last walk through the forest brings you back to Raithal by late morning.

Elevation: 25.0 km, 1,610 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📶Patchy phone signal

BSNL works best. Airtel and Jio are weak in places. BSNL and Vi work in Raithal and Barsu.

Bikat Adventures
🔌Charge in Raithal

Raithal is the last place to charge. Carry a power bank for the camps.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🍲Food on the trail

Operators serve three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Some days have eggs.

Bikat Adventures
⛺Tents, not the meadow

Camping is not allowed on the main meadow. Groups camp lower, at sites like Gui or Barnala Tal.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🚻Toilets

Each camp has a dry pit toilet in a tent. Carry your own toilet paper.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🌡️Weather

From December to March, days are 5 to 15 °C. Nights drop to −5 to −10 °C.

Bikat Adventures

Questions

How long is the Dayara Bugyal trek?

About 25.0 km of walking over 5 days on the trail.

How high does the Dayara Bugyal trek go?

The highest point is 3,750 m, about 1,610 m above the start.

How difficult is the Dayara Bugyal trek?

Its sources grade it Easy.

When is the best time for the Dayara Bugyal trek?

Dec–Mar. It is also possible in Apr–Nov.

How much does the Dayara Bugyal trek cost?

₹4,700 to ₹11,299 per person: base prices from 9 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Dayara Bugyal trek?

BSNL works best. Airtel and Jio are weak in places. BSNL and Vi work in Raithal and Barsu.

What should I pack for the Dayara Bugyal trek?

Heavy down jacket, Fleece jacket + sweater, Thermals, 1–2 sets, Wind- and waterproof shell, Poncho or raincoat, Trek pants, Gloves + liners, Warm hat + balaclava or scarf. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Dayara Bugyal trek in winter?

Yes: Dec–Feb is among its best months.

Is the Dayara Bugyal trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy and it takes 5 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,750 m.

Which treks are near the Dayara Bugyal trek?

Dodital, Bali Pass, Kedartal, Har Ki Dun, Phulara Ridge.
