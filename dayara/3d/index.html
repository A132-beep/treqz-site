Stories and nature

A meadow that plays Holi with butter

🧈The festival

Andhudi Utsav

Each August the people of Raithal climb to Dayara for the Butter Festival. Instead of colours, they throw butter, milk and buttermilk, thanking the mountain deities as their cattle return from summer grazing.

🐄The meadow

A summer pasture

Bugyal means a high meadow. Through the summer, villagers graze their herds up here; in winter the same slopes lie under deep snow.

🏔️The view

The Garhwal skyline

From the top: Bandarpoonch (6,316 m) and Black Peak (6,387 m) to the north, the Gangotri peaks (Gangotri I, 6,672 m) and Jaonli to the east.

Best time for the Dayara Bugyal trek

Two seasons

Winter brings snow from December to the end of March. The rest of the year, the meadows are green.

❄️Dec–Mar

A winter snow trek. Gui lake freezes and the whole meadow is white.

🧈Mid-August

The Butter Festival on the meadow: 16–17 August in 2026, according to Incredible India. Check the date before you plan.

🌧️Monsoon

July–September brings rain and slippery forest trails, though the festival falls in this window.

💰Guided trek

Bikat Adventures charges ₹10,500 + 5% GST, Raithal to Raithal, with one night's stay in the village.

How to reach the Dayara Bugyal trek

Getting to Raithal

Raithal is on the road from Uttarkashi toward Gangotri. Everyone reaches it through Uttarkashi.

  1. Dehradun🚌 Bus to Uttarkashi: state buses leave early morning, private buses hourly
    or 🚕 private taxi all the way, usually ₹5,000 and up
  2. Uttarkashi🚕 Shared cab to Raithal or Bhatwari, 30–40 km; buses run but rarely
  3. Raithal
💵Bring cash

Draw cash in Dehradun or Uttarkashi before you go up.

🗺️Two starts

Raithal and Barsu both lead up to the meadow, each about 10–11 km one way.

⛰️Hardest part

Day 4: the climb across the meadow to the top, then about 1,000 m down to Nayata.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Patchy phone signal

BSNL works best. Airtel and Jio are weak in places. BSNL and Vi work in Raithal and Barsu. Bikat Adventures

🔌Charge in Raithal

Raithal is the last place to charge. Carry a power bank for the camps. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍲Food on the trail

Operators serve three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Some days have eggs. Bikat Adventures

⛺Tents, not the meadow

Camping is not allowed on the main meadow. Groups camp lower, at sites like Gui or Barnala Tal. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚻Toilets

Each camp has a dry pit toilet in a tent. Carry your own toilet paper. Himalayan Dream Treks

🌡️Weather

From December to March, days are 5 to 15 °C. Nights drop to −5 to −10 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Basic health centre at Bhatwari, 10–12 km from Raithal. District hospital in Uttarkashi, about an hour's drive. Himalaya Shelter

🎒Offloading

A porter or mule for your bag costs ₹1,500 with Bikat or Himalayan Dream Treks, ₹1,000 with Trekkaro. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared cab cost

Bikat's cab leaves Dehradun ISBT at 7 a.m. A Sumo for 5–6 people costs ₹6,500 each way. A Tempo for 11–12 costs ₹10,000. Bikat Adventures

🚑Rescue

Rescue is by road from the trailhead. Its cost is not in the trek fee, so get trek insurance. Bikat Adventures
