Stories and nature

A ridge among legends

🪜Swargarohini

Stairway to heaven

The Pandavas are said to have climbed Swargarohini toward heaven; only Yudhishthira made it. The massif is the source of the Tons.

🐒Bandarpunch

Hanuman's tail

Bandarpunch means monkey's tail: Hanuman is said to have put out his burning tail on its summit after the battle in Lanka.

🌸The name

Why Phulara

Himalaya Shelter links the name to flowers: in summer the Pushtara meadows bloom, and monals are often seen on the way down.

Best time for the Phulara Ridge trek

Spring and autumn

The narrow ridge is risky in bad weather, and closed in winter.

🌸May–June

Days 10–20 °C lower down; below freezing higher up at night.

🌧️Monsoon

Not recommended.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear views of Swargarohini.

❄️Winter

Snow from late November; the trek closes.

How to reach the Phulara Ridge trek

Getting to Sankri

Sankri is a long day from Dehradun. Purola has the last ATM.

  1. Dehradun🚌 ~197 km via Mussoorie, Purola, Mori
  2. Sankri

The trek ends at Taluka, 12 km of rough road from Sankri. Only BSNL and Jio work in Sankri.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹11,500 + 5% GST, Sankri to Sankri.

🪪Permits

Park permits are arranged by operators. Bring photo ID and a medical certificate.

💧Water

The 11 km ridge day has only one water source. Carry enough.

🌬️Wind

Strong winds are common on the ridge, and parts are narrow scree.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶No signal on the ridge

Jio and BSNL work at the start. On the ridge, signal is weak or gone. Himalayan Dream Treks

🔌Charging

Sankri homestays have plugs, but power cuts are long. No electricity after Sankri. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

🚻Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents. Every camp has a dry pit toilet. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is in Mori, 21 km from Sankri. Himalaya Shelter

🎒Offloading

A porter or mule for your bag costs ₹1,224 with Bikat, ₹1,600 with The Searching Souls and ₹1,800 with Trekkaro. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared cab cost

Bikat's cab leaves Dehradun ISBT at 7 a.m. A Sumo for 5–6 people costs ₹7,500 each way. A Tempo for 11–12 costs ₹11,000. Bikat Adventures

🚌Bus to Sankri

Buses to Sankri leave the Mussoorie bus stand near Dehradun railway station at 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 a.m. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚑Rescue

Rescue is by road from the trailhead. Its cost is not in the trek fee, so get trek insurance. Bikat Adventures
