Stories and nature

The meadows of the gods

🙏The pond

Bedni Kund

Pilgrims offer pindas to their ancestors at this holy pond, beside an old Nanda Devi temple and a shrine to the local god Latu.

🤫The meadow

A place of silence

Folklore forbids picking flowers, singing, playing the flute or shouting on Bedni Bugyal, home of the gods.

⚖️The law

Protected bugyals

In 2018 the Uttarakhand High Court banned overnight stays on the high meadows. On 20 July 2026 the Supreme Court set aside the camping limit and group cap; the other rules still stand (JustWravel, Aug 2026).

Best time for the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek

Almost all year

Bikat runs the trek nearly year-round: snow in winter, wildflowers through the monsoon.

🌸May–June

Days 15–20 °C, nights 5–7 °C.

🍂Sep–Nov

Days 10–15 °C, nights −5 to 5 °C, and clear views of Trishul.

🌼Monsoon

Flowers bloom on the meadows from April–May to September.

❄️Winter

Snow on the meadows. Bikat lists December batches.

How to reach the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek

Getting to Lohajung

Lohajung is a long day by road from Dehradun or Kathgodam.

  1. Dehradun🚌 294 km, 10–12 h via Rudraprayag and Karnaprayag
  2. Lohajung🚙 6 km to the Kulling trailhead
  3. Kulling

From Kathgodam, Lohajung is about 200–216 km by shared jeep. Sources give 8 to 12 hours.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹11,250 + 5% GST, Lohajung to Lohajung.

🪪Permits

Several permits are needed; operators include them. Carry ID and a medical certificate.

💵Cash

The nearest ATM is in Kathgodam. Bring what you need.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Little phone signal

BSNL works only here and there on the trail. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

No electricity at the camps. Carry a power bank. Bikat Adventures

💧Drinking water

Camp water is boiled or treated with chlorine tablets. Refill only where your leader says. Trekkaro

🏡Homestays

Didina village, on day 2, has homestays serving home-made food. Himalayan Dream Treks

⛺Toilets

Camps have dry-pit toilet tents. Bring your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared jeep

There is no direct bus from Kathgodam. A shared jeep costs about ₹900 and takes around 12 hours. Bikat Adventures

🚙Cab from Dehradun

Bikat's cab is paid on the spot: ₹8,000 a Sumo for 5–6 people, ₹12,000 a Tempo for 11–12, each way. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹1,600 to have your backpack carried for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Dewal, about 30 km from Lohajung. Himalaya Shelter

⚖️Camping on the meadows

In July 2026 the Supreme Court set aside the 2018 ban on camping on the bugyals. Forest permits are still needed. JustWravel
