Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Ali–Bedni is an easy trek with a big view: two days of forest, two days on meadows that run for kilometres.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Kullingkm 0.1

Above Lohajung

294kmFrom Dehradun · Day 1, 10–12 h to Lohajung

Day 1 is the long drive from Dehradun to Lohajung. This morning a short drive reaches the trailhead at Kulling.

02Day 2 · Didinakm 5.0

Fields of Ram Dana

5kmSo far today · Through village fields

Didina is the last village on the climb, its fields red with Ram Dana in season.

02Day 2 · Tolpani · 2,810 mkm 7.5

A camp in the forest

7.5kmToday · 4–5 h

The first camp is at Tolpani, a clearing in the forest.

03Day 3 · Ali Bugyal · 3,300 mkm 14.0

The first meadow

+500mGain today · 6.5 km · 4–4.5 h

Out of the forest and onto Ali Bugyal, a long, open high meadow.

04Day 4 · Bedni Bugyal · 3,354 mkm 15.9

Face to face with Trishul

3,803mHigh point · Bedni Top

The day crosses Bedni Bugyal, the second great meadow. Bedni Top, at 3,803 m, has a small stone temple hung with prayer flags and the whole face of Trishul and Nanda Ghunti.

04Day 4 · Gehroli Patal · 3,196 mkm 20.4

Back into the trees

6.5kmToday · 4–4.5 h

From the meadow the trail drops into the forest to Gehroli Patal.

05Day 5 · Wan · 2,450 mkm 26.9

Down to Wan

6.5kmToday · 3–3.5 h

The last walk drops through Rankandhar to Wan, and a jeep takes you back to Lohajung.

Elevation: 27.0 km, 1,300 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📶Little phone signal

BSNL works only here and there on the trail.

Bikat Adventures
🔌No charging

No electricity at the camps. Carry a power bank.

Bikat Adventures
💧Drinking water

Camp water is boiled or treated with chlorine tablets. Refill only where your leader says.

Trekkaro
🏡Homestays

Didina village, on day 2, has homestays serving home-made food.

Himalayan Dream Treks
⛺Toilets

Camps have dry-pit toilet tents. Bring your own toilet paper.

Bikat Adventures
🚕Shared jeep

There is no direct bus from Kathgodam. A shared jeep costs about ₹900 and takes around 12 hours.

Questions

How long is the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

About 27.0 km of walking over 6 days on the trail.

How high does the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek go?

The highest point is 3,803 m, about 1,300 m above the start.

How difficult is the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

Its sources grade it Easy.

When is the best time for the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Nov. It is also possible in Jul–Aug, Dec.

How much does the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek cost?

₹8,950 to ₹13,500 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

BSNL works only here and there on the trail.

What should I pack for the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

Padded jacket (−10 °C), Thermal inners, Fleece or woollen layers ×2, Rain jacket or poncho, Trek pants ×2, Socks, 3–4 pairs + 2 woollen, Sun cap and woollen cap, Gloves. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek in winter?

It is possible: its sources list Dec as workable, with snow and cold to plan for.

Is the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy and it takes 6 days on the trail, with a highest point of 3,803 m.

Which treks are near the Ali–Bedni Bugyal trek?

Brahmatal, Roopkund, Pindari Glacier, Pangarchulla, Kafni Glacier.
