Dodital goes 584 m higher (4,150 m against 3,566 m). Har Ki Dun takes 1 more day on the trail. Har Ki Dun is the easier of the two (Easy against Moderate). Both are at their best in Oct–Dec. The trailheads are about 30 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Dodital if you want fewer days away (6 on the trail), more height (4,150 m) and to go in Apr–Jun.

Pick Har Ki Dun if you want an easier trek (Easy) and to go in Sep.

Questions

Which is easier, Dodital or Har Ki Dun?

Har Ki Dun, graded Easy against Moderate.

Which costs less, Dodital or Har Ki Dun?

The middle operator price is ₹11,600 for Dodital and ₹10,500 for Har Ki Dun, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Dodital or Har Ki Dun?

Dodital reaches 4,150 m and Har Ki Dun 3,566 m.

Can I do Dodital and Har Ki Dun in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Oct–Dec.

How far apart are Dodital and Har Ki Dun?

About 30 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
