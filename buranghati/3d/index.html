Stories and nature

From the Yamuna to the Sutlej

🌊The river

Birthplace of the Pabbar

Chandranahan is the source of the Pabbar, which flows past Rohru to the Tons and the Yamuna. Across the pass, the water drains to the Baspa and Sutlej.

🌕The lake

The moon's bath

Some say Shiva's crescent moon bathed at Chandranahan, and that the Rohru valley's deity comes to dip in it every five years.

📜The forest

Birch-bark paper

Between Dayara and Litham the trail passes forests of bhoj, whose bark was used as paper in ancient times.

Best time for the Buran Ghati trek

Snow or gold

Two seasons with different passes.

❄️May–June

Snow on the upper trail and gullies to slide down.

🌧️Monsoon

Not run.

🍂Sep–Oct

Little snow, an easier climb, and gold oaks and birches.

🥶Winter

Closed by snow.

How to reach the Buran Ghati trek

Getting to Janglik

The road is bad after Chirgaon. You finish on the Kinnaur side.

  1. Shimla🚌 ~150 km via Rohru, 8–9 h
  2. Janglik

By public transport: bus to Rohru, jeeps to Chirgaon and Tangnu, then an hour's walk. The nearest ATM is at Rohru. Exit: Barua, about 250 km from Shimla.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹16,500 + 5% GST, Janglik to Barua. Treks and Trails charges ₹17,499.

🪪Permits

Forest and camping permits are arranged by operators. Bring photo ID.

🧗Pass day

The snow descent is steep and often roped. Go with an experienced team.

🤕Weather

Snowstorms have killed trekkers near Burua Kanda. Check forecasts and turn back if it closes in.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Cash only

No ATM at Janglik, on the trail or at Barua. Cards don't work on the trail. Carry small notes. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Airtel works well up to Janglik; Vodafone and BSNL are patchy there. On the trek, Airtel only at a hut 2 km before Dayara and a big boulder at Litham. Then nothing until Barua. Bikat Adventures

🔌Charging

Janglik is the only charging point, and its power comes and goes. Charge everything there. Trekkaro

🍲Food and plates

Three vegetarian meals a day, with eggs on some days. Bikat gives no plates or spoons: bring your own plate, spoon, mug and lunch box. Bikat Adventures

⛺Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents on all trek days. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Bring your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Temperatures

Lower down: days 15–20 °C, nights 5–10 °C. Higher up: days 10–15 °C, nights −5 to 5 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Rohru, about 40 km from Janglik. Himalaya Shelter

🚑Rescue

A sick trekker is carried down to the roadhead and driven to hospital. The trekker pays for the rescue, so take insurance. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹4,800 to carry your bag for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🌙Late finish

The drive back from Barua reaches Shimla around 10 p.m. or later. Book your onward trip for the next day. Trekkaro
