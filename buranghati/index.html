The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Janglik · 2,804 mkm 0.1

Where the devta decides

~150kmFrom Shimla · 8–9 h via Rohru

In Janglik the village deity, Jakh Devta, is consulted before decisions large and small. Trekkers take its blessing before they leave.

02Day 2 · Dayara · 3,366 mkm 7.6

Up the Pabbar

7.7kmToday · 5–6 h

Up the Pabbar valley to the meadow of Dayara.

03Day 3 · Litham · 3,533 mkm 13.3

Below the waterfall

5.6kmToday · 3–4 h

Litham sits below the waterfall where the Pabbar tumbles down from its source.

05Day 5 · Dhunda · 4,025 mkm 17.5

The high camp

4.3kmToday · 3–4 h

A short climb via Khubrini Thach to Dhunda, the last camp below the pass.

06Day 6 · Buran Ghati · 4,575 mkm 21.6

Down the snow wall

8kmPass day · 9–10 h

From Buran Ghati the Kinnaur side drops as a near-vertical snow wall. Groups rappel down it on fixed ropes, then slide down the snow patches below.

06Day 6 · Munirang · 3,338 mkm 25.5

Into Kinnaur

~1,200mHeight lost · From the pass

The long walk down ends at the river camp of Munirang.

07Day 7 · Barua · 2,510 mkm 31.2

Orchards of Barua

5.7kmToday · 2–3 h

The trail ends in Barua, an old Kinnauri village of wooden houses, alleys and fruit trees: apples, pears, apricots and peaches.

Elevation: 31.3 km, 1,780 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Cash only

No ATM at Janglik, on the trail or at Barua. Cards don't work on the trail. Carry small notes.

Trekkaro
📶Phone signal

Airtel works well up to Janglik; Vodafone and BSNL are patchy there. On the trek, Airtel only at a hut 2 km before Dayara and a big boulder at Litham. Then nothing until Barua.

Bikat Adventures
🔌Charging

Janglik is the only charging point, and its power comes and goes. Charge everything there.

Trekkaro
🍲Food and plates

Three vegetarian meals a day, with eggs on some days. Bikat gives no plates or spoons: bring your own plate, spoon, mug and lunch box.

Bikat Adventures
⛺Tents and toilets

Twin-sharing tents on all trek days. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Bring your own toilet paper.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Temperatures

Lower down: days 15–20 °C, nights 5–10 °C. Higher up: days 10–15 °C, nights −5 to 5 °C.

Bikat Adventures

How high does the Buran Ghati trek go?

The highest point is 4,575 m, about 1,780 m above the start.
