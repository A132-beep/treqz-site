Pin Parvati Pass goes 1,119 m higher (5,319 m against 4,200 m). Pin Parvati Pass takes 7 more days on the trail. Hampta Pass is the easier of the two (Moderate against Hard). Both are at their best in Jun–Sep. The trailheads are about 58 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Pin Parvati Pass if you want more height (5,319 m).

Pick Hampta Pass if you want an easier trek (Moderate), fewer days away (5 on the trail), a lower price (middle ₹12,425) and to go in Oct.

Questions

Which is easier, Pin Parvati Pass or Hampta Pass?

Hampta Pass, graded Moderate against Hard.

Which costs less, Pin Parvati Pass or Hampta Pass?

The middle operator price is ₹56,000 for Pin Parvati Pass and ₹12,425 for Hampta Pass, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Pin Parvati Pass or Hampta Pass?

Pin Parvati Pass reaches 5,319 m and Hampta Pass 4,200 m.

Can I do Pin Parvati Pass and Hampta Pass in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Jun–Sep.

How far apart are Pin Parvati Pass and Hampta Pass?

About 58 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
