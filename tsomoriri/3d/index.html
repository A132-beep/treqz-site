Stories and nature

Salt, cranes and nomads

🧂The salt road

The white lake

Tso Kar means 'white lake', after the salt on its shore. The Changpa sold this salt in Tibet, and the trail past Kyamar carried it to Leh.

🐦The birds

Cranes and geese

Tso Kar is a key breeding site for the black-necked crane. Tso Moriri is the bar-headed goose's only breeding ground in India. Both lakes are Ramsar wetlands.

🐐The people

The Changpa

The Changpa are nomadic herders of Tibetan origin. They keep yaks, sheep, horses and pashmina goats on these plateaus.

🛕The village

Korzok

Korzok is the only year-round village on Tso Moriri. In 2000 its people declared the lake a 'Sacred Gift for a Living Planet'.

Best time for the Rumtse to Tso Moriri trek

A short summer

Bikat runs this trek from late June to mid-September. Ju-Leh's departures run June to September.

🌼June

The season opens. Ju-Leh's first departures are in early June.

☀️July–August

The best months. Days 10–20 °C, nights 5 to −5 °C.

🍂September

The last departures, by mid-month.

❄️October–May

Not run. The roads to Leh are open only from about mid-May to October.

How to reach the Rumtse to Tso Moriri trek

Getting to Rumtse

Leh is the start and end. Spend two or three days there first to acclimatise.

  1. Leh🚙 About 80 km to Rumtse, 2 h
  2. Rumtse🥾 98 km over 7 days
  3. Korzok🚙 215 km back to Leh, about 7 h
  4. Leh

There is no bus from Korzok: book the return taxi before you start. Fly to Leh, or drive from Manali or Srinagar in about 2 days.

💰Cost

Bikat's 11-day trip from Leh is ₹55,000 + 5% GST. Ju-Leh's 11-day fixed departure is €1,250.

🪪Permits

Indians pay Leh's environment, Red Cross and wildlife fees online. Foreigners need a Protected Area Permit for Tso Moriri, through a Leh agent, in a group of two or more.

⛰️Grade

Bikat: difficult, BRS 6. Ju-Leh: level 4, with six passes above 5,000 m.

🤕Altitude

The whole trek stays above 4,000 m, so the risk of altitude sickness is high. Acclimatise in Leh first.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Leh has plenty of ATMs. There are none on the trail. Bikat Adventures

📶Phone signal

Only postpaid SIMs work in Ladakh. BSNL has the best coverage, then Airtel, but on the trek it comes and goes. Bikat Adventures

☎️Emergency phone and exit

Thugje village, about 3 km from the Ponganagu camp, has a satellite phone. Ponganagu is the only exit point if someone must leave the trek. Himalayan Ecotourism

🔌No charging

Your Leh hotel is the last place to charge. The camps have no electricity. Bikat Adventures

💧Carry water

There is no water source on the day from Ponganagu to Nuruchan. Fill up before you leave camp. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food and toilets

Vegetarian meals, with eggs on some days. Camps have dry-pit toilet tents; bring your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Cold nights

Summer days are about 10–20 °C. Nights at the camps fall to 5 °C or as low as −5 °C. Bikat Adventures

🪪Permit fees

Leh's fee covers an environment fee, the Red Cross fund and a wildlife fee. Pay it online on the district permit portal; agents get 10% off. Leh District permit portal

🐴Offloading

Bikat charges ₹3,750 to carry your rucksack for the whole trek. Himalaya Shelter charges ₹500 a bag a day, up to 12 kg. Himalaya Shelter

🚙Getting back

No buses run from Korzok to Leh. Book a taxi in advance for the 211–220 km drive. Himalaya Shelter
