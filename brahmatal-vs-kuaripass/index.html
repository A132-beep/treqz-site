Kuari Pass goes 80 m higher (3,814 m against 3,734 m). Brahmatal is the easier of the two (Easy–moderate against Moderate). Both are at their best in Dec–Feb. The trailheads are about 38 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Brahmatal if you want an easier trek (Easy–moderate).

Pick Kuari Pass if you want more height (3,814 m).

Questions

Which is easier, Brahmatal or Kuari Pass?

Brahmatal, graded Easy–moderate against Moderate.

Which costs less, Brahmatal or Kuari Pass?

The middle operator price is ₹8,750 for Brahmatal and ₹9,400 for Kuari Pass, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Brahmatal or Kuari Pass?

Brahmatal reaches 3,734 m and Kuari Pass 3,814 m.

Can I do Brahmatal and Kuari Pass in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Dec–Feb.

How far apart are Brahmatal and Kuari Pass?

About 38 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
