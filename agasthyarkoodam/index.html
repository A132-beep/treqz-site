Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📄Book on the state portal

Register at serviceonline.gov.in/trekking, then book. Pay by RuPay debit card or net banking; other cards don't work. The pass can't be transferred, and money is not refunded once it is issued.

Kerala Forest Department
🩺₹3,000 and a fitness certificate

The 2026 fee was ₹3,000: ₹2,420 trekking fee and ₹580 ecosystem fee. Bring a fitness certificate from a registered modern-medicine doctor, dated within 7 days of the trek. No certificate, no entry.

Hindu Devotional Blog
⏰Be at Bonacaud by 8 am

The group leaves the Bonacaud forest station at 8 am. Staff check the original photo ID named on your ticket first. For questions, call the Wildlife Warden, PTP Nagar, Thiruvananthapuram: +91 471 2360762.

Kerala Forest Department
🚌Bus to Bonacaud

Bonacaud is about 61 km from Thiruvananthapuram Central, via Nedumangad, Chullimanoor and Vithura. Buses run regularly from the Central bus depot.

Onmanorama, Jan 2026
🍛Food on coupons

Collect food coupons at the Bonacaud forest office. The Athirumala canteen serves lunch and dinner on them; in 2019 it ran 6:30 am to 8:30 pm. Take a packed lunch or snacks for the summit day.

Tripoto
⛺Dorm at Athirumala

The base camp is a dorm with a tin roof. It has toilets and bathrooms, and the canteen serves dinner.

Questions

How long is the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

About 48.0 km of walking over 3 days on the trail.

How high does the Agasthyarkoodam trek go?

The highest point is 1,868 m, about 1,270 m above the start.

How difficult is the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

Jan–Feb. It is also possible in Oct–Nov.

How much does the Agasthyarkoodam trek cost?

₹3,000 to ₹7,999 per person: base prices from 4 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

Expect little or no mobile network on the trek. The Forest Department uses its own wireless stations at Bonacaud and Athirumala.

What should I pack for the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

Quick-dry clothes, Raincoat, Cap and sunscreen, Trek shoes with good grip, Spare chappals, Small, comfortable backpack, Trekking pole, Bedsheet. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Agasthyarkoodam trek in winter?

Yes: Jan–Feb is among its best months.

Is the Agasthyarkoodam trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 3 days on the trail, with a highest point of 1,868 m.

Which treks are near the Agasthyarkoodam trek?

Meesapulimala, Chembra, Tadiandamol, Kumara Parvatha, Kudremukh.
