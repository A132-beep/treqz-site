Stories and nature

The sage's mountain

🧘The legend

Agastya

The peak is named after the sage Agastya, said to have done penance here. A small statue of him stands on the summit.

🚺The women

An old ban lifted

Kani tribal custom kept women off the climb. After a court order, women joined the trek in 2019. The Kani now call the sage Mootha Kani, King of the Kanis.

🌿The forest

A biosphere reserve

The Agasthyamala Biosphere Reserve joined UNESCO's world network in 2016. Its slopes hold about 2,000 medicinal plants.

🐘The animals

Elephants and tigers

Elephants, bison, leopards, tigers, bears and deer live in these forests. The Thamirabarani rises on the east side; the Neyyar and Karamana flow west.

Best time for the Agasthyarkoodam trek

A few weeks in winter

The forest is closed to trekkers most of the year. The season usually runs from mid-January to February.

📅Jan–Feb

The 2026 season ran from 15 January to 11 February. Passes sell out fast.

🍂Oct–Nov

In 2026 the Trivandrum Wildlife Division also sold an off-season package, 15 October to 15 November: ₹5,000 a person, two nights at Athirumala.

🚫Rest of the year

No trekking.

🌧️Rain

Wet granite makes the rope pitches harder.

How to reach the Agasthyarkoodam trek

Getting to Bonacaud

Thiruvananthapuram is the base. Bonacaud is about 61 km away.

  1. Thiruvananthapuram🚌 Via Nedumangad and Chullimanoor; buses from the Central depot
  2. Vithura🚌 On to the forest station
  3. Bonacaud

Nearest railway station: Thiruvananthapuram Central, about 61 km. Nearest airport: Trivandrum International, about 69 km from Bonacaud.

🎫Pass

Book online with the Kerala Forest Department. In 2026 the pass cost ₹3,000. Payment works only by RuPay debit card or net banking.

🩺Rules

Over 14 only. Carry a fitness certificate and the photo ID you booked with.

🚫Banned

No alcohol, smoking, cooking, plastic or puja items in the forest.

🌍Foreigners

The Forest Department's booking page says foreigners may not do this trek.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📄Book on the state portal

Register at serviceonline.gov.in/trekking, then book. Pay by RuPay debit card or net banking; other cards don't work. The pass can't be transferred, and money is not refunded once it is issued. Kerala Forest Department

🩺₹3,000 and a fitness certificate

The 2026 fee was ₹3,000: ₹2,420 trekking fee and ₹580 ecosystem fee. Bring a fitness certificate from a registered modern-medicine doctor, dated within 7 days of the trek. No certificate, no entry. Hindu Devotional Blog

⏰Be at Bonacaud by 8 am

The group leaves the Bonacaud forest station at 8 am. Staff check the original photo ID named on your ticket first. For questions, call the Wildlife Warden, PTP Nagar, Thiruvananthapuram: +91 471 2360762. Kerala Forest Department

🚌Bus to Bonacaud

Bonacaud is about 61 km from Thiruvananthapuram Central, via Nedumangad, Chullimanoor and Vithura. Buses run regularly from the Central bus depot. Onmanorama, Jan 2026

🍛Food on coupons

Collect food coupons at the Bonacaud forest office. The Athirumala canteen serves lunch and dinner on them; in 2019 it ran 6:30 am to 8:30 pm. Take a packed lunch or snacks for the summit day. Tripoto

⛺Dorm at Athirumala

The base camp is a dorm with a tin roof. It has toilets and bathrooms, and the canteen serves dinner. TripXL

🚯No plastic, no puja items

Plastic is strictly banned. So are puja materials, alcohol and drugs. Check posts in Neyyar, Peppara and Kottoor are watched during the season. Onmanorama, Jan 2026

📶Little or no signal

Expect little or no mobile network on the trek. The Forest Department uses its own wireless stations at Bonacaud and Athirumala. Airial

🩸Leeches

Carry a small pack of salt for leeches. Carry two 1-litre bottles; streams and waterfalls along the trail can refill them. AMJ Shots, 2018

🍂Off-season trek

The Trivandrum Wildlife Division also sells an off-season trek, 15 October to 15 November 2026, on Mondays, Thursdays and Saturdays. It costs ₹5,000 for one person or ₹25,000 for a group of up to 5, with two nights at Athirumala. Report at 8 am at the Bonacaud picket station. Trivandrum Wildlife Division
