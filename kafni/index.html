Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Kafni is Pindari's quieter twin: the same start, a fork at Dwali, a different glacier.

The last village

04Day 4 · Dwali · 2,632 mkm 30.9

The fork at Dwali

12kmToday · 5–6 h

At Dwali the Pindar and Kafni rivers meet, each from its own glacier. The left trail goes to Pindari; the right follows the Kafni.

Up the Kafni

A steep climb to the Kafni Glacier, under Nanda Kot. Unlike Pindari, you can walk right up to the ice.

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

The last ATMs on the road in are at Bharadi and Kapkot. Draw your cash before Khati.

Super Trekkers (Pindari trek)
📶Little phone signal

BSNL works up to Khati. There is no internet on the trek.

Himalayan High
🔌Charging

Khati has generator power for a fee. Beyond it, rely on a power bank.

eUttaranchal
💧Water on Dhakuri Pass

There is no water on the climb of about 9 km to Dhakuri Khal. Start with full bottles.

Himalaya Trekker
🚻Toilets

Camps have toilet tents over dry pits. Some village camps have basic fixed toilets.

Himalayan High
🌡️Weather

Higher up, about 15 °C by day and −5 °C at night.
