Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Gaumukh is a pilgrim trail and a glacier walk in one, capped at 150 people a day.

The route

6 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Gangotri · 3,415 mkm 0.1

Where the road ends

245kmFrom Dehradun · 8–9 h by road

The motor road ends at Gangotri. The trail starts about 100 m behind the temple, up a stairway.

02Day 2 · Chirbasa · 3,600 mkm 9.0

Among the pines

9kmToday · About 4 h

A gradual walk up the Bhagirathi valley to Chirbasa, named for its pine forest.

03Day 3 · Bhojbasa · 3,775 mkm 14.0

The last shelter

5kmToday · Past Gila Pahar

Bhojbasa is a riverside camp with the Lal Baba Ashram and a GMVN guest house.

04Day 4 · Gaumukh · 4,023 mkm 18.0

The cow's mouth

18kmFrom Gangotri · The snout

Over boulders and landslide zones to Gaumukh, the ice cave at the snout of the Gangotri glacier where the Bhagirathi is born.

04Day 4 · Tapovan · 4,460 mkm 20.0

The forest of penance

+460mLast stretch · In about 2 km

A steep, partly unmarked moraine climb leads to Tapovan, a meadow at the foot of Shivling.

06Day 6 · Gangotrikm 39.9

Down the Bhagirathi

14kmToday · Bhojbasa to Gangotri

Back past Gaumukh and Bhojbasa, skipping Chirbasa, to Gangotri.

Elevation: 40.0 km, 1,280 m up and back down

💵Bring cash

Uttarkashi is the last town with several ATMs, and cash can run short there. Draw cash in Dehradun.

Himalayan Dream Treks
📶Signal ends at Gangotri

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Gangotri. There is no network after it; guides use walkie-talkies.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🔌Charge in Gangotri

Gangotri is the last place to charge. Carry spare batteries and a power bank of 20,000 mAh or more.

Himalayan Dream Treks
💧Water on the way

There are many streams up to Bhojbasa; let the silt settle before you drink. Carry 2 litres from Gaumukh to Tapovan, where there may be none.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🍲Food on the trail

Only vegetarian food: rice, dal, roti, vegetables, paratha. Ask ahead for Jain meals.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🚻Toilets

Every campsite has a dry pit toilet. Carry your own toilet paper.

Questions

How long is the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

About 40.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Gaumukh Tapovan trek go?

The highest point is 4,460 m, about 1,280 m above the start.

How difficult is the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Apr–Jun, Sep–Oct. It is also possible in Nov.

How much does the Gaumukh Tapovan trek cost?

₹15,000 to ₹45,000 per person: base prices from 9 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Gangotri. There is no network after it; guides use walkie-talkies.

How do I reach the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

A Bikat Sumo from Dehradun for 5–6 people costs ₹9,000 each way. Himalayan Dream Treks charges ₹3,000 per person, return.

Where do I get cash before the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Uttarkashi is the last town with several ATMs, and cash can run short there. Draw cash in Dehradun.

What should I pack for the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Warm jacket, Full-sleeve sweater or fleece, Thermals, upper and lower, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Quick-dry track pants ×2, Rain jacket and pants, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Gaumukh Tapovan trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Gaumukh Tapovan trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,460 m.

Which treks are near the Gaumukh Tapovan trek?

Kedartal, Madmaheshwar, Dayara Bugyal, Satopanth Tal, Dodital.
