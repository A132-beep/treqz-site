Stories and nature

Where the Ganga begins

📉The glacier

A snout in retreat

The Gangotri glacier retreated 1,147 m between 1936 and 1996. Recent surveys put the snout's retreat at over 30 m a year.

🧭The explorers

The first map

John Hodgson and James Herbert made the first recorded European visit in 1817. J. B. Auden published the first scientific map of the glacier in 1937.

🌊Mauritius

Ganga water abroad

In 1972 Mauritius's prime minister Seewoosagur Ramgoolam poured water from Gaumukh into Grand Bassin, now also called Ganga Talao.

Best time for the Gaumukh Tapovan trek

Spring and autumn

Gangotri National Park is open from 1 April to 30 November; checkposts open at 6 a.m.

🌸April–June

Early in the season the Gaumukh stretch can still be under snow.

🌧️Monsoon

Landslide risk makes the trail unsafe.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear skies after the rain.

❄️Dec–March

The park is closed for winter.

How to reach the Gaumukh Tapovan trek

Getting to Gangotri

The motor road ends at Gangotri, a long day from Dehradun.

  1. Dehradun🚌 245 km via Uttarkashi, 8–9 h
  2. Gangotri

Nearest airport: Jolly Grant, Dehradun. Nearest railheads: Rishikesh and Haridwar.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹17,500 + 5% GST, Gangotri to Gangotri. Transport from Dehradun is extra.

🎫Permits

A Gangotri National Park permit (about ₹150 for Indians) from the Pathik portal or the forest offices in Uttarkashi or Gangotri. Only 150 a day.

🧭Guide

A registered guide is mandatory beyond Gaumukh to Tapovan.

🤕Altitude sickness

Tapovan is at 4,460 m. Tell your guide about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Uttarkashi is the last town with several ATMs, and cash can run short there. Draw cash in Dehradun. Himalayan Dream Treks

📶Signal ends at Gangotri

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Gangotri. There is no network after it; guides use walkie-talkies. Himalayan Dream Treks

🔌Charge in Gangotri

Gangotri is the last place to charge. Carry spare batteries and a power bank of 20,000 mAh or more. Himalayan Dream Treks

💧Water on the way

There are many streams up to Bhojbasa; let the silt settle before you drink. Carry 2 litres from Gaumukh to Tapovan, where there may be none. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍲Food on the trail

Only vegetarian food: rice, dal, roti, vegetables, paratha. Ask ahead for Jain meals. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚻Toilets

Every campsite has a dry pit toilet. Carry your own toilet paper. Himalayan Dream Treks

🌡️Weather

In Sep–Oct, Gangotri is 10 to 20 °C by day and 0 to 5 °C at night. Tapovan nights fall to −5 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is the District Hospital in Uttarkashi, near Vishwanath Chowk. Himalayan Dream Treks

🎒Offloading

A porter for your bag costs ₹3,000 with Himalayan Dream Treks, ₹4,000 with Bikat and ₹2,400 with Trekkaro. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚕Getting to Gangotri

A Bikat Sumo from Dehradun for 5–6 people costs ₹9,000 each way. Himalayan Dream Treks charges ₹3,000 per person, return. Bikat Adventures
