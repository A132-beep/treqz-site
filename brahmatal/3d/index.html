Stories and nature

Why it is called Brahma's lake

🧘The lake

Where Brahma sat

Locals say Lord Brahma meditated beside the lake below the summit ridge, and the lake carries his name: Brahma tal, Brahma's lake.

🔱The view

Trishul, the trident

The peak that fills the view from the top is Trishul, 7,120 m, named for the trident. Nanda Ghunti, 6,309 m, stands beside it.

🤫The first camp

A lake that goes quiet

Bekal Tal sits deep in the forest. In peak winter it freezes over, and trekkers remember it most for the silence.

Best time for the Brahmatal trek

A winter trek

The season runs from mid-November to mid-March. The deepest snow is from late December to February.

❄️Late Dec–Feb

Deep snow on the ridge and frozen lakes. The coldest and most beautiful weeks.

🌤️Nov and March

Less snow, clearer trails, milder nights.

🌧️Avoid July–Sept

Monsoon rain makes the forest trail slippery and hides the peaks.

📅Fixed departures

Bikat Adventures runs weekly batches from late November 2026 to March 2027, up to 15 people each.

How to reach the Brahmatal trek

Getting to Lohajung

Lohajung is where the walk starts. Kathgodam is the nearest railhead; Dehradun is the other common start.

  1. Kathgodam🚕 Taxi or shared jeep: about 220 km, 8–10 h, via Almora and Gwaldam
    or from Dehradun: about 290 km, 10–12 h
  2. GwaldamA good place to break the journey
  3. Lohajung

There are no direct buses from Kathgodam to Lohajung. Most operators sell railhead transport as an add-on.

💵Bring cash

The last ATM is at Dewal, before Lohajung. Better still, draw cash in Kathgodam or Dehradun.

💰Cost

Guided treks cost about ₹8,000–12,000 per person. Bikat Adventures charges ₹10,500 + 5% GST.

🌡️Weather

Days in single digits °C. Nights well below freezing, coldest in January.

🪪Permits

Operators arrange forest permits. On your own, ask at the forest check post in Dewal or Lohajung.

⛰️Hardest part

Summit day, then the 8.5 km descent to Lohajung, the longest day of the trek.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL works, and only now and then on the trail. Tell family you'll be offline. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

No power on the trail. Lohajung is the last place to charge, so carry a power bank. Trekkaro

💧Carry water

Water is scarce above Tilandi. Carry 2–3 litres. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍲Food

Three veg meals a day, eggs on some days. Bring your own plate, spoon and mug. Bikat Adventures

⛺Stays and toilets

Homestay in Lohajung, tents on the trail. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent: bring toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹1,600 to have your backpack carried for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared cab

A cab from Rishikesh costs about ₹6,500 one way per 5–6 seat car, split between the group. Trekkaro

🚌Direct bus

A direct bus to Lohajung leaves Rishikesh ISBT at 5 a.m. Himalayan High

🏥Nearest hospital

Dewal, about 30 km from Lohajung. Himalaya Shelter

🚑Rescue

The team carries you down to the road head, then a car takes you to a hospital. You pay, unless insurance covers it. Bikat Adventures
