The route

4 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Faraspalkm 0.1

Into the Bailadila hills

~2hFrom Jagdalpur · Leave at 7 am

The walk starts at Faraspal, about two hours by road from Jagdalpur, with a local guide.

02Balancing Rockkm 2.0

A quarter of the way

¼About · Of the way up

About a quarter of the way up, the trail passes the Balancing Rock.

03Dholkal · 3,000 ftkm 8.0

The Ganesha on the hill

~3ftIdol · One stone

A steep scramble over boulders for the last 150–250 m reaches the top of Dholkal, about 3,000 ft, where a Ganesha carved from a single stone sits on the rock.

04Faraspalkm 15.9

Down the same way

16kmTrek total · There and back

Down the same way to Faraspal. The idol was recorded in 1934 by the geologist Crookshank during the Bailadila mine survey, rediscovered in 2012, and restored by the Archaeological Survey of India after it was found broken in 2017.

Elevation: 16.0 km, 560 m up and back down

🚌Getting to Dantewada

Regular buses run from Raipur, about 400 km. Two trains a day come from Visakhapatnam. Jagdalpur has a small airport with flights to Raipur and Visakhapatnam.

Dantewada district
🛏️Where to stay

Jagdalpur has better hotels than Dantewada. Tribal homestays and night camping can be booked through Dantewada Tourism or Unexplored Bastar.

Questions

How long is the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek?

How high does the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek go?

How difficult is the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek?

Dec–Feb.

Regular buses run from Raipur, about 400 km. Two trains a day come from Visakhapatnam. Jagdalpur has a small airport with flights to Raipur and Visakhapatnam.

What should I pack for the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek?

Light woollens, Rain jacket, Hat, Walking or trekking shoes, Steel water bottle, Sunglasses, Sunscreen, Your medicines. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Dholkal (Bailadila) trek in winter?
