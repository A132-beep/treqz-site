Stories and nature

The source of the Beas

🧘The lake

Where Vyas bathed

Beas Kund is said to be where the sage Vyas bathed during his meditation. Local belief holds that bathing here cures skin ailments.

🌊The river

Vipāś, the unfettered

Legend says Veda Vyasa created the Beas from this lake. The Rigveda calls the river Vipāś, the unfettered.

👑The view

Indra's seat

Indrasan, seen from the trail, is believed to be where Lord Indra stays when he comes to earth: Indra's aasana, his seat.

Best time for the Beas Kund trek

A summer trek

The Himachal trekking portal lists the trek as open from May to October.

🌼Mid-May–June

Snow lingers on the upper trail early in the season.

🌧️Monsoon

Rain raises the river crossings. Take extra care in July and August.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear days before the trek closes at the end of October.

❄️Nov–Apr

Bikat does not recommend it: heavy snow and nights down to −10 °C.

How to reach the Beas Kund trek

Getting to Dhundi

Dhundi is up the Solang valley, a short drive from Manali.

  1. Delhi🚌 About 550 km by road to Manali
  2. Manali🚙 About 21 km via Solang, under an hour
  3. Dhundi

Nearest airport: Bhuntar (Kullu–Manali). Nearest broad-gauge railheads: Una, Kiratpur Sahib, Kalka, Chandigarh and Pathankot.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹7,500 + 5% GST, Manali to Manali.

🪪Permits

Forest permits are needed. Operators arrange them; the trek is listed on Himachal's trekking portal.

⛰️Hardest part

Day 2: boulder-hopping over old moraine and river crossings, harder in the monsoon.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs after Manali. Use the ATMs in Manali's main market. Bikat Adventures

📶Patchy signal

Bikat says Airtel and Jio work on most of the trail. Himalaya Shelter says there is no network after Manali. Make your calls before you leave. Bikat Adventures, Himalaya Shelter

🔌No charging

The last charging point is your hotel in Manali. Charge your phone and power bank before you go. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

Summer days are 10–15 °C low down and 0–5 °C near the lake. Bikat Adventures

⛺Tents and toilets

You share a two-person tent; a single tent costs ₹900 more with Bikat. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Bikat Adventures

🐴Offloading

Porters or mules carry a bag of up to 12 kg for ₹500 a day with Himalaya Shelter. Himalaya Shelter

🧳Leave extra luggage

Bikat keeps extra bags at its Jagatsukh office, 8 km from Manali. Or use the Manali bus stand cloakroom. Bikat Adventures

🚕From the airport

A taxi from Bhuntar airport to Manali costs about ₹700–1,000. Treks and Trails India

🏥Nearest hospital

Civil Hospital, Manali: 01902 253385. Manali police: 01902 252326. HP trekking portal

🚑Rescue

A sick trekker is walked down to the road and driven to hospital. Rescue is not in the trek fee; insurance can cover it. Bikat Adventures
