Stories and nature

Kheer, tents and a park

🍚The myth

Parvati's kheer

Kartikeya is said to have meditated here. Parvati cooked kheer for him, and it was later turned into water. That is why the spring runs milky white.

⛺The rule

Five hundred tents

In April 2024 the forest department counted about 500 tents at Kheerganga and garbage along the trail. It banned camping that July. In 2025 it set a limit of 320 visitors a day, against more than a lakh a season.

🌲The park

Khirganga National Park

The forests and peaks above the spring are part of Khirganga National Park, 710 km² set up in 2010.

Best time for the Kheerganga trek

Spring and autumn

The forest department opens the route from April to October. Guides add March and November in good years.

🌸April–June

Cool, moderate days.

🌧️July–August

Slippery, muddy trail, leeches and mist.

🍂Sep–Oct

After the rains. Recommended by most guides.

❄️Dec–Feb

Snow on the trail. The kund can freeze.

How to reach the Kheerganga trek

Getting to Barshaini

Kasol is the base. Taxis and buses run on to Barshaini, where the trail starts.

  1. Delhi🚌 485 km by overnight bus, about 12 h
  2. Bhuntar🚌 Local bus toward Manikaran
  3. Kasol🚕 16 km, 30–40 min
  4. Barshaini

A taxi from Kasol to Barshaini costs ₹400–600. Nearest airport: Bhuntar (Kullu–Manali), about 31 km from Kasol, but flights are few. Nearest railhead: Joginder Nagar, about 4 h by cab.

💰Cost

A day trip costs ₹1,500–2,500 a person. Registration at the Barshaini checkpost is ₹100; bring photo ID.

⏰Timings

Reach Kheerganga before 10 a.m. and leave by 2 p.m. No camping at the top. Only 320 visitors are allowed a day.

💵Cash

The last ATM is in Kasol.

⚠️Stay on the trail

Shortcuts have led to fatal falls. Since 2011, 116 people have gone missing in the Parbati valley.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last ATM is in Kasol. There is none between Kasol and Barshaini. Discover with Dheeraj

📶Little signal

Phones work in Barshaini, Kalga and Tosh. Make your calls there; at the top there may be BSNL only. Treks and Trails

🎫Register at Barshaini

Pay ₹100 a person at the Barshaini checkpost and show photo ID. Discover with Dheeraj

⏰Day trek only

Camping at Kheerganga was banned in July 2024. Reach the top before 10 a.m. and leave by 2 p.m. Sleep in Nakthan, Barshaini, Kalga or Tosh. The Tribune

💧Water

Carry at least 2 litres. Refill at streams and waterfalls on the way. Treks and Trails

🍲Food on the trail

Cafés in Nakthan serve Maggi and fresh juice. There is a dhaba at Rudra Nag and Shalom Waterfall Café about halfway. Discover with Dheeraj

🔋Power bank

There is no electricity at the top. Bring a power bank. Himalaya Shelter

🌡️Temperatures

About 13–28 °C in summer and 3–15 °C in autumn. Treks and Trails

🎒Offloading

Himalayan Hikers carries your bag for ₹800. Himalayan Hikers

🚕Getting to the trailhead

A taxi from Kasol to Barshaini costs ₹400–600 one way. Book ahead for a 5 a.m. start. Discover with Dheeraj
