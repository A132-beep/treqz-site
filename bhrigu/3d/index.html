Stories and nature

A lake that never fully freezes

🧘The lake

Where Bhrigu sat

The lake is named after the sage Bhrigu, who is said to have meditated beside it. Locals treat it as sacred and believe it never freezes over completely.

🪢The river

Vashisht, the cord-breaker

Legend says the sage Vashishta tied himself with cords and leapt into the river, which broke them and saved him. He named it Vipasa, the cord-breaker, and Vashisht village bears his name.

🏔️The view

Deo Tibba, hill of the gods

In local folklore the dome of Deo Tibba, seen from the trail, is where the gods assemble. Deo means gods; tibba means hill.

Best time for the Bhrigu Lake trek

A summer trek

The Himachal trekking portal lists the trek as open from May to October. Sources disagree on the best weeks.

🌼Mid-May–June

Outlook Traveller suggests going before June, ahead of the monsoon.

🌧️July

Outlook warns of landslide risk on the road and trail in the monsoon.

📅June–September

Bikat runs the trek through the monsoon: days 15–20 °C, nights 4–7 °C.

🍂Aug–Oct

After the heaviest rain, until the portal closes the trek at the end of October.

How to reach the Bhrigu Lake trek

Getting to Gulaba

Gulaba is on the Rohtang Pass road above Manali.

  1. Delhi🚌 About 550 km by road to Manali
  2. Manali🚙 21–27 km up the Rohtang road, 1–2 h
  3. Gulaba

Nearest airport: Bhuntar (Kullu–Manali). Nearest broad-gauge railheads: Una, Kiratpur Sahib, Kalka, Chandigarh and Pathankot.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹7,500 + 5% GST, Manali to Manali.

🪪Permits

Forest permits are needed. Operators arrange them; the trek is listed on Himachal's trekking portal.

⛰️Hardest part

Day 3: 10 km to the lake and back, about 8 hours.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs after Manali. Use the ATMs in Manali's main market. Bikat Adventures

📶Patchy signal

Bikat says Airtel and Jio work on most of the trail. Himalaya Shelter says signal is almost gone at the higher camps. Don't count on it. Bikat Adventures, Himalaya Shelter

🔌No charging

The last charging point is your hotel in Manali. Bring a full power bank. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food on the trail

Operators serve breakfast, lunch, tea and dinner. Meals are vegetarian, with eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

⛺Tents and toilets

You share a two-person tent; a single tent costs ₹1,500 more with Bikat. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Bikat Adventures

🐴Offloading

Bikat carries your bag for ₹1,600 for the trek. Book it with your seat. Bikat Adventures

🧳Leave extra luggage

Bikat keeps extra bags at its Jagatsukh office, 8 km from Manali. Or use the Manali bus stand cloakroom. Bikat Adventures

🚕From the airport

A taxi from Bhuntar airport to Manali costs about ₹700–1,000. Treks and Trails India

🏥Nearest hospital

Civil Hospital, Manali: 01902 253385. Manali police: 01902 252326. HP trekking portal

🚑Rescue

A sick trekker is walked down to the road and driven to hospital. Rescue is not in the trek fee; insurance can cover it. Bikat Adventures
