Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Rupin Pass changes every day: villages, forest, meadows, a waterfall, snow, and a pass between two states.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Dhaula · 1,554 mkm 0.1

The river's bed

210kmFrom Dehradun · About 8 h by road

The drive from Dehradun ends at Dhaula, the last roadhead in Uttarakhand.

02Day 2 · Sewa · 1,920 mkm 8.9

One idol, many temples

9kmToday · Along the Rupin

Up the valley to Sewa. Its temple is shut most of the time: its single idol is shared among 15–17 temples.

03Day 3 · Jhaka · 2,750 mkm 19.0

The hanging village

10kmToday · Via Gosangu and Jiskun

Past Jiskun to Jhaka, which clings to a cliff above the river.

05Day 5 · Dhanderas Thatch · 3,566 mkm 32.0

Below the waterfall

6kmToday · 3–4 h from Saruwas

Day 4 camps at Saruwas Thatch (not mapped). Day 5 climbs to Dhanderas Thatch, a wide meadow below the Rupin waterfall.

06Day 6 · Upper Waterfall camp · 4,080 mkm 35.9

Up beside the falls

4kmToday · About 3 h

A steep climb beside the falls to the Upper Waterfall camp, where the Rupin begins.

07Day 7 · Rupin Pass · 4,650 mkm 38.9

Two seas from one ridge

6–7hTo the pass · From 5 a.m.

Past Rati Pheri, a narrow 200 m gully leads to the prayer-flag ridge of Rupin Pass, with the Kinner Kailash range ahead.

08Day 8 · Sangla · 2,675 mkm 58.0

Into Kinnaur

13kmToday · 5–6 h

Down from Ronti Gad past Sangla Kanda to Sangla in Kinnaur.

Elevation: 58.0 km, 3,080 m up and back down

💵Bring cash

On the Dehradun route, the last ATM is in Purola, on the way to Dhaula.

Thrillophilia
🚌Starting from Shimla

Groups starting at Jiskun meet at Shimla Old Bus Stand at 6:30 a.m. Shared cabs both ways cost ₹3,500 a person. Rohru is the last dependable ATM.

Trekkaro
📶Phone signal

Jiskun is the last place with any signal. BSNL and Vodafone work there now and then. Nothing reliable after that.

Trekkaro
🔌No electricity

The camps have no electricity. Bring a power bank.

Bikat Adventures
🍲Food and plates

Three vegetarian meals a day, with eggs on some days. Bikat gives no plates or spoons: bring your own.

Bikat Adventures
🚻Toilets

Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. No water inside. Bring your own toilet paper.

Questions

How long is the Rupin Pass trek?

About 58.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Rupin Pass trek go?

The highest point is 4,650 m, about 3,080 m above the start.

How difficult is the Rupin Pass trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Rupin Pass trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How much does the Rupin Pass trek cost?

₹15,750 to ₹22,000 per person: base prices from 7 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Rupin Pass trek?

Jiskun is the last place with any signal. BSNL and Vodafone work there now and then. Nothing reliable after that.

Where do I get cash before the Rupin Pass trek?

On the Dehradun route, the last ATM is in Purola, on the way to Dhaula.

What should I pack for the Rupin Pass trek?

Thermal set, Quick-dry full-sleeve T-shirts ×2, Full-sleeve fleece, Windproof or heavy jacket, Track pants ×2, Poncho, Gloves, two pairs, Sun cap. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Rupin Pass trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Rupin Pass trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,650 m.

Which treks are near the Rupin Pass trek?

Buran Ghati, Har Ki Dun, Kedarkantha, Phulara Ridge, Bali Pass.
