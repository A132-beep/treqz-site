Stories and nature

One river, two seas

🌊The divide

Two seas from one ridge

The Rupin side drains to the Yamuna and the Bay of Bengal; the Sangla side to the Sutlej and the Arabian Sea.

🛕The temple

One idol, many temples

Sewa's temple stays shut most of the time because its one idol is shared among 15–17 temples.

🏔️The legend

The Sarasvati

Some sources cited by Wikipedia say the Sarasvati glacier near the pass is the origin of the ancient Sarasvati river.

Best time for the Rupin Pass trek

Two windows

Snow bridges and snowfields stay on the upper trail; the pass can be knee-deep in snow.

🌸May–June

Days 15–25 °C, nights 5–10 °C.

🍂Sep–Oct

Days 10–15 °C, nights below zero.

🌨️Late September

In 2026 police rescued shepherds and about 1,700 sheep and goats caught by heavy snow near the pass.

🌧️Monsoon

Rivers run high. Operators do not list July–August.

How to reach the Rupin Pass trek

Getting to Dhaula

Dhaula is a long day by road from Dehradun. You finish in Sangla, on the Shimla side.

  1. Dehradun🚌 210 km, about 8 h
  2. Dhaula

Exit: Sangla to Shimla is about 225 km and 7 hours. Bikat's current trek starts at Jiskun instead, 198 km from Shimla.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹16,650 + 5% GST for its 7-day Jiskun–Sangla version. Shimla transport is ₹4,000 extra.

⚠️Check access

In 2022 Kinnaur's administration banned treks linking Uttarkashi and Kinnaur after fatal accidents. We could not confirm whether it still applies. Ask your operator.

⛰️Hardest part

The 200 m snow gully below the pass, prone to falling stones, plus snow bridges and river crossings.

🤕Altitude sickness

Tell your leader about any symptoms. A trekker fell ill in a whiteout below the pass in 2019.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

On the Dehradun route, the last ATM is in Purola, on the way to Dhaula. Thrillophilia

🚌Starting from Shimla

Groups starting at Jiskun meet at Shimla Old Bus Stand at 6:30 a.m. Shared cabs both ways cost ₹3,500 a person. Rohru is the last dependable ATM. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Jiskun is the last place with any signal. BSNL and Vodafone work there now and then. Nothing reliable after that. Trekkaro

🔌No electricity

The camps have no electricity. Bring a power bank. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food and plates

Three vegetarian meals a day, with eggs on some days. Bikat gives no plates or spoons: bring your own. Bikat Adventures

🚻Toilets

Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. No water inside. Bring your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Rohru, about 40 km from Jiskun. Himalaya Shelter

🚑Rescue

A sick trekker is carried down to the roadhead and driven to hospital. The trekker pays for the rescue, so take insurance. Bikat Adventures

🐴Offloading

A mule can carry one soft bag under 12 kg for ₹2,800 for the whole trek. Treks and Trails

🪪Papers

ID proof and a medical certificate are mandatory. Send copies first and carry the originals. Bikat Adventures
