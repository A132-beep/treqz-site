Stories and nature

Climbers, a yeti and a lost lake

🧗The climbers

Bauer's spur

With Nepal closed, pre-war Kangchenjunga expeditions came up the Zemu Glacier. Paul Bauer's Germans tried the northeast spur in 1929 and 1931, and reached 7,400 m in 1929.

👣The legend

Tombazi's yeti

In 1925 N. A. Tombazi reported an upright, human-like figure at about 4,600 m near the Zemu Glacier. He watched it for about a minute.

🧊The glacier

The vanished lake

The Zemu is about 26 km long. Green Lake, once fed by it, has drained away: Harish Kapadia found it gone in 1976.

🌊The flood

October 2023

A flash flood from South Lhonak Lake tore down the Teesta, destroying roads, bridges and the Chungthang dam. Lachen was shut to tourists for almost three years.

Best time for the Green Lake trek

Spring and autumn

Operators run it in April–May and October–November. Himalaya Shelter also lists March, June and September.

🌸April–May

Spring. Rhododendrons bloom in the lower forest.

🌧️June–September

🍂October–November

The second season, after the monsoon.

Not offered by the operators we found.

How to reach the Green Lake trek

Getting to Lachen

Fly to Bagdogra or take the train to New Jalpaiguri, then drive via Gangtok.

  1. Bagdogra🚙 About 124 km to Gangtok, 5 h
  2. Gangtok🚙 135 km to Lachen, 6–7 h
  3. Lachen🥾 92 km out and back, 12 days

Since September 2026, vehicles must cross the Toong check-post by 4 pm in both directions, so leave Gangtok early.

💰Cost

Himalaya Shelter charges ₹78,000 + 5% GST from NJP. Glacier Treks quotes ₹1,70,000 for Indians and $2,500 for foreigners.

🪪Permits

A restricted-area permit from Sikkim's Home Department, through a registered Sikkim agency, for groups of two or more. Allow months.

⚠️2026 status

Lachen reopened to tourists on 11 September 2026. We found no operator with confirmed dates for this trek since the 2023 flood. Ask a Gangtok agency.

🤕Altitude

You sleep at 4,725 m and 4,935 m. The plan has rest days at Jakthang and Green Lake.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚧Lachen has just reopened

Lachen was closed to tourists after the October 2023 flood. Permits resumed on 11 September 2026, but landslides can still close the road. ETV Bharat

🕓Cross Toong by 4 pm

Tourist vehicles to and from Lachen must cross the Toong check-post by 4 pm. Leave Gangtok early. Livetraveller

🪪Permits take time

The restricted-area permit comes from Sikkim's Home Department, only through a registered agency, for groups of two or more. Organising it can take months. Walkopedia

💧Drinking water

Spring water is generally safe, but carry purifying tablets. The camp cooks also give boiled water. Far Out Trek

🍲Food

Hot vegetarian meals: porridge, pancakes, noodle soup, chowmein, fried rice and dal. Far Out Trek

⛺Tents and toilets

Twin-share tents with sleeping mats, plus kitchen, dining and toilet tents. Far Out Trek

🐴Offloading

Himalaya Shelter carries a personal bag for ₹500 a day, up to 12 kg. Himalaya Shelter

🚙Getting to Lachen

Bagdogra to Gangtok is about 124 km (5 h). Gangtok to Lachen is 135 km (6–7 h). Trekking in Sikkim

💸Budget for delays

Landslides or strikes can hold you up, and any extra hotel nights or transport are at your cost. Adarsh Tours
