The route

03Day 3 · Lachen · 2,730 mkm 0.1

Back after the flood

135kmFrom Gangtok · 6–7 h by road

Lachen is the trailhead. After the Teesta flood of October 2023, tourists were kept out for almost three years; permits resumed on 11 September 2026.

03Day 3 · Talem · 3,240 mkm 12.1

Where two rivers meet

12kmToday · 5–6 h

Talem sits where the Lhonak Chu joins the Zemu Chu.

04Day 4 · Jakthang · 3,430 mkm 24.0

A day to rest

12kmToday · 5–6 h

The trail climbs over a spur and drops to Jakthang, where the plan spends a rest day.

06Day 6 · Yabuk · 4,040 mkm 32.0

At the glacier

8kmToday · 4–5 h

Above Jakthang the trail reaches the Zemu Glacier. Yabuk is a cold, windy camp near the ice.

07Day 7 · Rest Camp · 4,725 mkm 40.0

Along the moraine

8kmToday · 3–4 h

The trail follows the glacier's side moraine up to Rest Camp.

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

🚧Lachen has just reopened

Lachen was closed to tourists after the October 2023 flood. Permits resumed on 11 September 2026, but landslides can still close the road.

ETV Bharat
🕓Cross Toong by 4 pm

Tourist vehicles to and from Lachen must cross the Toong check-post by 4 pm. Leave Gangtok early.

Livetraveller
🪪Permits take time

The restricted-area permit comes from Sikkim's Home Department, only through a registered agency, for groups of two or more. Organising it can take months.

Walkopedia
💧Drinking water

Spring water is generally safe, but carry purifying tablets. The camp cooks also give boiled water.

Far Out Trek
🍲Food

Hot vegetarian meals: porridge, pancakes, noodle soup, chowmein, fried rice and dal.

Far Out Trek
⛺Tents and toilets

Twin-share tents with sleeping mats, plus kitchen, dining and toilet tents.

Questions

How long is the Green Lake trek?

About 46.0 km of walking over 12 days on the trail.

How high does the Green Lake trek go?

The highest point is 4,935 m, about 2,230 m above the start.

How difficult is the Green Lake trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Green Lake trek?

Apr–May, Oct–Nov. It is also possible in Mar, Jun, Sep.

How much does the Green Lake trek cost?

₹78,000 to ₹170,000 per person: base prices from 2 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.
