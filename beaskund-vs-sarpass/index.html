Sar Pass goes 523 m higher (4,223 m against 3,700 m). Sar Pass takes 2 more days on the trail. Beas Kund is the easier of the two (Easy against Moderate). Both are at their best in Jun. The trailheads are about 50 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Beas Kund if you want an easier trek (Easy), fewer days away (3 on the trail), a lower price (middle ₹6,975) and to go in Sep–Oct.

Pick Sar Pass if you want more height (4,223 m) and to go in May.

Questions

Which is easier, Beas Kund or Sar Pass?

Beas Kund, graded Easy against Moderate.

Which costs less, Beas Kund or Sar Pass?

The middle operator price is ₹6,975 for Beas Kund and ₹8,474 for Sar Pass, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Beas Kund or Sar Pass?

Beas Kund reaches 3,700 m and Sar Pass 4,223 m.

Can I do Beas Kund and Sar Pass in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Jun.

How far apart are Beas Kund and Sar Pass?

About 50 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
