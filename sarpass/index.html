A village without a road

📶Little signal

Kasol has good signal. Grahan has none, but the guesthouse has wi-fi. Jio and Airtel work in patches, as at Nagaru camp.

Bikat Adventures
🔌Last charging in Grahan

The Grahan guesthouse has power points, but supply is patchy. After Grahan there is no electricity.

Bikat Adventures
🛒Shop before you arrive

Kasol has a market, but the group leaves for Grahan on Day 1. Don't leave buying to the last minute.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Weather

In May and June Kasol is 15–25 °C. Higher up, days are 5–10 °C and nights drop below freezing.

Bikat Adventures
🐴Offloading is dear

Locals set the price: ₹1,000 a day for a 10 kg bag. Bikat's add-on is ₹5,000 for the trek.

Questions

How long is the Sar Pass trek?

About 28.0 km of walking over 5 days on the trail.

How high does the Sar Pass trek go?

The highest point is 4,223 m, about 2,560 m above the start.

How difficult is the Sar Pass trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Sar Pass trek?

May–Jun. It is also possible in Sep–Nov.

How much does the Sar Pass trek cost?

₹5,999 to ₹12,500 per person: base prices from 8 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Sar Pass trek?

Kasol has good signal. Grahan has none, but the guesthouse has wi-fi. Jio and Airtel work in patches, as at Nagaru camp.

Where do I get cash before the Sar Pass trek?

Kasol has plenty of ATMs. There are none on the trail.

What should I pack for the Sar Pass trek?

Thermal top and bottom, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Full-sleeve sweater, Warm jacket, Rain jacket and rain pants, Quick-dry trek pants ×2, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Sar Pass trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Sar Pass trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate and it takes 5 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,223 m.

Which treks are near the Sar Pass trek?

Kheerganga, Pin Parvati Pass, Prashar Lake, Hampta Pass, Bhrigu Lake.
