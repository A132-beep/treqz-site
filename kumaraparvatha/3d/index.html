Stories and nature

The mountain of Kumara

👣The legend

A god's footprint

Legend says Lord Subramanya left his footprint in the hilltop temple. The summit has shrines to Shiva and Subramanya.

🦜The forest

Pushpagiri

Evergreen forest, shola grassland and montane forest in the Pushpagiri Wildlife Sanctuary: home to the Malabar grey hornbill, lion-tailed macaque and Nilgiri langur.

🚧The rules

Too popular

In January 2024 more than 1,500 trekkers queued in one weekend and there was a stampede at the gate. Since then: online booking only, capped at 300 a day.

Best time for the Kumara Parvatha trek

After the monsoon

Bikat runs it from September to February. The trail closes for summer and the monsoon, but sources disagree on when: June–September (Wikipedia), 1 March–30 September (Trekup), or from 15 February (DownToEarth). Check before you book.

🌤️Sep–Feb

Green after the rains, then dry and clear.

🚫Summer and monsoon

Closed. Sources put the closure anywhere from mid-February or March to September.

💰Cost

₹3,700 + 5% GST with Bikat; the forest office charges ₹250 a person.

How to reach the Kumara Parvatha trek

Book on Aranya Vihaara

Tickets are booked only online on the Karnataka forest department's portal, with a government ID; one phone number can book up to 10.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚌Bus to Kukke

KSRTC buses to Kukke Subrahmanya leave from Platform 2A at Majestic in Bangalore. Book ahead on ksrtc.in or redBus. The trail gate is a 1.5 km walk from the bus stand. Trekup India

🚆By train

Subrahmanya Road station is about 11 km from the temple. A shared jeep costs about ₹60 a seat. An auto costs about ₹300. Trekup India

🏨Stay in Kukke

Kukke has budget lodges and homestays for about ₹800–1,500 a night. Explurger

📶Little phone signal

Expect patchy or no signal on the trek. Bikat Adventures

🍛One food stop

Bhatta's house at Bhattara Mane is the only food stop. It gets crowded, so carry dry fruit, nuts and snacks. Trekup India

🚻Last toilets

Bhattara Mane has restrooms. There are no public toilets after it. Bikat Adventures

☀️Little shade up high

From Kallu Mantapa to the top you are in the sun. Sip water every 15 minutes. Trekup India

🩸Leeches

After the monsoon, leeches are on the trail from the forest start. Most people notice them at Bheema Rock. Trekup India

🚯No plastic

Plastic bottles, carry bags and food wrappers are banned on Karnataka trails. Bags are checked. Liquor, cigarettes and lighters bring a fine. Outlook Traveller

🎒Leave gear below

On summit day you can leave tents and extra gear at the forest office, then pick them up on the way down. Bikat has no offloading. Bikat Adventures

🗓️Check the closed season

Trekup India says the forest department closes the trail from 1 March to 30 September each year. Check the portal before you plan. Trekup India
