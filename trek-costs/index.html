What a trek in India really costs

344 prices from 76 operators, 59 treks, read in October 2026. No sales, no affiliate links.

The short answer

The middle price for a trek in India is ₹10,500 per person, from 344 prices that 76 operators publish for 59 treks. Half of them cost between ₹5,500 and ₹17,200. Spread over the days on the trail, that is about ₹1,959 a day.

What the price leaves out

Questions

How much does a trek in India cost?

The middle published price is ₹10,500 per person, and half of the 344 prices we found sit between ₹5,500 and ₹17,200. Longer and harder treks cost more.

How much does a Himalayan trek cost?

Across the 296 prices for Himalayan treks, the middle price is ₹12,500 per person.

What is the cheapest trek in India?

Kalsubai has the lowest middle price in the Treqz atlas, ₹1,099 per person across 4 operators.

What is the most expensive trek in India?

Green Lake has the highest middle price, ₹124,000 per person across 2 operators.

Is GST included in trek prices?

Often not. 209 of the 344 prices say GST is extra, and 29 say taxes are included. Check each operator's page before you compare.

Is transport to the trailhead included?

Often not. 67 prices say transport costs extra, and most start from the trailhead village or the nearest city. Each trek guide shows where every price starts.
