Kedartal goes 1,000 m higher (4,750 m against 3,750 m). Kedartal takes 2 more days on the trail. Dayara Bugyal is the easier of the two (Easy against Moderate–hard). Their best seasons don't overlap: Dayara Bugyal in Dec–Mar, Kedartal in May–Jun, Sep–Oct. The trailheads are about 38 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Dayara Bugyal if you want an easier trek (Easy), fewer days away (5 on the trail), a lower price (middle ₹6,500) and to go in Dec–Mar.

Pick Kedartal if you want more height (4,750 m) and to go in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

Questions

Which is easier, Dayara Bugyal or Kedartal?

Dayara Bugyal, graded Easy against Moderate–hard.

Which costs less, Dayara Bugyal or Kedartal?

The middle operator price is ₹6,500 for Dayara Bugyal and ₹15,750 for Kedartal, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Dayara Bugyal or Kedartal?

Dayara Bugyal reaches 3,750 m and Kedartal 4,750 m.

Can I do Dayara Bugyal and Kedartal in the same season?

Not at their best: Dayara Bugyal is best in Dec–Mar and Kedartal in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How far apart are Dayara Bugyal and Kedartal?

About 38 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
