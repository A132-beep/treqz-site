Sandhan Valley takes 1 more day on the trail. Both are graded Easy. Both are at their best in Oct–Mar. The trailheads are about 13 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Kalsubai if you want fewer days away (2 on the trail) and a lower price (middle ₹1,099).

Questions

Which is easier, Kalsubai or Sandhan Valley?

Both are graded Easy.

Which costs less, Kalsubai or Sandhan Valley?

The middle operator price is ₹1,099 for Kalsubai and ₹2,750 for Sandhan Valley, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Can I do Kalsubai and Sandhan Valley in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Oct–Mar.

How far apart are Kalsubai and Sandhan Valley?

About 13 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
