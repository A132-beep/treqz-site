Buran Ghati goes 1,615 m higher (4,575 m against 2,960 m). Buran Ghati takes 7 more days on the trail. Kheerganga is the easier of the two (Easy–moderate against Moderate–hard). Both are at their best in May–Jun, Sep–Oct. The trailheads are about 89 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Buran Ghati if you want more height (4,575 m).

Pick Kheerganga if you want an easier trek (Easy–moderate), fewer days away (1 on the trail), a lower price (middle ₹1,800) and to go in Apr.

Questions

Which is easier, Buran Ghati or Kheerganga?

Kheerganga, graded Easy–moderate against Moderate–hard.

Which costs less, Buran Ghati or Kheerganga?

The middle operator price is ₹17,500 for Buran Ghati and ₹1,800 for Kheerganga, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Buran Ghati or Kheerganga?

Buran Ghati reaches 4,575 m and Kheerganga 2,960 m.

Can I do Buran Ghati and Kheerganga in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How far apart are Buran Ghati and Kheerganga?

About 89 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
