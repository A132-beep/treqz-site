Stories and nature

The Curzon Trail

🚪The name

The doorway

Kuari Pass means "the doorway": the gap in the ridge that opens onto the giants of Garhwal.

🧭The history

Lord Curzon's route

Lord Curzon explored this route, and it is still known as the Curzon Trail.

🏔️The view

The giants

Nanda Devi, Dronagiri, Hathi Ghoda, Kamet, Chaukhamba and Trishul, all from one pass.

Best time for the Kuari Pass trek

A winter trek

Bikat runs it from December to March, when the trail is under snow.

❄️Dec–Feb

Deep snow; −5 to −15 °C at night up high.

🌤️March

Days about 5–12 °C, nights near or below freezing.

💰Cost

₹9,500 + 5% GST with Bikat. Dehradun transport is ₹3,000 extra.

How to reach the Kuari Pass trek

Getting to Joshimath

Joshimath is the base town; groups meet there on day one and drive up to Tugasi the next morning.

  1. Rishikesh / Dehradun🚌 Long drive up the Alaknanda valley
  2. Joshimath🚙 Short drive
  3. Tugasi
⛰️Hardest part

Day 2: 800 m of climbing to Khullara with a full pack.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Joshimath has the last ATMs, and they can run out of cash. Draw what you need in Rishikesh. Trekkaro

📶Little phone signal

Signal in Joshimath, and a weak one at Guling, the first camp. None on the rest of the trail. Himalayan Hikers

🔌No charging

No charging points on the trek. Charge everything in Joshimath the night before. Himalayan Dream Treks

💧Carry water

There is no water on the route to the pass. Fill every bottle at Khullara. Trekkaro

⛺Toilets

Camps have dry-pit toilet tents. Bring your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🪪Permits and papers

Operators get the permits, and the cost is in the trek fee. Carry ID and a medical certificate. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹1,200 to have your backpack carried for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🚌Bus or taxi

Buses from Dehradun to Joshimath take 12–13 h and cost ₹500–700. A taxi starts at about ₹3,500. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Joshimath, with basic facilities. Himalaya Shelter

🚑Rescue

The team carries you down to the road head, then a car takes you to a hospital. You pay, unless insurance covers it. Bikat Adventures
