Kedarkantha goes 110 m higher (3,810 m against 3,700 m). Phulara Ridge takes 1 more day on the trail. Phulara Ridge is the easier of the two (Easy against Easy–moderate). Their best seasons don't overlap: Kedarkantha in Dec–Feb, Phulara Ridge in May–Jun, Sep–Oct. The trailheads are about 3 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Kedarkantha if you want fewer days away (5 on the trail), a lower price (middle ₹7,000), more height (3,810 m) and to go in Dec–Feb.

Pick Phulara Ridge if you want an easier trek (Easy) and to go in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

Questions

Which is easier, Kedarkantha or Phulara Ridge?

Phulara Ridge, graded Easy against Easy–moderate.

Which costs less, Kedarkantha or Phulara Ridge?

The middle operator price is ₹7,000 for Kedarkantha and ₹9,330 for Phulara Ridge, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Kedarkantha or Phulara Ridge?

Kedarkantha reaches 3,810 m and Phulara Ridge 3,700 m.

Can I do Kedarkantha and Phulara Ridge in the same season?

Not at their best: Kedarkantha is best in Dec–Feb and Phulara Ridge in May–Jun, Sep–Oct.

How far apart are Kedarkantha and Phulara Ridge?

About 3 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
