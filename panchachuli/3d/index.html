Stories and nature

Pandavas, traders and climbers

🧗The climbs

Five first ascents

ITBP teams first climbed Panchachuli I (1972) and II (1973). An Indo-British team led by Chris Bonington and Harish Kapadia climbed V in 1992; a New Zealand team climbed IV in 1995.

🏠The huts

Igloos below the glacier

In 2021 KMVN built igloo-style huts for 30 at the base camp, 4 km from Dugtu.

🌨️The danger

Avalanche country

Blizzards often set off avalanches on the high peaks. In September 2003, nine ITBP members were caught in one.

How to reach the Panchachuli Base trek

Getting to Dharchula

Dharchula is far: about 600 km from Delhi.

  1. Delhi🚌 ~600 km, about 14 h
  2. Dharchula🚙 About 6 h via Tawaghat and Sobla
  3. Dugtu

Nearest airstrip: Naini Saini, Pithoragarh. Nearest railhead: Kathgodam. The road up the valley is rough and depends on the weather.

💰Cost

eUttaranchal charges ₹14,500 for a 6-day trek from Dharchula. Homestays in the valley cost ₹50–100 a night.

🪪Permits

An Inner Line Permit from the SDM office in Dharchula: free and same-day for Indians, not online. Foreigners need a Protected Area Permit.

🧭Trail

The path from Dugtu to the glacier is hard to follow. Go with a local guide.

🤕Altitude sickness

Tell your guide about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

No ATM beyond Dharchula, and no UPI or cards in the valley. Draw cash in Dharchula. Discover with Dheeraj

📶Little phone signal

Only BSNL works, on and off, near the road. Jio and Airtel don't work. Expect no signal past Sobla. Discover with Dheeraj

🔌Charging

Dantu has phone charging, but its solar power is limited. Carry a power bank. Super Trekkers

💧Water

Streams are plentiful, but purify before you drink. Carry purification tablets. Discover with Dheeraj

🛒Food and supplies

Dharchula is the last market. You won't find supplies on the trek. Budget ₹300–500 a day for food. Discover with Dheeraj

🌡️Weather

May–June: days 10 to 20 °C, nights 0 to 5 °C at high camps. Sep–Oct: days 5 to 15 °C, nights well below zero at base camp. Discover with Dheeraj

🎒Porter or guide

A local porter or guide costs about ₹800–1,200 a day. Discover with Dheeraj

🚌Bus to Dharchula

A UTC bus runs from Delhi's Anand Vihar ISBT. About 14 hours and ₹890 one way. Book at utconline.uk.gov.in. Discover with Dheeraj

🚙Jeep up the valley

A jeep from Dharchula to Sobla costs ₹1,500–2,500. Past Tawaghat the road is rough. Discover with Dheeraj
