Stories and nature

A valley found by accident

🧗The discovery

Lost on the way back

In 1931 Frank Smythe and fellow British mountaineers lost their way returning from Kamet and walked into this valley. They named it the Valley of Flowers.

🌼The flowers

What blooms

Brahma Kamal, Primula, Geranium, Marsh Marigold and rhododendrons, at their best from mid-July to the end of August.

🙏The shrine

Hemkund Sahib

The highest gurudwara of the Sikh community, beside a glacial lake that stays frozen for about six months a year.

Best time for the Valley of Flowers trek

A monsoon trek

The flowers come with the rains. From November to April the valley is under snow.

🌼Mid-Jul–Aug

Peak bloom. Highs near 17 °C, lows near 7 °C.

❄️Nov–Apr

Snow covers the valley and the lake.

⏰Hemkund day

Leave by 5 am and start down by 2 pm.

How to reach the Valley of Flowers trek

Getting to Govindghat

Govindghat is on the Alaknanda road beyond Joshimath. Jeeps run on to Pulna, where the trail begins.

  1. Rishikesh🚌 Long drive up the Alaknanda valley
  2. Govindghat🚙 Jeep, saves 3 km
  3. Pulna
⛰️Hardest part

Day 3: the steep climb to Hemkund, over 1,000 m above Ghangaria.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🎫Entry permit

₹200 for Indians, valid three days. Only 300 people a day, so book early at valleyofflower.uk.gov.in. Discover with Dheeraj

⏰Gate hours

Open 1 June to 31 October. Gate opens 7 a.m., last entry 2 p.m., everyone out by 5 p.m. Discover with Dheeraj

💵Last ATM

The last ATM is in Joshimath. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Only BSNL and Jio work reliably in Ghangaria. Don't expect data. Himalayan Hikers

🏨Stays

No camping in the valley. Ghangaria has a GMVN guesthouse, tented camps, homestays and a Gurudwara. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍲Food

Ghangaria has tea shops and a Gurudwara serving free langar. Trekkaro

🐴Ponies and porters

About ₹1,000–2,000 per person per day. A helicopter seat is about ₹10,800 before tax. Himalayan Dream Treks

🚙Jeep to Pulna

Shared jeeps from Govindghat to Pulna cost about ₹50 a person. Discover with Dheeraj

🚕Shared cab

A cab from Rishikesh to Joshimath costs about ₹7,500 one way per 5–6 seat car, split between the group. Trekkaro

🏥Nearest hospital

Joshimath, 25 km from Govindghat. Himalaya Shelter
