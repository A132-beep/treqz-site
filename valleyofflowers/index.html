Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Two round trips from one village: one into a national park of wildflowers, one up to a holy lake.

The route

5 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Govindghatkm 0.1

Up the Lakshman Ganga

13kmToday · About 6 h, to Ghangaria

The walk starts at Govindghat on the Alaknanda. Jeeps can take you on to Pulna, saving about 3 km.

01Day 1 · Ghangariakm 13.0

The last village

+1,300mGain today · From Govindghat

Ghangaria is the base for the next two days. There is no camping inside the valley, so every walk is a round trip from here.

02Day 2 · Valley of Flowerskm 18.0

A valley in bloom

10kmToday · There and back

Into the Valley of Flowers: meadows of Brahma Kamal, Primula, Geranium, Marsh Marigold and rhododendrons.

03Day 3 · Hemkund Sahib · 4,329 mkm 28.0

The highest gurudwara

10kmToday · There and back, start 5 am

A steep early climb from Ghangaria to Hemkund Sahib, beside a glacial lake. It is the highest gurudwara of the Sikh community.

04Day 4 · Govindghatkm 46.0

Down the same way

13kmToday · Downhill

Back down beside the Lakshman Ganga to Govindghat and the road.

Elevation: 46.0 km, 2,390 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

🎫Entry permit

₹200 for Indians, valid three days. Only 300 people a day, so book early at valleyofflower.uk.gov.in.

Discover with Dheeraj
⏰Gate hours

Open 1 June to 31 October. Gate opens 7 a.m., last entry 2 p.m., everyone out by 5 p.m.

Discover with Dheeraj
💵Last ATM

The last ATM is in Joshimath.

Trekkaro
📶Phone signal

Only BSNL and Jio work reliably in Ghangaria. Don't expect data.

Himalayan Hikers
🏨Stays

No camping in the valley. Ghangaria has a GMVN guesthouse, tented camps, homestays and a Gurudwara.

Himalayan Dream Treks
🍲Food

Ghangaria has tea shops and a Gurudwara serving free langar.
