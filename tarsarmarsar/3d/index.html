Stories and nature

The twin sisters

💌The poem

Twin sisters

Tarsar and Marsar are separated by one mountain and called twin sisters. Yusuf Shah Chak, a 16th-century ruler of Kashmir, compared his tears to their streams in a love poem.

🌊The water

Where Dal Lake begins

Marsar's outflow runs through Dachigam and becomes the Telbal Nallah, the main source of Dal Lake in Srinagar. Tarsar drains the other way, into the Lidder.

🦌The wildlife

Hangul country

The Tarsar basin and Dachigam are among the main homes of the hangul, the Kashmir stag, with ibex, musk deer, snow leopard and brown bear.

How to reach the Tarsar Marsar trek

Getting to Aru

Aru is 12 km beyond Pahalgam, a short drive from Srinagar.

  1. Srinagar🚙 About 103 km via Pahalgam, about 2 h
  2. Aru

Srinagar airport is about 12 km from the city centre. The nearest railheads are Jammu Tawi and Udhampur.

⚠️Check it's open

Kashmir's trekking routes were shut during security reviews after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack. As of July 2026 no official reopening notice had been found (Travel Coffee, July 2026). Confirm before you book.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹17,250 + 5% GST, Aru to Aru. Srinagar transport is ₹1,600 extra.

🪪Permits

Forest and trek permits are needed. Bring ID, a medical certificate and copies.

⛰️Grade

Moderate. Bikat asks for one earlier high-altitude trek.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Phone signal

Only BSNL postpaid works in Aru. There is no signal on the trail. Bikat Adventures

🔌No charging

Aru is the last place with power. Carry a power bank of 10,000 mAh or more. Himalayan Hikers

⛺Tents and toilets

You sleep in tents at every camp. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

Days 15–20 °C. Nights 1–5 °C. TrekUp India

🏥Nearest hospital

Government Hospital, Pahalgam, 12 km from Aru. Serious cases go to GMC Anantnag, 55 km away. TrekUp India

🚑Rescue

There is no road beyond Aru. An injured trekker is carried out by stretcher or pony: 6–10 hours from the farthest camps. TrekUp India

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹2,250 to carry your backpack for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🚕Srinagar to Aru

A seat in a shared 5–6 seat cab is about ₹600–700. The drive takes about 3 hours. Treks and Trails India

✈️From the airport

Prepaid taxis at Srinagar airport cost about ₹600–900. Himalayan Hikers

📄Copies for permits

Bring four copies of your ID and four photos. You also sign a disclaimer form. Kashmir Travels
