Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Tarsar Marsar is a lake trek: pine forest, shepherds' meadows, and three high lakes in a row.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Aru · 2,450 mkm 0.1

Beyond Pahalgam

103kmFrom Srinagar · About 2 h by road

The drive from Srinagar runs through Pahalgam to Aru, 12 km further up the valley. Aru is called Adav in Kashmiri.

02Day 2 · Lidderwat · 2,750 mkm 8.0

Along the Lidder

8kmToday · Through pine forest

A gradual climb through pine forest, then a level walk beside the Lidder river to the camp at Lidderwat.

03Day 3 · Sekhwas · 3,400 mkm 14.0

Up the meadows

+650mGain today · 6 km · about 3 h

A steep hour out of Lidderwat, then level ground and a gentle climb to Sekhwas, high in the meadows.

04Day 4 · Tarsar · 3,800 mkm 17.9

The first sister

4kmToday · About 2 h

A short climb brings the first view of Tarsar, an almond-shaped lake under bare ridges. Camp is beside it.

05Day 5 · Sundersar · 3,950 mkm 24.0

Over the ridge to the moon lake

6kmToday · Over the pass

An hour's climb to the ridge above Tarsar, then down along a stream to Sundersar.

06Day 6 · Marsar ridge · 4,024 mkm 26.4

The second sister

4,024mHigh point · Marsar ridge

An hour from Sundersar the ridge opens over Marsar, the twin of Tarsar, often wrapped in cloud.

07Day 7 · Arukm 46.0

Down to the valley

13kmToday · About 6 h

Down past Lidderwat to Aru by afternoon, then the drive back to Srinagar.

Elevation: 46.0 km, 1,640 m up and back down

🔌No charging

Aru is the last place with power. Carry a power bank of 10,000 mAh or more.

Himalayan Hikers
⛺Tents and toilets

You sleep in tents at every camp. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Carry your own toilet paper.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Weather

Days 15–20 °C. Nights 1–5 °C.

TrekUp India
🏥Nearest hospital

Government Hospital, Pahalgam, 12 km from Aru. Serious cases go to GMC Anantnag, 55 km away.

TrekUp India
🚑Rescue

There is no road beyond Aru. An injured trekker is carried out by stretcher or pony: 6–10 hours from the farthest camps.

Questions

How long is the Tarsar Marsar trek?

About 46.0 km of walking over 7 days on the trail.

How high does the Tarsar Marsar trek go?

The highest point is 4,024 m, about 1,640 m above the start.

How difficult is the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Jul–Aug. It is also possible in Jun, Sep.

How much does the Tarsar Marsar trek cost?

₹13,500 to ₹17,250 per person: base prices from 8 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Only BSNL postpaid works in Aru. There is no signal on the trail.

Do I need a permit or pay an entry fee for the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Bring four copies of your ID and four photos. You also sign a disclaimer form.

What should I pack for the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Thermal top and bottom, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Quick-dry trek pants ×2, Full-sleeve sweater, Warm jacket, Rain jacket and rain pants, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap and sun hat. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Tarsar Marsar trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Tarsar Marsar trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate and it takes 7 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,024 m.

Which treks are near the Tarsar Marsar trek?

Kolahoi Glacier, Kashmir Great Lakes, Chadar, Markha Valley, Kareri Lake.
