Stories and nature

A trader's path

🗺️The name

Bailey's survey

A British officer, Bailey, surveyed the route for map-making. Long before, Tibetan and Indo-Tibetan traders brought yak products down it to the markets of Assam.

🪖1962

The war on the trail

In the 1962 war Chinese troops took the trail from Tulung La to Thembang. Army bunkers still stand between Thungri and Changla.

🏔️The view

Gorichen and Kangto

Gorichen (6,538 m), Kangto (7,090 m), Nyegyi Kangsang and Tapka Shiri are "a constant" on this trek. It passes Monpa villages, oak and juniper forest and rhododendrons.

How to reach the Bailey Trail trek

Permits first

Indian visitors need an Arunachal Inner Line Permit (eilp.arunachal.gov.in); foreign visitors need a Protected Area Permit.

⛰️Experience needed

Bikat asks for 7–10 days of Himalayan trekking and a previous trek above 3,700 m.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs on the trek. Carry cash for your own expenses. Trekup India

📶Last signal

The network is patchy from Bomdila on, and gone from Lagam. Call home from Bomdila. Bikat Adventures

🔌No electricity

There is no electricity or phone network on the trail. Bring a power bank or solar charger. UnExploredNorthEast

🛒Last shops

Bomdila has a decent choice of shops for last-minute buys. Don't leave anything important to the last minute. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

Days 13–18 °C, nights 0–7 °C, colder as you climb. Expect snow in the April–June season, little in September–October. Bikat Adventures

⛺Camps and toilets

Tents for two or three, with a pit toilet tent at each camp. There is no bathing on the trek. Bikat Adventures

🍲Food

Vegetarian meals, plus eggs. Lunch is packed on trekking days, so bring your own tiffin box and mug. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

A porter for your rucksack costs about ₹600–700 a bag, booked in advance, with at least 4 bags in the group. Banzaras

🚙Getting to Bomdila

Banzaras starts at Bomdila at 1 p.m.; the drive from Guwahati or Tezpur is extra. Weather buffer days cost about ₹3,000 a day. Banzaras

🩺Papers

Bikat needs your ID and a medical certificate: send soft copies ahead and carry the originals. Bikat Adventures

🆘Evacuation

Bikat maps an evacuation plan for each region and carries oxygen in its first-aid kit. Bikat Adventures
