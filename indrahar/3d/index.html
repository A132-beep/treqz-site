Stories and nature

Indra, shepherds and the missing

⚡The name

Indra's pass

The pass is named after Indra, god of rain and king of heaven. Locals see it as a gateway he watches over.

🐑The shepherds

A Gaddi highway

Gaddi shepherds still use the pass to take their flocks to summer pastures in the upper Ravi valley and Lahaul.

🔍The missing

Lost near Moon Peak

In August 2013 two young French trekkers left their group near Triund to try Moon Peak. In 2016, remains believed to be theirs were found at the pass.

Best time for the Indrahar Pass trek

Before and after the rains

Bikat recommends May, June, September and October.

🌸April–June

Snow lingers on the upper trail in early summer.

🌧️July–August

Rain, slippery trail, leeches and poor views.

🍂Sep–Nov

Finish before mid-November, when snow can fall.

❄️Winter

Closed above 3,000 m under Kangra's winter orders.

How to reach the Indrahar Pass trek

Getting to McLeodganj

Dharamshala is the gateway. Bikat's groups meet in McLeodganj.

  1. Delhi🚌 475–500 km by overnight bus, about 13 h
  2. Dharamshala🛺 5–6 km by auto or taxi
  3. McLeodganj

Nearest airport: Gaggal, about 13 km from Dharamshala. Nearest major railhead: Pathankot, 85–90 km.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹8,800 + 5% GST, McLeodganj to McLeodganj, plus ₹165 insurance.

🎫Permits

Register at the Gallu forest check post and pay the entry fee, last reported at ₹100 a day.

📶Signal

Airtel and BSNL work up to Triund. There is no coverage beyond.

🧭Route-finding

Above Lahesh Cave the trail is confusing. Don't go without someone who knows it.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Signal stops at Triund

Airtel and BSNL work up to Triund. There is no coverage beyond it. Bikat Adventures

🎫Entry fee

Register at the Gallu forest check post with photo ID and pay the entry fee: ₹100 a day at the last reported rate. Treks and Trails

❄️Winter closure

Kangra's winter orders close the trail above 3,000 m, so Snowline Café, Laka Got and the pass are off limits in winter. Treks and Trails

🍵Food on the trail

Tea stalls line the way to Triund. Magic View Café, at about 2,400 m, is the usual tea stop. Treks and Trails

💧Carry water

Carry at least 2 litres. Snowline Café is a refreshment stop on the way. Himalaya Shelter

🌡️Temperatures

April–June: days 12–20 °C, nights 2–8 °C. September–November: days 10–18 °C, nights 0–5 °C. Colder and windy near the pass. Himalaya Shelter

⛺Camps

Bikat uses twin-sharing tents with a toilet tent and a dining tent at each camp. Bikat Adventures

🧭Take a guide

Beyond Lahesh Caves the route is confusing. Go with an experienced guide. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Himalayan Hikers carries your bag for ₹400. Himalayan Hikers

✈️Getting there

Gaggal airport is about 13 km from Dharamshala. Pathankot, the nearest major railway station, is 85–90 km away. Bikat Adventures
