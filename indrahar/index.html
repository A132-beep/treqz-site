Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Indrahar starts as the easy Triund walk, then climbs into rock and snow for two more days.

The route

6 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · McLeodganj · 1,785 mkm 0.1

Under the white wall

9kmToday · To Triund

The walk starts in McLeodganj, above Dharamshala, and climbs the Dhauladhar to the border of Kangra and Chamba.

01Day 1 · Triund · 2,875 mkm 9.0

Camp on the ridge

9kmToday · Past Galu Devi

The first 2 km are steep. The trail eases after Galu Devi temple, then the last kilometre climbs hard to Triund, a flat meadow on a ridge.

02Day 2 · Laka Got · about 3,100 mkm 16.0

At the glacier's snout

7kmToday · 3–4 h

Up past Snowline Café and across to the grazing ground of Laka Got, right under the Dhauladhar wall.

03Day 3 · Lahesh Cave · about 3,300 mkm 17.5

The hermits' cave

RockTerrain · No more plants

Above Laka Got the trail turns to boulders, up to Lahesh Cave, where hermits are said to have meditated.

03Day 3 · Indrahar Pass · 4,342 mkm 22.0

Over the Dhauladhar

2–6hUp from Lahesh · Steep and rocky

Indrahar Pass sits on the crest of the Dhauladhar, on the border of Kangra and Chamba districts.

04Day 4 · McLeodganj · 1,785 mkm 44.0

Down in one day

16kmToday · Lunch at Triund

A long, rocky descent past Triund and Galu Devi to McLeodganj, back by late afternoon.

Elevation: 44.0 km, 2,590 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📶Signal stops at Triund

Airtel and BSNL work up to Triund. There is no coverage beyond it.

Bikat Adventures
🎫Entry fee

Register at the Gallu forest check post with photo ID and pay the entry fee: ₹100 a day at the last reported rate.

Treks and Trails
❄️Winter closure

Kangra's winter orders close the trail above 3,000 m, so Snowline Café, Laka Got and the pass are off limits in winter.

Treks and Trails
🍵Food on the trail

Tea stalls line the way to Triund. Magic View Café, at about 2,400 m, is the usual tea stop.

Treks and Trails
💧Carry water

Carry at least 2 litres. Snowline Café is a refreshment stop on the way.

Himalaya Shelter
🌡️Temperatures

April–June: days 12–20 °C, nights 2–8 °C. September–November: days 10–18 °C, nights 0–5 °C. Colder and windy near the pass.

Questions

How long is the Indrahar Pass trek?

About 44.0 km of walking over 4 days on the trail.

How high does the Indrahar Pass trek go?

The highest point is 4,342 m, about 2,590 m above the start.

How difficult is the Indrahar Pass trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate.

When is the best time for the Indrahar Pass trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct. It is also possible in Apr, Nov.

How much does the Indrahar Pass trek cost?

₹6,499 to ₹8,800 per person: base prices from 2 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Indrahar Pass trek?

Airtel and BSNL work up to Triund. There is no coverage beyond it.

Do I need a permit or pay an entry fee for the Indrahar Pass trek?

Register at the Gallu forest check post with photo ID and pay the entry fee: ₹100 a day at the last reported rate.

What should I pack for the Indrahar Pass trek?

Two quick-dry track pants, Warm base layers, Thermals, top and bottom, Fleece or down jacket, Waterproof jacket and pants, Warm gloves, hat and sun hat, Wool socks, High-ankle trekking boots. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Indrahar Pass trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Indrahar Pass trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate and it takes 4 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,342 m.

Which treks are near the Indrahar Pass trek?

Triund, Kareri Lake, Beas Kund, Bhrigu Lake, Prashar Lake.
