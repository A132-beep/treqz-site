Best running apps in India, compared

The 30-second answer
  • Six widely used apps, compared on what their own store listings and pages say: tracking, social features, offline use, wearables and whether they are free.
  • All six are free to download on the Indian App Store; some have paid tiers, and Garmin Connect needs a Garmin device.
  • We do not rank them. Pick by what you need: a social feed, guided coaching, a watch you already own, or trail maps.

There is no single best running app, because the apps do different jobs: some are social feeds, some are coaches, some belong to a watch brand, and some are really map tools. Below is a plain comparison of six apps Indian runners commonly use, based on what each company says on its own page or Indian App Store listing.

How we compared them

We read each app's own description, not reviews. Where a page did not say something (for example, whether an app works offline), we have written "not stated" instead of guessing. Features and paid tiers change often, so check the app before you commit.

Availability: we opened the listings on the Indian App Store for all six. We could not load the Google Play pages, so Android users should confirm on Play.

What stands out for Indian runners

  • Guided coaching may differ in India. Nike Run Club's listing says its training plans are available only in a list of countries that does not include India, and that guided runs are "available in select countries" without naming India. Tracking and challenges are separate from that.
  • Paid tiers exist. Strava, Runkeeper (GO) and komoot all have paid options. Their Indian listings show rupee prices, but the listings do not always label the billing period, so read the checkout screen carefully.
  • Your watch may decide for you. If you own a Garmin, Garmin Connect is already part of the package, and it can pass runs to Strava.
  • Trail running needs maps. komoot's offline maps are the only offline map feature in this list that its listing describes in detail. For treks, see our guide to offline maps for treks in India.

Questions to ask before you install

  • Do I need an app at all? A phone or watch that logs distance and time is enough for a first 5K. Add an app when you want a feed, a plan or a place to keep history.
  • Will it drain my battery? Several listings (Strava, Nike Run Club, Garmin Connect, Runkeeper) warn that continuous GPS use in the background can reduce battery life, so charge before a long run.
  • Who sees my runs? Social apps can show your location and routes. Check the privacy settings before your first run; our Strava alternatives guide explains Strava's visibility options.
  • Will my data move if I switch? Some apps pass activities to others (Garmin Connect lists Strava sync), which makes it easier to change later.

Picking by need

  • You want friends and a feed: Strava, or Nike Run Club's challenges.
  • You want someone to tell you what to run: adidas Running or Runkeeper both mention training plans; check which plans are available to you in India.
  • You already own a watch: start with the watch brand's own app, then add a social app if you want one.
  • You run trails: komoot.

If you are just starting, a basic tracker is enough. Our 5K plan for beginners in India works with any of them, or with no app at all.

Quick questions

Is Strava free in India?

The Indian App Store lists Strava as free to download, with a free version and a paid subscription version that adds premium features. The listing does not say exactly which features are premium.

Which running app works with a Garmin watch?

Garmin Connect is Garmin's own app and needs a compatible Garmin device. Strava, ASICS Runkeeper and komoot also say they connect with Garmin.

Is there a running app that works offline?

Strava's listing says routes can be saved for offline use, and komoot's says you can download maps for offline navigation. Other apps' pages we read do not say.
