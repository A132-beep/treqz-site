Uttarakhand vs Himachal: where to do your first Himalayan trek

For most people in North India, the first big trek is in one of two states: Uttarakhand or Himachal Pradesh. Both have beautiful beginner treks. The real difference is when you can go, and that's where the choice usually starts.

This comparison uses the 33 treks in these two states in the Treqz atlas (21 in Uttarakhand, 12 in Himachal), with seasons and grades from each trek's cited sources.

Winter snow: Uttarakhand

If you want your first trek to be a snow trek in December to February, the choice is easy. In the atlas, 8 Uttarakhand treks are at their best in winter, and no Himachal trek is. The classic first winter summits:

  • Kedarkantha: 3,810 m, 5 days, easy–moderate, best Dec–Feb
  • Brahmatal: 3,734 m, 5 days, easy–moderate, best Dec–Feb
  • Dayara Bugyal: 3,750 m, 5 days, easy, best Dec–Mar
  • Nag Tibba: 3,022 m, 3 days, best Dec–Mar (a good weekend-plus option)

Torn between the first two? See Kedarkantha vs Brahmatal side by side, or all December treks.

Short summer trips: Himachal

Himachal's strength is short, easy treks in spring, summer and autumn, many of them reachable from Dharamshala, Mandi or Kullu. Six of its 12 atlas treks are graded easy or easy–moderate:

  • Triund: 2,875 m, 2 days, easy
  • Prashar Lake: 2,730 m, 2 days, easy, best Apr–Jun and Sep–Nov
  • Kareri Lake: 2,934 m, 3 days, easy, best May–Jun and Sep–Nov
  • Kheerganga: 2,960 m, 1 day, easy–moderate, best Apr–Jun and Sep–Oct

These stay below 3,000 m, a gentle introduction to the mountains without the effects of high altitude.

Your first high pass

Ready to go above 4,000 m? Both states have strong options in the summer and autumn windows:

  • Himachal: Hampta Pass (4,200 m, 5 days, moderate, best Jun–Oct) crosses from green Kullu into stark Lahaul, a dramatic change in a single day. Bhrigu Lake (4,300 m, 4 days, graded easy) is a shorter high-altitude option.
  • Uttarakhand: Kuari Pass (3,814 m, 5 days, moderate) is the winter choice with views of Nanda Devi; for summer and autumn, Ali–Bedni Bugyal (3,803 m, 6 days, easy) is a meadow trek with a gentle rhythm.

Before any trek above 3,000 m, read how to read altitude gain.

So which should you pick?

  • Going in December–February and want snow? Uttarakhand.
  • Only have a weekend? Himachal's 1–2 day treks.
  • Going in May–June or September–October? Both. Pick by the view you want, and compare the routes in 3D.

Quick questions

Which is better for a first Himalayan trek?

It depends on when you go: Uttarakhand for winter snow (Kedarkantha, Brahmatal, Dayara Bugyal), Himachal for short summer and autumn treks (Triund, Prashar Lake, Kareri Lake).

What is the easiest trek in Himachal?

In the atlas, Triund, Prashar Lake, Beas Kund, Kareri Lake and Bhrigu Lake are graded easy in their sources. Triund and Prashar Lake take 2 days.

Can I trek in Himachal in winter?

None of the Himachal treks in the atlas lists December to March among its best months. For winter snow treks, look at Uttarakhand.
