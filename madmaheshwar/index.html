Stories and nature

Shiva's navel

🐂The legend

The middle part

After the Kurukshetra war Shiva hid from the Pandavas as a bull. It sank into the ground and came up in five parts. The navel surfaced here, so this is the middle (madhya) Kedar.

🙏The priests

From Karnataka

The priests are Jangamas of the Veerashaiva community from Mysore. In winter the deity moves down to the Omkareshwar temple in Ukhimath.

🪞The old shrine

Budha Madmaheshwar

A tiny blackened shrine on the ridge above the temple faces Chaukhamba. At sunrise the peaks are reflected in the pond beside it.

🦌The sanctuary

Musk deer and monal

The whole trail is inside the Kedarnath Wildlife Sanctuary, home to the Himalayan monal and the musk deer, locally called kasturi deer.

Best time for the Madmaheshwar trek

Spring and autumn

The temple is open from about late May to November. Avoid the monsoon.

🌸May–June

The temple opened on 21 May in 2026. Days 8–20 °C, nights 5–10 °C.

🌧️July–August

Monsoon: a slippery climb after Bantoli, leeches lower down, and landslides on the road.

🍂Sep–Oct

The clearest views. Days 10–17 °C, nights 1–10 °C.

❄️Nov–Apr

The temple closes in November. Heavy snow blocks the trail in winter.

How to reach the Madmaheshwar trek

Getting to Ransi

Rishikesh and Haridwar are the railheads. Jolly Grant, Dehradun, is the nearest airport. Ukhimath is 202 km from Haridwar.

  1. Rishikesh🚌 Bus to Ukhimath, 12–13 h, about ₹300
  2. Ukhimath🚙 Shared cab, 20 km
  3. Ransi

The road goes on 1.5 km past Ransi to Aktholi Dhar, the last motorable point.

💰Cost

Himalayan Dream Treks charges ₹10,999 + 5% GST, Rishikesh or Haridwar and back, with transport and permits.

🎫Registration

StayVista says you register at the Ransi forest checkpost, ₹150–200, with a photo ID. Carry one either way.

🏠Stays

Homestays and lodges at Ransi, Bantoli and the temple. Bikat quotes about ₹500–600 a night with meals.

🦵Knees

The trail is a long descent one way and a steep climb the other. Good shoes and a trekking pole help.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last ATMs are in Ukhimath. There are none at Ransi or on the trail. StayVista

📶Phone signal

Major networks work up to Ransi. There is no coverage on the trail. Bikat Adventures

🔌Charging

There is no electricity near the temple. Carry a power bank. Treks and Trails

🍲Food and water

Homestays serve meals and let you refill bottles. Small stalls on the way sell snacks and drinks. Bikat Adventures

🏠Stays

Homestays and lodges at Ransi, Bantoli and the temple, about ₹500–600 a night with meals. Camping is allowed if you bring your own gear. Bikat Adventures

🎫Registration

Register at the Ransi forest checkpost on the day, ₹150–200 a head, with a photo ID. No advance permit is needed. StayVista

🌡️Temperatures

In September and October, about 10–17 °C by day and 1–10 °C at night. Himalayan Dream Treks

🐴Mules and porters

Mules and porters can be hired at Ransi. The fee is negotiable. StayVista

🚌By bus

Buses and shared taxis leave Haridwar and Rishikesh from 4 am. Change at Rudraprayag and Ukhimath for Ransi. Tripoto

🛕Temple dates

The temple opened on 21 May in 2026. It closes around Kartik Purnima, in early November. Himalayan Dream Treks
