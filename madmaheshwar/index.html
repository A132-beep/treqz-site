Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Madmaheshwar is the second of the five Panch Kedar temples, and the least visited.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Ransi · 1,890 mkm 0.1

The end of the road

20kmFrom Ukhimath · By shared cab

The drive from Rishikesh runs via Rudraprayag and Ukhimath to Ransi, the village where the walk starts.

01Day 1 · Gaundar · 1,800 mkm 5.0

Down to the river

5kmToday · Mostly downhill

The first day is mostly a descent to Gaundar, by the Madmaheshwar Ganga.

02Day 2 · Bantolikm 6.7

Where two rivers meet

~2kmFrom Gaundar · Flat to Bantoli

Bantoli sits at the meeting of two streams that form the Madmaheshwar Ganga.

02Day 2 · Madmaheshwar · 3,497 mkm 18.0

The middle Kedar

13kmToday · 6–7 h from Gaundar

The trail tops out in a meadow below a ridge, where the Madmaheshwar temple stands.

03Day 3 · Budha Madmaheshwar · 3,800 mkm 18.8

Chaukhamba at sunrise

2kmClimb · 1–2 h, steep

Start in the dark to reach Budha Madmaheshwar, a meadow with a tiny old shrine, by sunrise.

03Day 3 · Gaundar · 1,800 mkm 33.0

The long way down

15kmToday · 7–8 h

After the sunrise, back to the temple and all the way down to Gaundar.

04Day 4 · Ransi · 1,890 mkm 38.0

Up and out

5kmToday · About 2 h

A short climb takes you back to Ransi and the road.

Elevation: 38.0 km, 1,540 m up and back down

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

The last ATMs are in Ukhimath. There are none at Ransi or on the trail.

StayVista
📶Phone signal

Major networks work up to Ransi. There is no coverage on the trail.

Bikat Adventures
🔌Charging

There is no electricity near the temple. Carry a power bank.

Treks and Trails
🍲Food and water

Homestays serve meals and let you refill bottles. Small stalls on the way sell snacks and drinks.

Bikat Adventures
🏠Stays

Homestays and lodges at Ransi, Bantoli and the temple, about ₹500–600 a night with meals. Camping is allowed if you bring your own gear.

Bikat Adventures
🎫Registration

Register at the Ransi forest checkpost on the day, ₹150–200 a head, with a photo ID. No advance permit is needed.

Questions

How long is the Madmaheshwar trek?

About 38.0 km of walking over 4 days on the trail.

How high does the Madmaheshwar trek go?

The highest point is 3,497 m, about 1,540 m above the start.

How difficult is the Madmaheshwar trek?

Its sources grade it Easy–moderate.

When is the best time for the Madmaheshwar trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct. It is also possible in Mar–Apr, Nov.

How much does the Madmaheshwar trek cost?

₹5,999 to ₹16,000 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Madmaheshwar trek?

Major networks work up to Ransi. There is no coverage on the trail.
