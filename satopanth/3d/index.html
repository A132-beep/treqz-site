Stories and nature

The lake of the trinity

🔺The lake

Three corners, three gods

Satopanth means the path of truth. Brahma, Vishnu and Shiva are believed to meditate at its three corners, and Mana families immerse the ashes of their dead in it.

🏹The epic

The Pandavas' last walk

The Pandavas are said to have taken this valley on their way to heaven. Bhim Pul at Mana and Vasudhara are both tied to the story; guides disagree on where each brother fell.

🧊The glacier

A river is born

The Alaknanda begins where the Satopanth and Bhagirath Kharak glaciers meet. The Satopanth glacier pulled back about 2.3 km between 1962 and 2013.

🏔️The mountain

The Queen of the Garhwal

Nilkantha, 6,596 m, stands 9 km east of Badrinath. Legend says Shiva blocked a path between Kedarnath and Badrinath with it. Its first accepted ascent was in 1974.

Best time for the Satopanth Tal trek

Late spring and early autumn

Bikat runs the trek in May–June and September–October. Wikipedia says the lake is frozen from late September to mid-May or early June.

🌸May–June

10–20 °C by day lower down, 5–15 °C near the lake. Nights 0–10 °C or colder.

🌧️July–August

Himalaya Shelter advises against the monsoon. Landslides often block the highway near Joshimath.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear skies. In October it can drop to 0–10 °C or below up high.

❄️Nov–April

Snow and freezing nights. Mana's people move down the valley for the winter.

How to reach the Satopanth Tal trek

Getting to Mana

Joshimath is the last big town. Badrinath and Mana are at the end of the road.

  1. Dehradun🚌 About 300 km, 10–13 h
  2. Joshimath🚌 46 km
  3. Badrinath🚕 3 km
  4. Mana

Nearest airport: Jolly Grant, Dehradun. Nearest railheads: Rishikesh, Haridwar and Dehradun. Rishikesh to Joshimath is about 247 km.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹22,550 + 5% GST, Joshimath to Joshimath, with stays in Joshimath and Badrinath.

🎫Permits

Operators arrange the forest permit. Himalayan Dream Treks says it comes from the Nanda Devi Biosphere Reserve forest office in Joshimath. Some guides say an inner line permit is also needed; we could not confirm this from an official page.

⛺Camping

Lake-side camping was stopped in September 2018 under a High Court order, so groups camp at Chakratirtha. The Supreme Court lifted the meadow camping ban on 20 July 2026; ask whether lake camping is allowed again.

🆘Rescue

In October 2025 a trekker died here and three were rescued. The SDRF team took until the next morning to reach them. Guides advise going with an authorised operator.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last reliable ATMs are in Joshimath: SBI, PNB, HDFC and ICICI. There are none at Mana or on the trail. Carry small notes. Trekkaro

📶Phone signal

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Badrinath and Mana. Signal is patchy up to Laxmi Van and absent at Chakratirtha and the lake. Trekkaro

🔋No charging

There is no charging beyond Mana. Bring a solar panel or a large power bank. MountRoutes

🍲No food on the trail

There are no food or shelter stops on the trail. Everything goes with your group. Himalaya Trekker

🩺Medical certificate

The medical certificate must come from a medical facility in Chamoli district. You can get it at Joshimath or Badrinath. Himalayan Hikers

🪪Papers

Carry an original photo ID with a photocopy, a doctor-signed medical certificate and a signed disclaimer form. Trekkaro

🎒Offloading

Trekkaro carries your bag for ₹2,500 + 5% GST for the whole trek, up to 9 kg. Trekkaro

🐴Mules

The Searching Souls says a mule for your backpack costs up to ₹700 a bag a day. The Searching Souls

🚐Shared transport

Bikat's optional cars leave ISBT Dehradun at 7 a.m.: a Sumo for 5–6 costs ₹8,000 and a Tempo for 11–12 costs ₹12,000, each way. Bikat Adventures

🚫Not beyond the lake

Going on past the lake towards the Swargarohini glacier needs a mountaineering permit. MountRoutes
