Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Satopanth is a high glacier walk from the last road at Badrinath to a lake held sacred by the people of Mana.

The route

6 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

03Day 3 · Mana · 3,200 mkm 0.1

The first village

3kmFrom Badrinath · Up the road

Mana, once called India's last village, is now called the first. Its people move down the valley for the winter.

03Day 3 · Laxmi Van · 3,658 mkm 7.2

Under the birches

7.3kmToday · Up the Alaknanda

The path climbs the Alaknanda valley with the Vasudhara falls dropping 400 ft from the cliffs across the river.

04Day 4 · Chakratirtha · 4,162 mkm 14.3

Over the moraine

7kmToday · The hardest day

Boulders and scree lead past Sahastra Dhara, then a long walk on the lateral moraine of the Satopanth glacier.

05Day 5 · Satopanth Tal · 4,346 mkm 16.7

The path of truth

4.9kmUp and back · From Chakratirtha

Short, tricky boulder fields lead to Satopanth Tal, a three-cornered lake fed by the Satopanth and Bhagirath Kharak glaciers.

Down the valley

Back down the moraine to camp at Chamtoli.

Back to Badrinath

The last walk out to Mana, then the drive down to Joshimath.

💵Bring cash

The last reliable ATMs are in Joshimath: SBI, PNB, HDFC and ICICI. There are none at Mana or on the trail. Carry small notes.

📶Phone signal

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Badrinath and Mana. Signal is patchy up to Laxmi Van and absent at Chakratirtha and the lake.

Trekkaro
🔋No charging

There is no charging beyond Mana. Bring a solar panel or a large power bank.

MountRoutes
🍲No food on the trail

There are no food or shelter stops on the trail. Everything goes with your group.

Himalaya Trekker
🩺Medical certificate

The medical certificate must come from a medical facility in Chamoli district. You can get it at Joshimath or Badrinath.

Himalayan Hikers
🪪Papers

Questions

How long is the Satopanth Tal trek?

About 37.4 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Satopanth Tal trek go?

The highest point is 4,346 m, about 1,210 m above the start.

How difficult is the Satopanth Tal trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Satopanth Tal trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Oct. It is also possible in Jul–Aug.

How much does the Satopanth Tal trek cost?

₹15,000 to ₹55,000 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Satopanth Tal trek?

Jio, Airtel and BSNL work in Badrinath and Mana. Signal is patchy up to Laxmi Van and absent at Chakratirtha and the lake.

Where do I get cash before the Satopanth Tal trek?

The last reliable ATMs are in Joshimath: SBI, PNB, HDFC and ICICI. There are none at Mana or on the trail. Carry small notes.

What should I pack for the Satopanth Tal trek?

Padded jacket (rated to −10 °C), Thermal inners, Fleece or woollen layers, Rain jacket, Trek pants, Quick-dry tops, Sun cap, Woollen cap and balaclava or buff. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Satopanth Tal trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Satopanth Tal trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,346 m.

Which treks are near the Satopanth Tal trek?

Valley of Flowers, Madmaheshwar, Kuari Pass, Pangarchulla, Chopta Chandrashila.
