Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Pin Parvati is one of the hardest treks here: ten walking days, a glacier, and two very different valleys.

The route

8 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

01Day 1 · Barsheni · 2,580 mkm 0.1

Up the Parvati

50kmFrom Bhuntar · About 2.5 h by road

The road up the Parvati valley ends at Barsheni. A short walk reaches the first night's stay at Kalga.

02Day 2 · Kheerganga · 2,960 mkm 8.0

Shiva's hot spring

8kmToday · About 6 h

Past Nakthan and the Rudranag temple to Kheerganga, a meadow with a hot spring.

03Day 3 · Tunda Bhuj · 3,200 mkm 19.3

Into the wild

11.4kmToday · 4–5 h

Beyond Kheerganga the villages end. The trail follows the Parvati upstream to Tunda Bhuj.

05Day 5 · Odi Thach · 3,815 mkm 34.2

Over the Pandavas' bridges

9.7kmToday · 4–5 h

Between Thakur Kuan and Odi Thach the trail crosses the Parvati twice on huge boulders called Pandu Pul.

06Day 6 · Mantalai · 4,096 mkm 44.6

Where the Parvati begins

10.3kmToday · 5–6 h

Past a Shivling at 'Mini Mantalai' to Mantalai lake, where the Parvati river begins.

07Day 7 · Base Camp I · 4,800 mkm 49.0

The edge of the glacier

4.5kmToday · 5–6 h

A steep day up scree and moraine to Base Camp I at the edge of the Parvati glacier.

08Day 8 · Pin Parvati Pass · 5,319 mkm 53.9

Two worlds, one pass

6.4kmToday · 7–8 h

Across the glacier to Pin Parvati Pass. From the top you see both valleys at once: the green, Hindu Parvati behind, and the cold-desert, Buddhist Pin valley of Spiti ahead.

10Day 10 · Mudh · 3,970 mkm 82.4

Down into Spiti

15.3kmToday · About 5 h

Day 9 drops to Wichkurung Thatch. Day 10 crosses the Pagal Nala, the 'mad stream' that floods suddenly when ice melts in the day's heat, to Mudh.

Elevation: 82.5 km, 3,110 m up and back down

🚙Road transport is extra

With Bikat you pay the jeeps on the spot. Bhuntar to Barsheni: ₹3,500 a Sumo (5–6 people) or ₹5,000 a Tempo. Kaza to Manali: ₹11,000 a Sumo or ₹17,000 a Tempo.

Bikat Adventures
💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs on the trek. Carry cash for every day you are out.

Trekup India
📶Book Kaza first

You lose signal at the start of the trek. Book your Kaza room before you leave.

Bikat Adventures
💧Water on the glacier

On pass day there may be just one stream on the glacier. Fill your bottles at Base Camp I. There is no water either between Wichkurung and Mudh.

Bikat blog
🍲Food and water at camp

Food and drinking water are only at the campsites. Carry snacks for the long days.

Questions

How long is the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

About 82.5 km of walking over 12 days on the trail.

How high does the Pin Parvati Pass trek go?

The highest point is 5,319 m, about 3,110 m above the start.

How difficult is the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

Its sources grade it Hard.

When is the best time for the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

Jun–Sep. It is also possible in May, Oct.

How much does the Pin Parvati Pass trek cost?

₹44,500 to ₹70,000 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Where do I get cash before the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

There are no ATMs on the trek. Carry cash for every day you are out.

What should I pack for the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

Warm beanie or balaclava, Quick-dry T-shirts ×2, Thermal top and bottoms, Fleece jacket, Down or synthetic puffer jacket, Waterproof shell jacket, Quick-dry trek pants ×2, Waterproof rain pants. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Pin Parvati Pass trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Pin Parvati Pass trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Hard and it takes 12 days on the trail, with a highest point of 5,319 m.

Which treks are near the Pin Parvati Pass trek?

Kheerganga, Sar Pass, Hampta Pass, Prashar Lake, Bhrigu Lake.
