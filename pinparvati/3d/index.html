Stories and nature

From Shiva's valley to Spiti

🗺️The crossing

First crossed in 1884

Sir Louis Dane made the first recorded crossing in August 1884. Spiti's shepherds had used the pass long before.

♨️The spring

Shiva's hot spring

Kheerganga, the first camp, is where Hindu belief says Shiva meditated for 3,000 years.

🪨The bridges

Pandu Pul

Two huge boulders across the Parvati are said to have been placed by the Pandavas.

Best time for the Pin Parvati Pass trek

A monsoon trek

Bikat calls it one of the few Himalayan treks you can do in the monsoon: Spiti is in the rain shadow.

🌧️June–September

Bikat's season.

🍂May–October

Bajaj Finserv gives this wider window.

🧊Pass day

Glacier crossing with crevasses, whatever the month.

❄️Winter

Snow closes the pass.

How to reach the Pin Parvati Pass trek

Getting to Barsheni

Fly to Bhuntar, near Kullu. The trek ends on the far side, in Spiti.

  1. Bhuntar🚙 50 km, about 2.5 h
  2. Barsheni

Nearest railway: Chandigarh, about 300 km. Exit: Mudh to Kaza 2 h, then Kaza to Manali about 200 km and 10 h.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹56,000 + 5% GST, Barsheni to Kaza. Road transport is paid on the spot.

🪪Permits

Forest, trek and IMF permits are needed; Bikat includes them. The Pin side is in Pin Valley National Park.

⛰️Grade

Very difficult (BRS 6). Bikat asks for several BRS 5 treks first.

🤕Altitude sickness

The pass is above 5,300 m. Tell your leader about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚙Road transport is extra

With Bikat you pay the jeeps on the spot. Bhuntar to Barsheni: ₹3,500 a Sumo (5–6 people) or ₹5,000 a Tempo. Kaza to Manali: ₹11,000 a Sumo or ₹17,000 a Tempo. Bikat Adventures

💵Bring cash

There are no ATMs on the trek. Carry cash for every day you are out. Trekup India

📶Book Kaza first

You lose signal at the start of the trek. Book your Kaza room before you leave. Bikat Adventures

💧Water on the glacier

On pass day there may be just one stream on the glacier. Fill your bottles at Base Camp I. There is no water either between Wichkurung and Mudh. Bikat blog

🍲Food and water at camp

Food and drinking water are only at the campsites. Carry snacks for the long days. Himalaya Shelter

🌡️Weather

July–August: days 10–18 °C, nights 1–7 °C. September: days 10–15 °C, nights 0–5 °C. Himalaya Shelter

⏳Buffer day

Bikat keeps Day 12 spare for bad weather. If it is used, you pay ₹5,000 more, collected in Kaza. Bikat Adventures

🐴Offloading

Bikat carries your bag for ₹7,854 for the whole crossing. Book it with your seat. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

On the Parvati side: Manikaran, 01902 222535. Manikaran police: 01902 273766. HP trekking portal

🚁Rescue

In July 2024 police, ITBP and the Kaza administration brought down a stranded group near Tenma Top, 18 km from Mudh. A sick trekker went to Civil Hospital, Kaza. The Tribune, July 2024
