Stories and nature

A chain of lakes

👯The twins

Vishansar and Kishansar

Kishansar is the twin of Vishansar, the first of the great lakes, and lies just above it.

💧The chain

Satsar

Seven lakes linked one to the next, at about 3,658 m.

🏔️The mountain

Gangabal and Harmukh

The last lake camp sits under Harmukh, with Nundkol lake close by.

Best time for the Kashmir Great Lakes trek

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

⚠️Check it's open

Kashmir's trek routes were shut after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack. As of July 2026 no official reopening notice was found. Get written permit confirmation before you pay. Travel Coffee, July 2026

💵Bring cash

The nearest ATM is in Srinagar. There is no ATM or card payment on the trail. Bikat Adventures

📶Phone signal

Signal works in Sonamarg and again at Naranag. Expect none after Shitkadi. Himalayan Hikers

🔌No charging

No electricity on the trail. The last power is in Sonamarg. Bring a charged power bank. Himalayan Hikers

⛺Tents and toilets

You sleep two to a tent. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Carry your own toilet paper. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

Days 10–20 °C. Nights 0–10 °C, and below freezing at the high camps. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Primary Health Centre, Sonamarg: 0194-2417231. Serious cases go to SKIMS in Srinagar. TrekUp India

🚑Rescue

Leaders bring you down to the road, and a car takes you to hospital. You pay for the rescue unless you are insured. Bikat Adventures

🎒Offloading

Bikat charges ₹2,570 to carry your backpack for the trek. Bikat Adventures

🚕Srinagar to Sonamarg

Bikat's cab, Srinagar and back, is ₹1,600. It leaves Dal Gate No. 1 around noon and reaches Sonamarg by 3 p.m. Bikat Adventures
