Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

A long traverse, not a summit: every camp from the third day on sits beside a lake.

The route

7 stops on the map, then the climb. Scroll the stops and the map follows.

02Day 2 · Sonamarg · 2,730 mkm 0.1

The meadow of gold

81kmFrom Srinagar · Driven on day 1

Day one is the 81 km drive from Srinagar to Sonamarg at about 2,730 m. The walk starts here, or a little on at Shitkadi.

02Day 2 · Nichnai · 3,500 mkm 10.0

The first camp

10kmToday · From Sonamarg

Camp at Nichnai, about 3,500 m, below the first pass of the trek.

03Day 3 · Vishansar · 3,658 mkm 21.0

Over Nichnai Pass to the first lake

11kmToday · Over Nichnai Pass, 4,150 m

Over Nichnai Pass at about 4,150 m and down to Vishansar, the first of the great lakes, at 3,658 m.

05Day 5 · Gadsarkm 31.0

The high pass

10kmToday · Over Gadsar Pass

Past Kishansar and over Gadsar Pass at about 4,206 m, the highest point of the trek, then down to Gadsar lake.

06Day 6 · Satsar · 3,658 mkm 43.0

Seven lakes

12kmToday · From Gadsar

Satsar is a chain of seven connected lakes, at about 3,658 m.

07Day 7 · Gangabal · 3,505 mkm 56.0

Under Harmukh

13kmToday · The longest day

A long day to Gangabal at about 3,505 m, below the snows of Harmukh. Nundkol lake lies close by.

08Day 8 · Naranag · 2,271 mkm 67.0

Down to the road

11kmToday · All downhill

The long descent to Naranag at about 2,271 m, and the drive back to Srinagar.

Elevation: 67.0 km, 1,530 m up and back down

⚠️Check it's open

Kashmir's trek routes were shut after the April 2025 Pahalgam attack. As of July 2026 no official reopening notice was found. Get written permit confirmation before you pay.

Travel Coffee, July 2026
💵Bring cash

The nearest ATM is in Srinagar. There is no ATM or card payment on the trail.

Bikat Adventures
📶Phone signal

Signal works in Sonamarg and again at Naranag. Expect none after Shitkadi.

Himalayan Hikers
🔌No charging

No electricity on the trail. The last power is in Sonamarg. Bring a charged power bank.

Himalayan Hikers
⛺Tents and toilets

You sleep two to a tent. Toilets are dry pits in a toilet tent. Carry your own toilet paper.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Weather

Days 10–20 °C. Nights 0–10 °C, and below freezing at the high camps.

Questions

How long is the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

About 67.0 km of walking over 8 days on the trail.

How high does the Kashmir Great Lakes trek go?

The highest point is 4,206 m, about 1,530 m above the start.

How difficult is the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

Its sources grade it Hard.

When is the best time for the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

Jul–Sep.

How much does the Kashmir Great Lakes trek cost?

₹14,500 to ₹42,500 per person: base prices from 11 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

Signal works in Sonamarg and again at Naranag. Expect none after Shitkadi.

Where do I get cash before the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

The nearest ATM is in Srinagar. There is no ATM or card payment on the trail.

What should I pack for the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

Thermal top and bottom, Full-sleeve T-shirts, Quick-dry trek pants ×2, Full-sleeve sweater, Warm jacket, Rain jacket and rain pants, Waterproof warm gloves, Woollen cap and sun hat. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Kashmir Great Lakes trek in winter?

Its sources do not list December to February as a trekking season.

Is the Kashmir Great Lakes trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Hard and it takes 8 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,206 m.

Which treks are near the Kashmir Great Lakes trek?

Tarsar Marsar, Kolahoi Glacier, Chadar, Markha Valley, Kareri Lake.
