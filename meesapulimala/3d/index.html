Stories and nature

The whiskered tiger

🐯The name

Meesa · puli · mala

Moustache, tiger, hill: seen from the south-west, the mountain looks like a whiskered big cat.

🕳️The legend

The Pandava cave

Villagers believe the Pandavas stayed in a cave here, between the base camp and Rhodo Valley, during their exile.

🐐The wildlife

Nilgiri tahr

Nilgiri tahr, sambar, gaur, wild dogs, sloth bears and elephants. From the top: Anamudi and the Mattupetty and Anayirangal reservoirs.

Best time for the Meesapulimala trek

Skip the monsoon

Treks and Trails runs it from September to March and advises avoiding June to August.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚌Bus to Munnar

From Bangalore, take an express bus to Munnar, about 463 km. A jeep takes you on from Munnar. Trekup India

📄Collect your permit

Book online or through an operator. Then collect the signed paper permit at the KFDC office in Munnar before you go to base camp. Book 2–4 weeks ahead for weekends. Explurger

📞KFDC office

KFDC Ecotourism is on Mattupetty Road, Munnar. Phone +91 4865 230332. Kerala Tourism

🚙Jeep costs extra

The jeep from Munnar to base camp is often billed apart, about ₹2,000. Check if your package includes it. Explurger

🚙Last road

From base camp, a 4 km jeep track runs to Rhodo Valley. It is the last point a four-wheeler can reach. Kerala Tourism

⛺Three stays

Base camp tents take at most 40 guests a night. Check in by noon; check out 23 hours later. Rhodo Mansion has an elephant trench around it. Kerala Tourism

🍛Meals included

KFDC packages include breakfast, lunch, evening tea, dinner and a campfire. Kerala Tourism

🩸Leeches

Leeches are very active in the monsoon, and fog often hides the top. Carry salt or Dettol and wear leech socks. Explurger

🧭Always with a guide

Elephants, gaur and sloth bears live here. Every group walks with an assigned forest guide. Explurger
