Stories and nature

Little Lhasa and the white wall

🎏The town

Little Lhasa

McLeodganj is known as "little Lhasa" for its Tibetan culture. The walk begins at the edge of town.

🛕The trail

Galu Devi

A small temple marks where the forest walk begins, through oak, deodar and rhododendron.

🏔️Beyond

Indrahar Pass

From Triund the trail continues to Indrahar Pass at 4,342 m, with views of Mani Mahesh Kailash and the Pir Panjal range.

How to reach the Triund trek

Getting to McLeodganj

McLeodganj is 5 km above Dharamshala, by auto or taxi.

  1. Dharamshala🛺 5 km by auto or taxi
  2. McLeodganj

Bikat's groups report in Dharamshala by 8 a.m. on day one. Bikat lists this trek as do-it-yourself, with no package price.

⛰️Hardest part

The steep 22 curves at the end of day one, and 19 km on day two.

🤕Altitude sickness

Headache, sickness or dizziness? Tell your leader, rest, and go down if it gets worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The last ATM is in McLeodganj. Draw cash there; other ATMs may not pay out. Himalayan Hikers

📶Patchy signal

Jio and Vodafone work on parts of the trail. There is no signal at the top. Himalaya Shelter

🎫Entry fee and limit

Register at the Gallu forest check post and pay the entry fee: ₹100 a day at the last reported rate. Only 40 people and 20 tents may stay on the ridge each night. Treks and Trails

❄️Winter closures

Kangra's winter orders close the trail above 3,000 m, including Snowline Café and Laka. Triund itself can close on bad-weather days. Treks and Trails

💧Carry water

Water sources are few. Carry at least 2 litres. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍵Food on the trail

Tea stalls line the forest section. Magic View Café, at about 2,400 m, is the usual tea stop. Treks and Trails

⛺Tents and rest house

Tents at Triund rent for about ₹600 for two, with sleeping bags. The forest rest house costs ₹600–800 a night; book it in Dharamshala. Trekup India

🌡️Night temperatures

About 4 °C in October and just below 0 °C in November. −5 to −7 °C from December to February. Even May–June nights are 7–10 °C. Treks and Trails

🚕Getting to the trailhead

Cars stop at Galu Devi temple, 6 km by road from McLeodganj, about 20 minutes by taxi. Or walk 3.5 km via Dharamkot. Treks and Trails

🎒Offloading

Himalayan Hikers carries your bag for ₹400 extra. Himalayan Hikers
