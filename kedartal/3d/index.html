Stories and nature

Shiva's lake

🧗The climbers

First up Bhrigupanth

Bhrigupanth (6,772 m), above Kedar Kharak, was first climbed in 1980 by an Indian-American women's expedition led by Arlene Blum.

🛕The temple

Amar Singh Thapa's Gangotri

The Gangotri temple was built by the Nepalese general Amar Singh Thapa. It closes on Diwali.

🐆The park

Gangotri National Park

The route lies in Gangotri National Park (1989), home to snow leopard, bears, musk deer, tahr and monal.

Best time for the Kedartal trek

Two short windows

The park is open April to November.

🌸May–June

Snow is possible even in May, and the lake can still be frozen.

🌧️July–August

Heavy rain and landslides: not recommended.

🍂Sep–Oct

Clear and cold.

❄️Winter

The park is closed.

How to reach the Kedartal trek

Getting to Gangotri

Gangotri is 99 km beyond Uttarkashi.

  1. Dehradun🚌 ~245 km via Uttarkashi and Harsil
  2. Gangotri

The nearest ATMs are in Uttarkashi. There's no phone network beyond Gangotri.

💰Cost

Bikat Adventures charges ₹18,000 + 5% GST, Gangotri to Gangotri.

🎫Permits

A Gangotri National Park permit (about ₹150 for Indians in 2026), checked at the Kedartal post near Surya Kund.

⛰️Grade

Moderate (BRS 4), for trekkers with high-altitude experience. Falling rock is a hazard.

🤕Altitude sickness

The lake is at 4,750 m. Tell your leader about any symptoms, and go down if they get worse.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Which SIM

BSNL and Airtel work best in the region. Both have signal in Gangotri. Bikat Adventures

🔌Charge in Gangotri

Gangotri rooms have plugs. After that there is none, so carry a power bank of 10,000 mAh or more. Keep batteries warm in your sleeping bag. Himalayan Dream Treks

🍲Food on the trail

Three vegetarian meals a day, plus evening tea and snacks. Eggs on some days. Bikat Adventures

🚻Toilets

Dry pit toilet tents at camp. On hard treks they may not go up to the highest camp. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Weather

In May–June, days are 15 to 20 °C lower down and 5 to 10 °C up high. High camps drop to −5 °C at night. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

The nearest hospital is the District Hospital in Uttarkashi, near Vishwanath Chowk. Himalayan Dream Treks

🎒Offloading

A porter for your bag costs ₹3,570 with Bikat, ₹3,600 with Trekkaro. Bikat Adventures

🚌Bus or shared taxi

Buses for Uttarkashi and Gangotri leave the Pahadi bus stand near Dehradun railway station. Shared taxis leave from Rispana Pul; change at Uttarkashi. Bikat Adventures

🚕Shared cab cost

A Bikat Sumo from Dehradun for 5–6 people costs ₹9,000 each way. A Tempo for 11–12 costs ₹14,000. Bikat Adventures

🚑Rescue

Rescue is by road from the trailhead. Its cost is not in the trek fee, so get trek insurance. Bikat Adventures
