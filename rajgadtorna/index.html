Stories and nature

Two forts and a ridge

🏯The fort

Torna, the first

Shivaji took Torna in 1646, aged 16. Aurangzeb later renamed it Futulgaib for how hard it was to take.

👑The capital

Rajgad, the seat

Rajgad was the first Maratha capital for almost 26 years. Shivaji's wife Saibai died here in 1659, and his son Rajaram was born here in 1670.

🪙The legend

Gold from Torna

While repairing Torna, Shivaji is said to have found buried gold, and to have used it to build Rajgad.

🌍The listing

World Heritage

In 2025 Rajgad was inscribed by UNESCO as part of the Maratha Military Landscapes of India.

Best time for the Rajgad to Torna trek

After the rains

Bikat's Torna guide calls September to February the best time. Its Rajgad guide says any season works.

🌸Sep–Oct

Flowers on the ridge after the monsoon.

🌤️Nov–Feb

The clearest views. Winter nights near 10 °C.

🌧️Jun–Sep

Green, but slippery rock. In 2025 tourism here was barred from 22 June to 30 September.

☀️Mar–May

Hot, with no tree cover on Torna.

How to reach the Rajgad to Torna trek

Start in Pune

Bikat drives the group from Pune to Gunjavane and back from Velhe. By bus, go to Velhe or Margasani.

  1. Pune🚙 58 km to Gunjavane, about 2 h
  2. Gunjavane🥾 Rajgad, the ridge, Torna
  3. Velhe🚙 Back to Pune

Buses run from Swargate to Velhe in about 1 h 45 min. For Gunjavane, get off at Margasani, 6.6 km away, and take a shared taxi.

💰Cost

Bikat charges ₹3,500 + 5% GST, Pune to Pune, with tents and meals. Treks and Trails runs it as a night trek for ₹1,499.

💧Water

There is no water source on the ridge. Carry 3 litres.

🌙Torna by 5 pm

By the District Collector's order, nobody may stay the night on Torna.

🎫Permits

No permit is needed for either fort.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚌Bus to the base

Buses run from Pune to Velhe, but not often. For Rajgad, get off at Margasani, 6.6 km from Gunjavane, and take a shared taxi. Bikat Adventures: Rajgad guide

🚕Or via Nasrapur

Take any bus towards Satara, Kolhapur or Bangalore to Nasrapur, 36 km from Pune. Shared taxis and autos go on to Velhe. Bikat Adventures: Torna guide

📶Signal on the tops

There is no signal in Gunjavane or on the climb. The Balekilla gets all major networks. On Torna a few bars of Airtel and Jio come and go. Bikat Adventures: Rajgad guide

🍛Food at the base

Small restaurants in Gunjavane serve food, pack lunches and refill bottles. A small shop on the plateau below Rajgad sells refreshments. Velhe hotels also pack food. Bikat Adventures: Rajgad guide

🚱Leave the tanks alone

Don't swim in the fort tanks or put your feet in them. Local people drink from them. Treks and Trails

⛺Where to sleep

Camp near or inside the Padmavati temple. Some Gunjavane hotels rent tents, and a few have basic rooms. Bikat Adventures: Rajgad guide

🚻Toilet tents

Bikat sets up portable toilet tents. There is nowhere to bathe. Bikat Adventures

🎒No offloading

You carry your own bag. Bikat has no option to offload it. Bikat Adventures

🪪Carry ID

Treks and Trails asks for one identity proof with your address. Treks and Trails

🧒Age limits

Bikat takes trekkers from age 9. Under-18s need a parent or guardian. Over-60s need a doctor's certificate. Bikat Adventures
