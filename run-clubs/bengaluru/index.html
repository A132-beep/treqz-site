Running in Bengaluru.

Where people run

Cubbon Park is a good place to start. Great Runs calls it a 300-acre central park with wide avenues and paths. It says the perimeter is about 5 km, and you can string together 5 to 7 km with the inner paths. It adds that the park is closed to traffic before 8 am and on Sundays. Wikipedia gives slightly different closure details, so check on the day.

Great Runs also lists Lalbagh Gardens, Ulsoor Lake (a path around the lake), Sankey Tank (a 1.6 km walkway), J.P. Narayana Park, and the GKVK and Bangalore University campuses. It names Hennur Trail for a longer off-road run, and says lake paths near Wipro and in Kasavanahalli and Haralur are usually about 2 km.

Weather and air

Wikipedia describes Bengaluru as mild for its latitude because of its height above sea level. Dry weather runs from December to February and summer from March to May. The monsoon is June to September. April is the hottest month, with an average high of 34.1 °C. It says most rain falls in the late afternoon or evening, and morning rain is uncommon. We did not find a sourced note on Bengaluru air quality, so we have left it out.

How run clubs work here

Event Flo's Bengaluru page lists clubs with weekly schedules, as of 25 September 2026. Most meet on Saturday or Sunday, with start times mostly between 6:30 and 8:00 am. Runs are about 3K to 10K, and some are run-or-walk. Many clubs are free, some charge for select runs, and some share the meeting point only after you RSVP.
