Running in Pune.

Where people run

Wikipedia says the Pune Municipal Corporation built a 300-metre footpath called the Nature Trail beside Pashan Lake, and calls the setting ideal for morning walks. A Magicpin guide lists Pune Race Course (a track of about 3 km), Hanuman Tekdi (a hill trail), the University of Pune campus, Kamla Nehru Udyan and the Sinhagad road climb as running spots. It says to confirm access rules first, and its own details are not always consistent.

Weather and air

Wikipedia describes three seasons: summer (mid-March to mid-June), monsoon (June to October) and winter (December and January). It says maximums sometimes reach 42 °C in summer, with May the warmest month. Nights stay fairly cool because of the city's height. Most rain falls from June to September, with July the wettest. In winter, overnight lows fall below 12 °C. We did not find a reliable official note on Pune air quality, so we have left it out.

How run clubs work here
