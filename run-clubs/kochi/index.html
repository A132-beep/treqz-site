Kochi Run Club

Where: Panampilly Park, Panampilly Nagar

When: Midweek and weekend mornings

All paces welcome. Marked its second anniversary with a run on 31 Oct.

Source and links: Endorfin · Instagram

Dawnage Run Club

Where: Fort Kochi and Kalamassery Metro Station

When: Weekends

Beach runs, cafe runs and Hyrox sessions.

Source and links: Endorfin · Instagram

EndorphinKrew

Where: Kakkanad; runs move around Kochi, such as Panampilly Nagar and Kaloor

When: Weekends

Mixes runs with track workouts and other sports.

Source and links: Endorfin · Instagram

Orange Runners & Riders Club

Where: Kochi

When: A Saturday Kochi Island Run, plus regular training sessions

Runners and cyclists; organises OrangeRun 2026.

Source and links: Club site

Soles of Cochin

Where: Changes: Kadavanthra, Marine Drive, Willingdon Island, Edappally, Kakkanad and Kalamassery

When: Sundays

Organises the Kochi Spice Coast Marathon, held every October.

Source and links: Club site · Race guide
