Running in Hyderabad.

Where people run

Great Runs calls Necklace Road around Hussain Sagar a favourite running spot, with a lake loop of 9.5 km. Sanjeevaiah Park has a perimeter loop of about 2.6 km, and Indira Park has a loop of about 2 km.

It describes KBR Park in Jubilee Hills as popular with runners, with a 5 km perimeter track called the Peripheral Jogging Track. It says a modest entry fee might apply and does not state park hours, so check at the gate. Great Runs also lists Durgam Cheruvu Walkway (a 4.4 km dirt track) and the Osmania University campus (a mapped 9.4 km loop).

Weather and air

Wikipedia says summers (March to June) are hot and dry, with maximums often above 40 °C between April and June. May is the hottest month. December and January are the coolest. Heavy monsoon rain falls from June to October, and August is the wettest month on average. We did not find a sourced note on Hyderabad air quality, so we have left it out.

How run clubs work here

Event Flo's Hyderabad page, last checked on 24 August 2026, lists eight clubs with fixed weekly schedules, most on Sundays. Two long-run groups assemble at 5:30 am and start at 5:45 am. Joining is free on the platform, and some clubs share the meeting point only after you RSVP. Distances listed run from 5K to 18K. See the full list of run clubs in Hyderabad above.
