Running in Delhi.

Where people run

Great Runs lists many Delhi spots. Nehru Park has a dirt track of about 2.4 km around its edge. Jahanpanah City Forest has non-technical routes of up to 7 km. Sanjay Van has easy dirt trails in a figure-eight of about 4 km. Swarn Jayanti (Japanese) Park has stone-paved paths of about 4.6 km. Bonta Park, also called Kamla Nehru Ridge Park, has nearly 8 km of trails.

Wikipedia calls Lodhi Gardens a popular spot for morning walks and exercise. Great Runs also lists the Rajpath route from National Stadium through India Gate to Rashtrapati Bhavan, and parks in Gurugram and Noida.

Weather and air

Wikipedia says the hot season runs from about mid-to-late March to mid-to-late June, with highs above 39 °C. The cold season runs from about late November to late February, with highs below 20 °C, and heavy fog often occurs. The monsoon arrives at the end of June.

The same page says the air quality index is usually moderate (101 to 200) from January to September and gets much worse from October to December. It lists stubble burning, Diwali firecrackers and cold weather among the causes. Check the day's air quality reading before a hard run.

Races near you

The Vedanta Delhi Half Marathon is a half marathon held in the city. Its official page, which we opened, gave no date for the next edition, so we cannot list one.

How run clubs work here

Time Out Delhi lists clubs such as The Bhag (3 km or 6 km runs followed by coffee, with a social focus) and 5am Social Run Club (early mornings at Connaught Place and Rohini). It says Run with Reeti sessions in Delhi usually start at Nehru Park. Joining is mostly through Instagram, an app or email. Many clubs also run in Gurugram and Noida.
