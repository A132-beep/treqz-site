Running in Chennai.

Where people run

Great Runs calls Marina Beach "the signature run in Chennai". It describes a promenade running south for more than 5 km, with an optional 2.5 km extension north to St. George Fort. The main road beside the beach is busy, so it says the most peaceful time is early. It says the sand is runnable but not optimal.

It also lists Elliott's Beach in Besant Nagar, a 4.5 km out-and-back promenade, the Adyar River paths, and parks in Anna Nagar.

Weather and air

Wikipedia says temperatures vary little across the year. April to June is the hottest period, with averages of 35 to 40 °C. December and January are the coolest, at 19 to 25 °C. Most rain comes from the northeast monsoon between October and December, and low-lying areas often flood. Afternoon humidity averages about 69% over the year. We did not find a sourced note on Chennai air quality, so we have left it out.

Races near you

The official Chennai Marathon page lists its 15th edition on Sunday, 3 January 2027. Categories are 10 km (minimum age 16), half marathon, twenty miler and full marathon (minimum age 18). It says the finish is at India Maritime University, Uthandi, and that start details will be updated. Check the page for registration.
