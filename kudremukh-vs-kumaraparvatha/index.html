Kudremukh goes 180 m higher (1,892 m against 1,712 m). Kudremukh is the easier of the two (Moderate against Moderate–hard). Both are at their best in Nov–Feb. The trailheads are about 66 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Kudremukh if you want an easier trek (Moderate), more height (1,892 m) and to go in Jun–Aug.

Pick Kumara Parvatha if you want to go in Oct.

Questions

Which is easier, Kudremukh or Kumara Parvatha?

Kudremukh, graded Moderate against Moderate–hard.

Which costs less, Kudremukh or Kumara Parvatha?

The middle operator price is ₹4,100 for Kudremukh and ₹3,750 for Kumara Parvatha, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Kudremukh or Kumara Parvatha?

Kudremukh reaches 1,892 m and Kumara Parvatha 1,712 m.

Can I do Kudremukh and Kumara Parvatha in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Nov–Feb.

How far apart are Kudremukh and Kumara Parvatha?

About 66 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
