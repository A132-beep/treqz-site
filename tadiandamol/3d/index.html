Stories and nature

A hill and a palace

🏰The palace

Built in exile

Dodda Vira Rajendra built the Nalknad Palace in 1792–94, after losing Mercara to Tipu Sultan. His second wedding was held there in 1796.

👑The last king

A last refuge

When war with the British began around 1832, Chikka Vira Rajendra fell back to Nalknad. The British forced his surrender and sent him to Benares.

⛰️The name

The broad hill

In Kodava, thadi means broad, anda is possessive and mol means hill. It is the third-highest peak in Karnataka.

⛺The rule

No more camping

Night camping was stopped in December 2016 because of litter and the harm to wild animals moving at night.

Best time for the Tadiandamol trek

After the rains

Bikat calls September to February the best time.

🌤️Sep–Feb

Cooler and clear. Days stay under about 28 °C.

🌸Mar–early May

Open on the booking portal; in 2026 it took bookings until 11 May.

🌧️Mid-May–Aug

The portal blocked bookings from 12 May to 31 August 2026. The forest path near the top gets slippery in rain.

How to reach the Tadiandamol trek

Start in Bangalore

Operators drive overnight from Bangalore to Kakkabe, about 270 km, and on to the palace.

  1. Bangalore🚌 ~270 km via Mysore, overnight
  2. Kakkabe🚙 To the Nalknad Palace
  3. Nalknad Palace🥾 7 km to the top
  4. Tadiandamol

Madikeri, the nearest town for shopping, is 36 km from Kakkabe. BSNL works in Kakkabe.

🎫Book the permit

Book on Karnataka's Aranya Vihaara portal 1 to 30 days ahead, up to 3 people per booking. Report at the start 10 minutes before your slot.

↩️Refunds

Cancel up to 8 days before for a 50% refund. Within 7 days there is no refund.

💰Cost

Bikat charges ₹3,700 + 5% GST, Bangalore to Bangalore. Treks and Trails charges ₹3,100.

🚫No nights on the hill

Camping has been banned since December 2016. Stay in a homestay below.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🪪ID for the permit

Keep a soft copy of your Aadhaar or Voter ID. It is needed for the forest permit. Plan The Unplanned

💧Carry water

Carry at least 2 litres. You can fill bottles at the homestay. BMC Adventures

🍱Packed lunch

Operators pack a vegetarian lunch to eat on top. Treks and Trails

🛏️Homestay below

Bikat's group sleeps in a homestay at the base, two or three to a room, with an attached bathroom. Bikat Adventures

🚌Pickups in Bangalore

Bikat picks up on Friday evening at Malleshwaram, Rajajinagar, Yeshwanthpur and Jalahalli. Bikat Adventures

🎒No porters

You carry your own bag. Bikat does not include portage of personal rucksacks. Bikat Adventures

🧒Age limits

Bikat takes trekkers from age 9. Under-18s need a parent or guardian. Over-60s need a doctor's certificate. Bikat Adventures

☎️Permit helpline

For booking problems, call the forest department's helpline, 1926, or email book-a-trek@aranya.gov.in. Aranya Vihaara
