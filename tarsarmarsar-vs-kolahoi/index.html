Tarsar Marsar goes 174 m higher (4,024 m against 3,850 m). Tarsar Marsar takes 2 more days on the trail. Kolahoi Glacier is the easier of the two (Easy–moderate against Moderate). Both are at their best in Jul–Aug. The trailheads are about 10 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Tarsar Marsar if you want a lower price (middle ₹16,499) and more height (4,024 m).

Pick Kolahoi Glacier if you want an easier trek (Easy–moderate), fewer days away (5 on the trail) and to go in Sep.

Questions

Which is easier, Tarsar Marsar or Kolahoi Glacier?

Kolahoi Glacier, graded Easy–moderate against Moderate.

Which costs less, Tarsar Marsar or Kolahoi Glacier?

The middle operator price is ₹16,499 for Tarsar Marsar and ₹25,000 for Kolahoi Glacier, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Which goes higher, Tarsar Marsar or Kolahoi Glacier?

Tarsar Marsar reaches 4,024 m and Kolahoi Glacier 3,850 m.

Can I do Tarsar Marsar and Kolahoi Glacier in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Jul–Aug.

How far apart are Tarsar Marsar and Kolahoi Glacier?

About 10 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
