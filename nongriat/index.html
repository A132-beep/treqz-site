Stories and nature

Bridges that grow

🌳The craft

Living roots

Khasi and Jaintia people guide the aerial roots of the rubber fig across streams until they grow into bridges.

🌉The landmark

Jingkieng Nongriat

The Double Decker, two bridges stacked one above the other, is thought to be about 200 years old. It appeared in the BBC's Human Planet.

🌧️The climate

One of the wettest places

Nongriat gets about 9,500 mm of rain a year on average.

Best time for the Nongriat Root Bridges trek

Between the rains

Meghalaya Tourism recommends October to March.

🌤️Oct–Mar

How to reach the Nongriat Root Bridges trek

Getting to Tyrna

Nongriat is about 15 km from Sohra (Cherrapunji); the steps start at Tyrna village.

  2. Tyrna🪜 Down the steps

🧭Guides

A local guide is advisable, and a 2025 district order reportedly makes one mandatory in East Khasi Hills.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

Don't count on an ATM or card payment in the valley. Carry cash for the guide, fees and homestay. Discover with Dheeraj

📶Weak signal

Phone signal is weak in the valley. Tell your hotel where you'll stay and when you'll be back. Discover with Dheeraj

🎫Fees

About ₹20 a person at the Double Decker, plus a small camera fee. Guides charge about ₹700–1,500 per group. Agree the rate at Tyrna. Discover with Dheeraj

🚕Getting to Tyrna

Tyrna is 30–45 minutes by taxi from Sohra. A small cab for the day costs about ₹3,500–4,500. Self-drive rental cars for tourists are banned since June 2025. Discover with Dheeraj

💧Water and snacks

Carry 2–3 litres of water. Villagers sell water, chips and snacks along the trail. Treks and Trails India

🥪Pack lunch for Rainbow Falls

There are no shops on the trail beyond Nongriat. Take lunch and a guide. Muddie Trails

🏠Homestays

Nongriat homestays are basic, with home-cooked meals. Hosts must register you on the state tourism app, so carry ID. Discover with Dheeraj

⏰Start early

Be on the steps by 8 a.m. on a day trip. Never climb out after dark. Discover with Dheeraj

🏊Pools

Swim only where your guide says. Streams rise fast after rain upstream. Discover with Dheeraj
