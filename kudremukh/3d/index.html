Stories and nature

The horse face

🐴The name

Kuduremukha

"Horse-faced" in Kannada, for one side of the mountain. It was once called Samseparvata, after the approach from Samse village.

⛏️The town

Iron ore

Kudremukha grew as a mining town. The government-run KIOCL mined iron ore for almost 30 years and closed in 2006 over environmental concerns.

🐒The forest

Three rivers

The Tunga, Bhadra and Nethravathi are said to rise here. The park shelters tigers, leopards, wild dogs and the lion-tailed macaque.

Best time for the Kudremukh trek

Monsoon green or winter clear

Bikat runs it June–August and November–February. The park is typically closed from about March to May.

🌧️Jun–Aug

The grassland at its greenest, in the rains.

🌤️Nov–Feb

Clearer, drier days.

💰Cost

₹3,950 + 5% GST with Bikat, forest permit included.

How to reach the Kudremukh trek

Book ahead

Permits are booked online on the Karnataka forest department's Aranya Vihaara portal, 1 to 30 days ahead. Bring the original ID you booked with.

  1. Bangalore🚌 Overnight
  2. Kalasa → Mullodi🚙 To the forest office
  3. Forest office
⏰Timings

Enter after 6:30 am, and be back past the checkpost by 5:30 pm. No camping in the park.

🚯Plastic

Single-use plastic is confiscated or tagged against a deposit.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

🚌Bus to Kalasa

Take an overnight bus from Bangalore towards Horanadu and get off at Kalasa. It costs about ₹800. The trek start is about 10 km from the Kalasa bus stand. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

📶Little phone signal

BSNL works in Kudremukh town. There is no signal inside the forest. Bikat Adventures

🍱No food on the trail

There are no food stalls on the trail. Mullodi homestays give breakfast and a packed lunch. Packets of chips, biscuits and chocolates are not allowed in the park. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

💧Carry your water

Carry 2.5–3 litres of water and some electrolytes, in a reusable bottle. TrekGo

🛏️Basic homestays

About three homestays in Mullodi take trekkers. They have separate dorms for men and women and shared bathrooms. You sleep on thin foam sheets, so bring a blanket or sleeping bag. Muddie Trails

🧭Guide on arrival

A local guide is assigned when you arrive. You don't need to book one ahead. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

🩸Leeches

The forest is full of leeches, most of all in the monsoon. Put Dettol or Savlon on your legs and shoes before you start. Trekup India

🌡️Weather

Winter days are about 14–33 °C. The coldest nights drop to about 10 °C. The monsoon brings heavy rain. Bikat Adventures

⏰Turn back by 1 pm

Start down from the top by 1 pm at the latest. The descent is tricky in the dark. Bikat Adventures DIY guide

🎒No offloading

Bikat has no option to offload your bag. Carry only a light daypack. Bikat Adventures
