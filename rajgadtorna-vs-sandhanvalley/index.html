Sandhan Valley takes 1 more day on the trail. Sandhan Valley is the easier of the two (Easy against Easy–moderate). Both are at their best in Oct–Feb. The trailheads are about 137 km apart in a straight line.

Which should you pick?

Pick Rajgad to Torna if you want fewer days away (2 on the trail) and to go in Sep.

Pick Sandhan Valley if you want an easier trek (Easy), a lower price (middle ₹2,750) and to go in Mar.

Questions

Which is easier, Rajgad to Torna or Sandhan Valley?

Sandhan Valley, graded Easy against Easy–moderate.

Which costs less, Rajgad to Torna or Sandhan Valley?

The middle operator price is ₹3,500 for Rajgad to Torna and ₹2,750 for Sandhan Valley, per person. GST and travel are often extra.

Can I do Rajgad to Torna and Sandhan Valley in the same season?

Yes: both are at their best in Oct–Feb.

How far apart are Rajgad to Torna and Sandhan Valley?

About 137 km in a straight line between the trailheads. By road it is further.
