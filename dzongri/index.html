02Day 2 · Yuksom · 1,780 mkm 0.1

Where Sikkim crowned its king

~150kmFrom NJP/Bagdogra · 6–8 h by road

In 1642 three lamas met at Yuksom and crowned Phuntsog Namgyal the first Chogyal of Sikkim, on the stone throne of Norbugang.

Into the park

6kmToday · From Bakhim

Past Sachen and Bakhim to Tshoka, inside Khangchendzonga National Park.

Through the rhododendrons

The climb from Tshoka to Dzongri gains over 1,000 m. Explurger breaks it with a night at Phedang.

The high meadow

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

💵Bring cash

The nearest ATMs are in Yuksom, Pelling and Gangtok. There are none on the trail.

Bikat Adventures (Goechala trek)
📶Little phone signal

Yuksom is the last place with reliable signal. Jio, Airtel and Vodafone work in spots up to Tshoka, and at Tshoka and Phedang in good weather.

Mountain Tours & Treks
🔦No electricity

There is no electricity on the trek. Carry a head torch with spare batteries.

Mountain Tours & Treks
💧Water

Carry about 2 litres from Yuksom and refill at streams. There is no water between Phedang and Deorali. Purify with iodine tablets.

Ashmita Trek
🏠Huts and toilets

Trekkers' huts at Tshoka and Dzongri, or twin tents. Toilets are a tent over a dry pit with a portable seat.

Himalaya Trekker
🌡️Weather up high

In autumn, from Phedang up: days 2 to 14 °C, nights −15 to 2 °C.

Questions

How long is the Dzongri trek?

About 57.7 km of walking over 7 days on the trail.

How high does the Dzongri trek go?

The highest point is 4,170 m, about 2,390 m above the start.

How difficult is the Dzongri trek?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard.

When is the best time for the Dzongri trek?

Mar–May, Oct–Nov. It is also possible in Dec–Feb.

How much does the Dzongri trek cost?

₹13,500 to ₹54,150 per person: base prices from 6 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Dzongri trek?

Yuksom is the last place with reliable signal. Jio, Airtel and Vodafone work in spots up to Tshoka, and at Tshoka and Phedang in good weather.

Where do I get cash before the Dzongri trek?

The nearest ATMs are in Yuksom, Pelling and Gangtok. There are none on the trail.

What should I pack for the Dzongri trek?

Thermal innerwear, Fleece or insulated jacket, Down jacket, Waterproof, windproof shell, Rain poncho, Quick-dry trekking pants, Woollen cap and neck gaiter, Gloves. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Dzongri trek in winter?

It is possible: its sources list Dec–Feb as workable, with snow and cold to plan for.

Is the Dzongri trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Moderate–hard and it takes 7 days on the trail, with a highest point of 4,170 m.

Which treks are near the Dzongri trek?

Goechala, Sandakphu, Green Lake, Bailey Trail (Tawang), David Scott Trail.
