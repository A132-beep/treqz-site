Stories and nature

Under the five treasuries

🦊The park

Red pandas and tragopans

Khangchendzonga National Park holds red panda, snow leopard, clouded leopard and about 550 bird species, including the satyr tragopan.

🏆The award

Best trekking circuit

Sikkim's forest department announced that the Goecha La–Dzongri circuit won the Outlook Traveller Awards 2025 Gold for Best Trekking Circuit in India.

🧗The school

A mountaineers' base

Yuksom is a base of the Himalayan Mountaineering Institute; from Gingay Dara, near Dzongri, you can see its training base camp.

How to reach the Dzongri trek

Getting to Yuksom

Yuksom is the last road village, a long drive from the plains.

  1. NJP / Bagdogra🚙 ~150 km via Jorethang and Legship, 6–8 h
  2. Yuksom

Gangtok is 4–5 hours from Yuksom.

💰Cost

Himalaya Shelter charges ₹28,000 + 5% GST for 8 days. Explurger estimates ₹12,000–18,000 for standard packages.

🎫Permits

A park permit (₹300 for Indians per Holidify), a route permit and police registration at Yuksom. Foreigners need a Restricted Area Permit.

🧭Guide

A registered local guide is mandatory; solo trekking is not allowed.

🤕Altitude sickness

The Tshoka–Dzongri climb gains over 1,000 m. Sleep at Phedang and go slowly.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

💵Bring cash

The nearest ATMs are in Yuksom, Pelling and Gangtok. There are none on the trail. Bikat Adventures (Goechala trek)

📶Little phone signal

Yuksom is the last place with reliable signal. Jio, Airtel and Vodafone work in spots up to Tshoka, and at Tshoka and Phedang in good weather. Mountain Tours & Treks

🔦No electricity

There is no electricity on the trek. Carry a head torch with spare batteries. Mountain Tours & Treks

💧Water

Carry about 2 litres from Yuksom and refill at streams. There is no water between Phedang and Deorali. Purify with iodine tablets. Ashmita Trek

🏠Huts and toilets

Trekkers' huts at Tshoka and Dzongri, or twin tents. Toilets are a tent over a dry pit with a portable seat. Himalaya Trekker

🌡️Weather up high

In autumn, from Phedang up: days 2 to 14 °C, nights −15 to 2 °C. Mountain Tours & Treks

🩺Fitness certificate

A medical fitness certificate is mandatory. It can be arranged in Yuksom. Indians also need original ID and 4 passport photos. Mountain Tours & Treks

🏥Nearest hospital

Primary Health Centre in Yuksom. District Hospital, Gyalshing, is about 5 hours by road: +91 3595 250648. Trekup India (Goechala trek)

🐴Offloading

A horse carries your rucksack for ₹3,500 + 5% GST for the whole trek. Keep it under 10 kg. Himalaya Trekker

🚙Jeep to Yuksom

Shared jeeps run Siliguri to Jorethang (about 4 h), then on to Yuksom (about 3 h). Seats aren't guaranteed. A private Sumo costs ₹5,500–7,000 one way. Himalaya Trekker
