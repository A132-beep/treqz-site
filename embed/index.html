A free 3D map for your trek page

Show your trekkers the whole route in 3D, camp by camp, from real elevation and satellite data. Copy one line of code. No sign-up, no cost, no ads.

Get the code

  1. Paste the code into your trek page where you want the map, in HTML or "Custom HTML" mode (WordPress, Wix and Squarespace all have one).
  2. Keep the credit line under the map. It's what keeps the map free.
  3. The map shows the route, camps, weather and altitude. It never lists other operators.
