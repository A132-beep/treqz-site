Stories and nature

The shepherds' lake

🐑The people

Gaddi kothis

Gaddi herders graze their flocks round the lake in summer, keeping a few huts on the far shore, then move down for winter.

🐦The birds

Magpies and griffons

Blue magpies in the pines; brown dippers, warblers and Himalayan griffon vultures in the grassland higher up.

🚁The rescues

Lost in the snow

In December 2020, 73 people lost in snowfall on the way to the lake were brought down by police, home guards and villagers. In June 2023, 26 tourists were rescued after floods cut the route.

Best time for the Kareri Lake trek

Spring and autumn

The lake freezes from early December to March or April.

🌸May–June

Good weather before the monsoon.

🌧️July–August

Leeches, slippery trail, risky stream crossings.

🍂Sep–Nov

Clear and cool.

❄️Dec–March

Snow on the trail. Crampons and snow experience needed.

How to reach the Kareri Lake trek

Getting to Kareri

Dharamshala is the base. Buses run to Ghera; taxis go on to Kareri village.

  1. Dharamshala🚌 20 km to Ghera, about 1.5 h
  2. Ghera🚶 About 2 h
  3. Kareri village

A taxi from Dharamshala to Kareri village costs ₹800–1,000. Nearest airport: Gaggal. Nearest major railhead: Pathankot.

💰Cost

Treks and Trails charges ₹4,000 + 5% GST for 3 days from Kareri village. Tents at Reoti rent for ₹200–300 a night.

🎫Permits

A forest entry permit for the Dhauladhar Wildlife Sanctuary has generally been required, from the forest office in Dharamshala or McLeod Ganj.

💵Cash

No ATMs in Kareri or Ghera.

🤕Weather

Snow and monsoon floods have both trapped trekkers here. Check the forecast.

Good to know

The small things

Cash, signal, water, permits and help on the way. Checked October 2026; confirm with your operator.

📶Little phone signal

Signal turns weak on the drive to Kareri village. Only sparse BSNL on the trail. Little or none at Reoti and the lake. Trekkaro

🔋No charging

There is no charging point on the trail. Bring a power bank. Trekkaro

🍲Food on the trail

Seasonal shacks at Nolli Bridge, about 3 km up, and at Reoti serve dal-rice, Maggi, chai and omelettes, May to October. Nothing beyond Reoti. Discover with Dheeraj

🛏️Rent at Reoti

Shacks at Reoti rent sleeping bags for about ₹100 a night, as well as tents. They shut in winter, so carry everything then. Discover with Dheeraj

🚻Toilets

Operators pitch toilet tents over dug pits. There is no bathing on the trek. Bikat Adventures

🌡️Nights at camp

About 5–10 °C in May, June, September and October. −2 to 3 °C in April and November. Trekkaro

🥶Winter nights

December to February: days 10–15 °C, nights 0 to −15 °C. Bikat Adventures

🏥Nearest hospital

Zonal Hospital, Dharamshala, about 27 km from Kareri village. There are no medical facilities on the trail. Discover with Dheeraj

🎒Offloading

Trekkaro carries your bag for ₹1,000 + 5% GST for the whole trek, up to 9 kg. Trekkaro

🚕Group pickup

Trekkaro meets at Dharamshala ISBT at 10 a.m. The shared cab to Kareri village is extra and split between trekkers. Trekkaro
