Overview

The trek in one line, and what you can do with it.

Kareri is an easy trek close to Dharamshala, and the first of the Dhauladhar's sacred lakes.

Before you go

The practical things, then the questions people ask.

📶Little phone signal

Signal turns weak on the drive to Kareri village. Only sparse BSNL on the trail. Little or none at Reoti and the lake.

Trekkaro
🔋No charging

There is no charging point on the trail. Bring a power bank.

Trekkaro
🍲Food on the trail

Seasonal shacks at Nolli Bridge, about 3 km up, and at Reoti serve dal-rice, Maggi, chai and omelettes, May to October. Nothing beyond Reoti.

Discover with Dheeraj
🛏️Rent at Reoti

Shacks at Reoti rent sleeping bags for about ₹100 a night, as well as tents. They shut in winter, so carry everything then.

Discover with Dheeraj
🚻Toilets

Operators pitch toilet tents over dug pits. There is no bathing on the trek.

Bikat Adventures
🌡️Nights at camp

About 5–10 °C in May, June, September and October. −2 to 3 °C in April and November.

Questions

How long is the Kareri Lake trek?

About 26.0 km of walking over 3 days on the trail.

How high does the Kareri Lake trek go?

The highest point is 2,934 m, about 1,160 m above the start.

How difficult is the Kareri Lake trek?

Its sources grade it Easy.

When is the best time for the Kareri Lake trek?

May–Jun, Sep–Nov. It is also possible in Dec–Mar.

How much does the Kareri Lake trek cost?

₹4,000 to ₹7,600 per person: base prices from 5 operators' own pages. GST and travel are often extra.

Is there phone signal on the Kareri Lake trek?

Signal turns weak on the drive to Kareri village. Only sparse BSNL on the trail. Little or none at Reoti and the lake.

What should I pack for the Kareri Lake trek?

Padded jacket, Thermal inners, top and bottom, Fleece or woollen sweater, Rain jacket or poncho, Trek pants ×2, Full-sleeve quick-dry T-shirts ×2–3, Sun cap, Woollen cap and buff. The full list is on the 3D page.

Can I do the Kareri Lake trek in winter?

It is possible: its sources list Dec–Feb as workable, with snow and cold to plan for.

Is the Kareri Lake trek good for beginners?

Its sources grade it Easy and it takes 3 days on the trail, with a highest point of 2,934 m.

Which treks are near the Kareri Lake trek?

Triund, Indrahar Pass, Beas Kund, Bhrigu Lake, Prashar Lake.
